## macOS: the game freezes the Mac on entering the world

- **Who:** Apple silicon Macs (M1 to M5) on macOS 26 and 27.
- **What:** the screen hangs when a character enters the world; WindowServer crashes, and sometimes the Mac restarts after a kernel panic.
- **Status:** reported by players, with no staff reply in the thread yet. Blizzard noted "stability issues on Macs" fixed in a September Beta build, but testers report mixed results. [Players' thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/macos-27-and-forever-windowserver-crash/2352734), [Beta patch notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/beta-client-update-september-22/2358655)
- **Workaround players report:** set Secondary Lighting to Fair in the graphics settings. Not confirmed by Blizzard.

## Addon settings reset after logging out

- **What:** some players report that addon settings aren't read back, so they reset each session.
- **Status:** reported by players, not acknowledged by Blizzard. [metammo.com](https://metammo.com/guides/wow-forever-addons-restedxp-questie-setup)
