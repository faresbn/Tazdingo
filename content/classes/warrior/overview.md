## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Arms for a two-hander and deliberate pulls, Fury when you have two strong weapons, Protection when you regularly tank dungeons.
- **Levels 40–59:** A level-appropriate weapon and a shield in your bags give you more options than copying an endgame split at 40. The 31-point choices are Mortal Strike, Bloodthirst and Shield Slam. Compare them by what you spend the next ten levels doing. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Hamstring, a stance change and your shield accessible. Fight near Horde support when possible and leave enough Rage for an exit if an opponent joins a pull. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

Blizzard fixed several Fury interactions with Heroic Strike and Cleave in November and made Mortal Strike require the actual talent on 23 November. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Arms](ledger:classes/warrior/arms/) | 32/17/2 | Two-handed questing, two-target cleave and pressure against enemy healers. |
| [Fury](ledger:classes/warrior/fury/) | 17/34/0 | Two-weapon damage and sustained kills when both weapons are competitive. |
| [Protection](ledger:classes/warrior/protection/) | 4/15/32 | Five-player tanking with a shield, interrupts and emergency cooldowns. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

Keep Hamstring, a stance change and your shield accessible. Fight near Horde support when possible and leave enough Rage for an exit if an opponent joins a pull. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
