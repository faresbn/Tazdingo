## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Hamstring, a stance change and your shield accessible. Fight near Horde support when possible and leave enough Rage for an exit if an opponent joins a pull.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Arms](ledger:classes/warrior/arms/) | 32/17/2 | Pick the 32/17/2 dungeon starting point if you want the same weapon for questing and group DPS. Published versions agree on the Arms core but differ on Rage and Enrage talents in Fury. |
| [Fury](ledger:classes/warrior/fury/) | 17/34/0 | The 17/34/0 build is a dual-wield PvE starting point. Compare the 18/33/0 alternative if Spearing Strike matters to the targets you intend to fight. |
| [Protection](ledger:classes/warrior/protection/) | 4/15/32 | Start at 4/15/32 for a dedicated tank. Compare 5/15/31 with more emphasis on simpler defensive and dungeon picks, then judge by the pulls you actually tank. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
