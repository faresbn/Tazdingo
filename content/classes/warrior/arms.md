## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Two-handed questing, two-target cleave and pressure against enemy healers.
- **Levels 40–59:** Take Mortal Strike as soon as your build reaches 31 Arms. Use Sweeping Strikes when two nearby enemies can be hit safely. Replace a lagging two-hander before spending gold on a respec. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 32/17/2 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Hamstring for an opponent’s escape and use Mortal Strike when healing is the reason they survive. Save a way to change stance and close space. Omnivore’s PvP build is a separate option for organised play. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Pick the 32/17/2 dungeon starting point if you want the same weapon for questing and group DPS. Published versions agree on the Arms core but differ on Rage and Enrage talents in Fury. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-pvp-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Mortal Strike gives sustained pressure. Build around Overpower and Slam, with Rend where you can use its ticks and Execute when the target is in range. If Sweeping Strikes would break control or hit an unwanted target, keep it for the next pull. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-pvp-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Use a current two-hander and carry a shield. Check actual weapon speed and damage before judging Rend and Slam with a new talent allocation. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-pvp-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
