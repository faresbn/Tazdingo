## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Five-player tanking with a shield, interrupts and emergency cooldowns.
- **Levels 40–59:** Have Shield Slam at 31 Protection points if you are tanking frequently. Keep a current shield and practise an opening on two or three targets before growing the pull. Carry a damage set for the time between groups. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warrior-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 4/15/32 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** A shield and a defensive stance give you time to leave a bad fight. Use Hamstring to make space and keep your shield set available even when questing in damage gear. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Start at 4/15/32 for a dedicated tank. Compare 5/15/31 with more emphasis on simpler defensive and dungeon picks, then judge by the pulls you actually tank. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/ludideo-protection-warrior-guide-1-60-talents-rotations-and-more) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Shield Slam and Revenge to stabilise threat, then spend on multi-target tools according to the pull. Keep a taunt for a loose enemy and reserve an interrupt for the dangerous cast. Dungeon versions invest more in Thunder Clap than the raid allocation. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/ludideo-protection-warrior-guide-1-60-talents-rotations-and-more) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warrior-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Bring a current shield and enough armour and health for the pack you intend to tank. Check whether incoming damage or threat is the bigger problem before leaning hard into one set. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/ludideo-protection-warrior-guide-1-60-talents-rotations-and-more)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
