## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Fire burst and critical-strike payoffs on targets you can safely cast at.
- **Levels 40–59:** Take Combustion when you can reach deep Fire and learn Frostfire Bolt if available from the trainer. Use the extra range and fast finishers to limit downtime. Keep Frost as your comparison when control is the thing you lack. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 16/32/3 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Fire Blast and quick casts let you keep moving, but save Blink and Counterspell for a chase or an enemy cast you need to stop. Carry a control-heavy setup for runs with heavy interference. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

16/32/3 is a Fire build with a small Arcane Blast component. Compare the deeper Fire version if you prefer less upkeep or want Burning Soul. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/mage/fire) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Maintain Scorch’s debuff when the group needs you to and the target will live long enough. Use Fireball and Heating Up’s faster Pyroblasts, then fit cooldowns to a robust pack or boss. Check resistance before committing. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/mage/fire) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Check that targets can take Fire damage before spending gold on a Fire-specific upgrade. Use a comparable set of debuffs when testing the Fire and Arcane-heavy variants. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/mage/fire)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
