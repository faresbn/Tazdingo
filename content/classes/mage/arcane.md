## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Mana-managed caster damage, control and short burst windows.
- **Levels 40–59:** Arcane Power becomes reachable at 31 Arcane points. Build enough mana to finish the pull and let Arcane Blast stacks fall when repeated casts are costing more than the kill is worth. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 32/16/3 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use Polymorph and Counterspell to buy the distance needed for a cast. Keep Blink for a real escape or a clean finishing window. Be sparing with Arcane Blast when a second fight can start immediately. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

32/16/3 is Method’s Arcane/Fire starting point. Its 38/10/3 alternative goes deeper into Arcane rather than relying on as much Fire synergy. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/arcane-mage-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Arcane Blast stacks for planned burst, consume useful Missile Barrage procs and take recovery time between burn windows. The Fire dip supports a hybrid version; the deeper Arcane build prioritises the Arcane toolkit. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/arcane-mage-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Compare spell damage and Intellect with the same number of Arcane Blast stacks and the same encounter duration. Keep mana gems ready for your next burn. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/arcane-mage-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
