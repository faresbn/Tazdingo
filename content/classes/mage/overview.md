## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Frost for the easiest resets, Fire for critical-hit burst, Arcane for deliberate burst and mana management.
- **Levels 40–59:** From 40, compare Frost’s control to how quickly Fire or Arcane can actually clear the same route. Compare complete pulls with travel and drinking time, then build a group setup at 60 if your usual pulls change. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** For Horde on a PvP ruleset, leave yourself a line to terrain before committing to a cast. Keep Blink, Polymorph and Counterspell free when you see a player approaching. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

November fixed Frost Nova staying up through certain damage, and corrected Arcane Concentration proc accounting. Old dungeon or AoE routines can behave differently. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Arcane](ledger:classes/mage/arcane/) | 32/16/3 | Mana-managed caster damage, control and short burst windows. |
| [Fire](ledger:classes/mage/fire/) | 16/32/3 | Fire burst and critical-strike payoffs on targets you can safely cast at. |
| [Frost](ledger:classes/mage/frost/) | 18/0/33 | Safe solo levelling, pack control and ranged damage with Shatter windows. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

For Horde on a PvP ruleset, leave yourself a line to terrain before committing to a cast. Keep Blink, Polymorph and Counterspell free when you see a player approaching. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
