## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** Cloth. Staves, daggers, one-handed swords and wands, according to the Forever client ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-mage)).
- **Role:** Ranged damage only. Blizzard counts the Mage as a pure damage class, with the Hunter, Rogue and Warlock.
- **Resource:** Mana. You conjure water from level 4 and food from 6, learn [Evocation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12051) at 20 and conjure a mana gem from 28.
- **Races:** Human, Gnome and High Order Skyborne for the Alliance; Orc, Undead and Troll for the Horde. Orc and Skyborne Mages are new.
- **New in Forever:** [Ice Lance](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1312002) returns as a Frost talent, [Arcane Blast](https://foreverdb.net/spell/400574) is a new Arcane talent and [Frostfire Bolt](https://foreverdb.net/spell/401502) is a trainer spell at 40. Missile Barrage, Heating Up and Fingers of Frost join the talents.
- **Talents:** One point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all, in one tree with Arcane, Fire and Frost sections. Respec at a trainer in a capital city; dual specialisation opens at 40.
- **Levelling pick:** Every guide read calls Frost the safe choice. Fire and Arcane level well too, but forgive fewer mistakes.
- **Utility:** Arcane Intellect, Polymorph, Remove Lesser Curse (which now also clears Banes), conjured food and water, city teleports from 20 and portals from 40.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

**Frost is the default for a first solo Mage** because control makes mistakes easier to recover from. Fire suits players who accept less reliable early crits for its burst; Arcane suits managed mana spending and group area damage. Neither damage preference proves a faster levelling route once drinking and deaths are included ([Frost](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/level-30-dps-overview), [Fire](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/fire/level-30-dps-overview)). Use a current wand early, then reassess its damage against learned spell ranks. Do not buy gear or choose a race using superseded Beta mechanics.

### How Forever changed the Mage

Blizzard's [Mage and Shaman deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/24302097) says the aim is to make each school matter in the moment and to open more builds that cross schools. The main changes, from that post and the beta client:

- **Arcane becomes a damage spec.** Arcane Blast is the 11-point Arcane talent, and [Missile Barrage](https://foreverdb.net/spell/400588) turns Arcane Missiles into a free, fast channel. Wowhead's Arcane guide says the tree is no longer there only to buff the other two.
- **Frost gets a proc rotation.** Ice Lance hits hard against Frozen targets, and [Fingers of Frost](https://foreverdb.net/spell/400647) lets your Chill effects treat a target as Frozen.
- **Fire gets Heating Up.** Fire crits shorten your next Pyroblast, so it becomes a regular cast rather than only an opener.
- **Frostfire Bolt at 40** counts as both Fire and Frost. Blizzard says it uses the higher of your Fire or Frost spell damage and the lower of the target's two resistances, and was designed for builds that mix Fire and Frost talents.
- **Anti-farming changes.** Cone of Cold lasts less time, Improved Blizzard's slow tops out at 40% (65% in Classic), and talents that give free effects, such as Arcane Concentration, trigger less often from low-rank spells. Blizzard links all three to gold farmers and boosters.
- **Comprehension.** A Mage-only minor tradeskill lets you decipher scrolls that drop in the world for buffs and effects.
- **Smaller changes.** Arcane Intellect lasts an hour, Remove Lesser Curse also removes Banes, and Arcane Missiles checks line of sight only when the channel starts ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-mage), citing Blizzard's notes). Early spell ranks take a much bigger share of spell power, and caster gear carries spell power from low levels.

The trees kept moving late in the beta. Build 1.60.1.70425 (16 October) reworked Arcane Blast, moved Missile Barrage to the first row, turned Wake of Fire into Improved Fire Blast, made Winter's Chill feed Cone of Cold and cut Ice Lance's Frozen bonus from 300% to 200% in the client files ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70425-game-files)). The first live client (24 October) made Arcane Wrath a row 4 Arcane talent instead of a trainer spell ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/live-client-1-60-1-70532-what-changed)). Several guides predate these builds, so check a guide's date before trusting its talent names.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | What the guides stress | Levelling verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Frost | Slows, freezes and shields; Ice Lance and Fingers of Frost procs; steady rather than top damage | ForeverDB's first pick; Icy Veins calls it safe and consistent; Wowhead found questing easy but not fast |
| Fire | Crits feed Ignite and fast Pyroblasts; burst AoE; needs gear to shine | Icy Veins says it is now fun to level; Wowhead found it weak at 30 for lack of crit chance |
| Arcane | Mana management, Arcane Blast into Missile Barrage, strong dungeon AoE | Icy Veins calls it viable and promising; Wowhead liked its AoE but not its single-target feel at 30 |

[ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-mage) recommends Frost for a first Mage. Frostbolt's slow and Frost Nova buy time on every pull, Ice Lance turns each freeze into damage from 20, and Shatter and Ice Block add safety by 30. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/frost-mage-leveling) agrees Frost is the safe option but lists it as mana-hungry, gear-dependent and reliant on procs. Its [Fire levelling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fire-mage-leveling) says Frost is no longer the only viable levelling spec. Wowhead's writers rated all three specs roughly level with each other up to 20 ([Frost overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/overview-pve-dps)).

In short, the guides point PvP players and anyone who wants control towards Frost, crit and burst fans towards Fire, and players who enjoy mana management and dungeon AoE towards Arcane.

### Levelling advice shared across specs

- **Wands.** Every guide calls wands strong early. Wowhead and Icy Veins suggest two points in Wand Specialization (Arcane) at first, then a respec once your spec's key talents arrive.
- **Professions.** Tailoring and Enchanting are the usual pair (ForeverDB, Icy Veins). Icy Veins' Fire and Arcane pages and Wowhead also rate Engineering highly for its gadgets.
- **Class wand quest at 30.** Wowhead describes a chain from any Mage trainer that runs through Scarlet Monastery's Library and ends with a choice of Icefury, Ragefire or Nether Force Wand, one per school.
- **Travel.** Teleports from 20 save time. Icy Veins advises against binding your hearthstone to a capital you can already teleport to.
- **Dungeons.** Icy Veins says dungeon quests give a large share of experience. ForeverDB notes that Blizzard halved the extra experience from dungeon quests on 1 October.

### What the simulators assume at 60

WoWSims Forever's Mage simulator defaults to an Arcane build with Frost points, and all five of its preset builds spend points in two or three sections (see the hybrid builds section). Its level 60 gear set is marked as a developer preset. Read its numbers as projections: the beta stopped at level 30.

### Where the guides disagree

- **Racials — resolved:** [26 October official notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7) set Skyborne Wind-Blessed to 2% haste and Human Ruthless to 1% crit. Old 1%/3% haste and weapon-restricted Human comparisons are obsolete. Orc Savage was removed; its restored pet racial does not improve Mage damage.
- **Teleport: Dalaran:** ForeverDB lists it at level 50 and Alliance-only; Icy Veins' Frost levelling table puts it at 40.
- **Wands and spell damage:** ForeverDB cites Blizzard's 24 September notes saying wands no longer gain from spell damage. Icy Veins' Frost guide says spell damage still raises wand damage.
