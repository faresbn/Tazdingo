## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** the safest levelling and kiting spec: slows, freezes and recovery tools for solo pulls and PvP, with steady rather than top damage.
- **Levelling build:** at 30, Icy Veins' calculator takes Improved Frostbolt 5, Frostbite 3, Ice Shards 5, Ice Lance, Missile Barrage 2, Frost Channeling 1, Shatter 3 and Ice Block ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/frost-mage-leveling)). Wowhead trades Improved Frostbolt ranks for Elemental Precision in dungeons; ForeverDB's build predates the 16 October rework.
- **Build at 60:** projection only: WoWSims' "Frost" is 32 Frost (Ice Shards 5, Shatter 3, Fingers of Frost 2, Winter's Chill 5, Cold Snap, Ice Barrier) plus 19 Arcane ([WoWSims](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/mage/dps/presets.ts)).
- **Priority:** levelling ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/frost-mage-leveling)): Frostbolt from maximum range, Frost Nova on arrival, Ice Lance while Frozen or on [Fingers of Frost](https://foreverdb.net/spell/400647) procs, Cone of Cold at five Winter's Chill stacks, finish with Fire Blast or wand. At 60 WoWSims also casts Cone of Cold on cooldown.
- **Stats:** Icy Veins: hit, then spell power and Frost damage, crit, Spirit and Intellect. ForeverDB adds that Elemental Precision covers part of the dungeon hit gap ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/pve)).
- **Key levels:** 20 [Ice Lance](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1312002) · 24 Frost Blast (trainer) · 25 Shatter · 30 Ice Block and the Mage wand quest · 30–35 Fingers of Frost · 35 Winter's Chill boosts Cone of Cold.
- **Gear and consumables:** Wowhead's level 30 list includes Sorcerer Collar, Belt of Arugal, Glinteye Slippers and the quest's Icefury Wand; Tailoring's Silky pieces only fit their crafter ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/level-30-dps-overview)). Icy Veins calls Wizard Oil the biggest boost; Lesser Arcane Elixir tops ForeverDB's simulator at 30 (+4.4%).
- **Watch out:** Ice Lance now gets a 200% increase against Frozen targets (not 200% damage); guides quoting 300% are out of date ([Blizzard](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6)). Any damage, your Ice Lance included, can break a freeze. Rank 1 Frostbolt no longer fishes Frostbite procs. Don't spam Ice Lance as a filler.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Use **Frost for controlled solo pulls and recovery tools**, then choose talents for the content: a safe questing or PvP build need not match a dungeon damage build. Ice Lance gains value against a genuinely frozen target or through Fingers of Frost; do not spam it as an unconditional filler ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/level-30-dps-overview)). The [later official notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6) settle its bonus as a **200% increase**, superseding 300% descriptions. Do not confuse "200% increase" with "200% of normal damage". Begin with manageable pulls before trying area farming; a damage model does not include the cost of a failed pull.

### Ice Lance and freezes

Blizzard's [deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/24302097) describes Ice Lance as instant with low base damage, raised by 300% against Frozen targets or targets treated as Frozen by Fingers of Frost. The 16 October client lowered that to 200% ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70425-game-files)), though the guides still quote 300%. Fingers of Frost gives Chill effects a 15% chance to treat your next spells as if the target were Frozen, two charges at 2/2 in the current client.

[Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/frost-mage-leveling) warns that any damage can break a freeze, including your own Ice Lance, though Ice Lance snapshots the Frozen state if cast first. Wowhead's [level 30 guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/level-30-dps-overview) notes that rank 1 Frostbolt no longer works for fishing Frostbite procs. ForeverDB ties this to Blizzard's 1 October change, which makes low ranks less likely to trigger talents.

### Rotation

**Single target, levelling:**
1. Pull at maximum range with Frostbolt.
2. When the mob arrives, Frost Nova it, then Ice Lance while it's Frozen or walk out of range ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-mage)).
3. If Fingers of Frost procs as a Frostbolt finishes, cancel your next cast and Ice Lance (Icy Veins).
4. If Frostbite freezes the target on hit, queue Ice Lance after the Frostbolt you are casting; don't cancel it (Icy Veins).
5. With one Fingers of Frost charge left, Icy Veins batches a Frostbolt with an Ice Lance so both count as Frozen.
6. At five Winter's Chill stacks, spend them with Cone of Cold (Icy Veins).
7. Finish with Fire Blast or your wand.

**AoE** (Icy Veins): on three or more targets, Cone of Cold to fish for procs, Ice Lance un-frozen mobs with Fingers of Frost and Frozen mobs until the freeze breaks, and Frost Nova to make more Frozen targets. On five or more, Cone of Cold on cooldown with Blizzard in between, and Arcane Explosion if you can't channel safely. Cone of Cold keeps up to four targets chilled about 90% of the time, or 100% with Permafrost.

**Simulators:** ForeverDB's own simulator at 30 runs Frostbolt with Ice Lance on Fingers of Frost, and found 8% more damage without Fire Blast, against ForeverDB's guide card ([ForeverDB PvE](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/pve)). The WoWSims [frost list](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/tree/master/ui/specs/mage/dps/apls) at 60:
1. Pre-cast Ice Barrier, then Frostbolt.
2. Read Leyline and Evocation below 5% mana.
3. Cooldowns.
4. On three or more targets (two with Improved Blizzard), Cone of Cold, Cold Snap, Ice Barrier and Blizzard.
5. Ice Lance at two Fingers of Frost charges; at one, Frostbolt then Ice Lance.
6. Cone of Cold on cooldown, even on one target, and Cold Snap to reset it.
7. Arcane Missiles on Missile Barrage; keep three Arcane Blast stacks.
8. Ice Barrier when it drops; Frostbolt as filler.

Both agree on Frostbolt into Fingers of Frost Ice Lances; the simulator leans harder on Cone of Cold, which fits the Winter's Chill change.

### Gear and consumables

- **Stats:** Icy Veins puts hit first, then spell power and Frost damage, crit, Spirit and Intellect. ForeverDB adds that Elemental Precision covers part of the hit gap in dungeons.
- **Level 30 gear** (Wowhead): Sorcerer Collar, [Belt of Arugal](https://foreverdb.net/item/6392), [Gaze Dreamer Pants](https://foreverdb.net/item/6903), Glinteye Slippers, [Acidic Walkers](https://foreverdb.net/item/9454), Scorn's Icy Choker and Bloodmage Mantle. Tailoring's Silky pieces boost Frost damage only, and only their crafter can wear them. The Mage wand quest at 30 offers [Icefury Wand](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/mage/frost/level-30-dps-overview).
- **Consumables:** Icy Veins calls Wizard Oil your biggest damage boost, then Lesser Mageblood Elixir, Lesser Spellblasting Potion and food such as Briny Seafood Stew. Lesser Arcane Elixir tops ForeverDB's simulator at 30 (+4.4%).
### Where the guides disagree or aren't sure

- **Ice Lance's bonus — resolved:** the [16 October official notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6) changed the increase against Frozen targets from 300% to 200%. Earlier guide values are obsolete.
- **Elemental Precision and Piercing Ice:** the client still shows five ranks of Elemental Precision and Piercing Ice. One player reported a hotfix cutting Elemental Precision to three ranks and removing Piercing Ice ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/mage-talent-hotfix-elemental-precision-piercing-ice-player-report)); WoWSims' tree and Icy Veins' calculator both match that report.
- **Fingers of Frost timing:** Icy Veins' text says you get it at 30, but its own order delays it to 31–35 because of the two Missile Barrage points.
- **Wands:** Icy Veins says spell damage boosts wands; ForeverDB cites Blizzard's notes saying it no longer does.
