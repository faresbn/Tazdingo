## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Safe solo levelling, pack control and ranged damage with Shatter windows.
- **Levels 40–59:** Ice Barrier is a deep Frost target at 40. Use Frostbolt to control approach and practise consuming Fingers of Frost with something that benefits. Pull smaller groups if other players can force you to move across your own pack. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 18/0/33 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Blink and Frost Nova ready when crossing exposed ground. The November fix restored roots breaking from Blizzard and other damage, so use the intended freeze before your next hit breaks it. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

18/0/33 is Method’s single-target build. For regular five-player pack control, compare its 9/5/37 Improved Blizzard option. [Current spec guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/frost-mage/talents) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Frostbolt as the baseline, Ice Lance during appropriate freeze or Fingers of Frost windows, and Blizzard when you can safely channel across a group. Method’s dungeon allocation gives up some single-target choices for pack control. [Spec guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/frost-mage/talents) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Choose gear for the fights you actually do. Compare Frostbolt and Ice Lance damage on one target and track survival and channel time separately on packs. [Spec and gearing guide](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/frost-mage/talents)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
