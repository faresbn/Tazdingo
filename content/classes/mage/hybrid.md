## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/mage-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** For Horde on a PvP ruleset, leave yourself a line to terrain before committing to a cast. Keep Blink, Polymorph and Counterspell free when you see a player approaching.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Arcane](ledger:classes/mage/arcane/) | 32/16/3 | 32/16/3 is Method’s Arcane/Fire starting point. Its 38/10/3 alternative goes deeper into Arcane rather than relying on as much Fire synergy. |
| [Fire](ledger:classes/mage/fire/) | 16/32/3 | 16/32/3 is a Fire build with a small Arcane Blast component. Compare the deeper Fire version if you prefer less upkeep or want Burning Soul. |
| [Frost](ledger:classes/mage/frost/) | 18/0/33 | 18/0/33 is Method’s single-target build. For regular five-player pack control, compare its 9/5/37 Improved Blizzard option. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-mage-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
