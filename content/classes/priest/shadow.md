## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Solo questing, ranged pressure and party support through Shadow damage.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 36, you have 27 normal points. If you want Shadowform at 40, commit to 31 Shadow points by then or plan one respec if you took Meditation early. Keep Spirit Tap for repeated solo kills. Use Shadow Word: Death as a finisher when retaliation damage would be risky. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 15/3/33 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use a shield or terrain to move between casts. Save Psychic Scream or talented Silence for a threat you can actually stop, and leave Shadowform when a necessary Holy heal outweighs staying in form. Dwarf Chastise changes do not affect Horde priests. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

15/3/33 trades the solo Spirit Tap emphasis for damage and mana support in group content. Try it around 60 when running groups regularly. Keep your levelling and PvP priorities in the other allocation if needed. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Keep Shadow Word: Pain on targets that will live, weave Mind Blast with full Mind Flay channels when safe, and use Devouring Plague where its damage and mana cost are justified. Use Vampiric Embrace for support with its current party healing value. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Current Shadow tuning

The [November hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) reduced Vampiric Embrace’s party healing to 10% of the casting Priest’s Shadow damage and doubled the healing that effect gives the caster. They also fixed Dark Inspiration stacking between Priests and its Spirit gain when triggered by Mind Flay. Evaluate healing support using these numbers, even if an older build still looks useful.

### Gear and practical checks

Check spell upgrades and keep a current wand if you still finish frequent solo kills with it. Evaluate spell hit against the level of the enemies you are actually fighting. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
