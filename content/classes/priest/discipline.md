## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Tank support, pre-emptive shields and fast recovery with Penance.
- **Levels 40–59:** Around 36, keep Penance and the shield talents if you mostly heal dungeons. Aim for Power Infusion at 40 if you have 31 points in Discipline. Take a solo damage second build at 40 if levelling outside groups is slow. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 31/20/0 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** With a Horde levelling partner, shield before an expected switch, use Dispel Magic to free them and keep Psychic Scream for someone closing on you. Avoid a long cast in the open when an interrupt is available. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

31/20/0 is a healing starting point with Discipline’s capstone and Holy support. Published Holy-side point choices vary between Renew, mana and direct-heal talents. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/priest/discipline) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Shield before predictable damage, heal with Penance when the target will need several ticks, and use your direct or group heals for damage already taken. Coordinate Weakened Soul with another Priest and assign Power Infusion ahead of time. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/priest/discipline) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Levelling with a partner

If you are around 36 and already healing dungeons, staying in Discipline until 40 preserves Penance and lets you reach Power Infusion without interrupting the route for a four-level respec. Talk through which pulls need a shield before damage and which need a heal afterward. Keep a wand or direct damage setup for the gaps between pulls. [Priest levelling routes](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60) · [Power Infusion hotfix](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026)

### Gear and practical checks

Keep separate healing and solo pieces if they make a practical difference. Compare mana left at the end of a pull with the same tank and similar shield usage. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/priest/discipline)

### Battleground healing

[Hydra’s 19 November PvP guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-pvp-guide) focuses on position, shields and the opponent’s interrupt. Use its dedicated allocation for a healer queuing battlegrounds. If you are levelling with a partner, practise preserving dispels and a shield for the next player who engages.

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
