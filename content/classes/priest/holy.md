## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Healing several allies with different direct, instant and group heals.
- **Levels 40–59:** Around 36, use Binding Heal when you and an ally are hurt and Prayer of Healing when several party members need it. If you are going deep Holy, plan for Prayer of Mending at 40. Take a solo build alongside it if most of your levelling is outside dungeons. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 18/33/0 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Stay behind allies or terrain and use instant tools and Dispel Magic while repositioning. Save Psychic Scream for a real peel, especially if the fear would run someone into more enemies. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

18/33/0 is a balanced healing start. The 17/34/0 alternative changes shield and threat talents. Prefer efficient heals you can reliably land over a nominal gain on a heal you rarely cast. [Current spec guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/holy/overview-pve-healer) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use the right-sized direct heal on the tank, pass Prayer of Mending before predictable repeated hits, and use Prayer of Healing for real group damage. Keep mana in hand for emergencies rather than casting the fastest heal into every deficit. [Spec guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/holy/overview-pve-healer) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)

### Updating the toolkit

[Wowhead’s 19 November overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/holy/overview-pve-healer) describes the live Holy tree with Binding Heal, Prayer of Mending and Litany of Light. Use those tools when moving into higher-level groups. Plan around the tank’s intake and the number of hurt party members before picking a heal.

### Gear and practical checks

Start with healing and mana gear you can acquire from open dungeons. Track how many heals you can land before drinking between packs. [Spec and gearing guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/priest/holy/overview-pve-healer)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
