## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/priest-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** A Horde Priest questing with a partner can keep Fear Ward and dispels accessible and reserve Psychic Scream for a player joining the pull. On Undead, plan when to trade health for mana with Dark Sacrifice. On Troll, use Shadowguard when pressure is likely.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Discipline](ledger:classes/priest/discipline/) | 31/20/0 | 31/20/0 is a healing starting point with Discipline’s capstone and Holy support. Published Holy-side point choices vary between Renew, mana and direct-heal talents. |
| [Holy](ledger:classes/priest/holy/) | 18/33/0 | 18/33/0 is a balanced healing start. The 17/34/0 alternative changes shield and threat talents. Prefer efficient heals you can reliably land over a nominal gain on a heal you rarely cast. |
| [Shadow](ledger:classes/priest/shadow/) | 15/3/33 | 15/3/33 trades the solo Spirit Tap emphasis for damage and mana support in group content. Try it around 60 when running groups regularly. Keep your levelling and PvP priorities in the other allocation if needed. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-priest-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
