## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Low-downtime Cat questing or Bear tanking with separate priorities and gear.
- **Levels 40–59:** Dire Bear is a level-40 milestone. Plan Shifting Power for your Cat route and the Lacerate talent for Bear pulls. Keep a separate Bear gear set for tanking and practise the form switch before depending on it outdoors. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/druid-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 9/32/10 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use Prowl to choose fights, then use travel, roots or Bear durability to leave when you are outnumbered. Preserve enough mana to shift and heal after a bad trade. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Method’s Cat and main-tank Bear versions both use 9/32/10 with different talents. Its 8/33/10 option is for players who expect to change roles during the same fight. [Current spec guide](https://www.neverff.com/wow-forever/specs/druid/feral) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

In Cat, choose Rake, Rip or a quicker finisher by lifetime and spend Energy without capping. In Bear, establish threat with your up-to-date tank attacks and Lacerate on durable targets. The same 9/32/10 split can have materially different point allocations. [Spec guide](https://www.neverff.com/wow-forever/specs/druid/feral) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### The live Cat and Bear capstones

[Blizzard’s final talent notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/) moved Lacerate into the Feral tree and replaced Berserk with Primal Bite for Bear and Shifting Power for Cat. The 23 November hotfix also repaired the four-piece Feralheart Energy effect. Prefer current talent and gear descriptions when choosing whether to pursue the bonus.

### Gear and practical checks

Keep Bear durability gear separate from your Cat damage gear. Learn which trade-offs matter in forms by testing the same enemy level with the appropriate gear and talents. [Spec and gearing guide](https://www.neverff.com/wow-forever/specs/druid/feral)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
