## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Feral for efficient solo or Bear tanking, Balance for ranged damage, Restoration for healing and mobility.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40 compare Dire Bear and the Feral Cat/Bear talents, Moonkin Form or Wild Growth according to the job you actually play. Keep a separate gear set if you make regular use of a second role. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/druid-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Horde Druids can use Prowl, Travel Form and Bear durability to control whether an outdoor fight continues. Save enough mana to shift and heal if another player joins a pull. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

Feral’s launch tree replaced Berserk with Cat and Bear choices and made Lacerate a talent. Wild Growth’s November fixes make the cast and cooldown important. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Balance](ledger:classes/druid/balance/) | 38/0/13 | Ranged questing, roots and a caster role that can still shift or heal in an emergency. |
| [Feral](ledger:classes/druid/feral/) | 9/32/10 | Low-downtime Cat questing or Bear tanking with separate priorities and gear. |
| [Restoration](ledger:classes/druid/restoration/) | 11/0/40 | Proactive healing over time and mobility between heals. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

Horde Druids can use Prowl, Travel Form and Bear durability to control whether an outdoor fight continues. Save enough mana to shift and heal if another player joins a pull. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
