## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** leather (cloth also works). The beta client lets Druids use staves, daggers, maces, fist weapons and, from level 20, polearms, plus an idol in the relic slot from level 1 ([ForeverDB levelling guide](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-druid)).
- **Roles:** all four. Bear tank and Cat damage share the Feral Combat tree, Balance is the ranged caster and Restoration the healer.
- **Resources:** mana in caster form and Moonkin Form, Rage in Bear Form, Energy and combo points in Cat Form. Shifting into Bear or Cat Form costs 55% of your base mana, so you pick a form before the pull and stay in it.
- **Forms:** [Bear Form](https://foreverdb.net/spell/5487) at 10 and [Aquatic Form](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1066) at 16 from class quests, [Cat Form](https://foreverdb.net/spell/768) at 20 from a new quest, [Travel Form](https://foreverdb.net/spell/783) at 30 from the trainer, Dire Bear Form at 40 and [Moonkin Form](https://foreverdb.net/spell/24858) as the level 40 Balance capstone.
- **Races:** Night Elf and High Order Skyborne (Alliance), Tauren and Windshaper Skyborne (Horde). Wowhead notes the Skyborne are a paid upgrade.
- **Biggest Forever change:** shapeshifting stops getting in the way. Potions, weapon enchants, procs and racials now work in form, and Bear and Cat attacks use your weapon's damage.
- **New tools:** [Revive](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1237951) (out-of-combat resurrection at 12), [Primal Bite](https://foreverdb.net/spell/407995), [Berserk](https://foreverdb.net/spell/417141) and Lacerate for bears, [Wild Growth](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408120) for healers, [Eclipse](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408248) for Balance.
- **Levelling pick:** most guides point a first Druid to Feral. Restoration levels slowly, so ForeverDB suggests levelling as Feral or Balance and respeccing to heal.

## What the guides say

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [class deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301515) frames the Druid update around making forms less restrictive. Almost all consumables now work in form, including Rage potions and Thistle Tea. Weightstones, Crusader and Windfury apply while shifted, and racials such as Shadowmeld and War Stomp work in form. In Bear, Cat and Dire Bear Form your auto-attack damage per second equals your weapon's, at a fixed 1.0 or 2.5 second swing depending on the form. Most engineering bombs still can't be used in form.

Several old talents are now trainer abilities. [Omen of Clarity](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16864) arrives at 20 for every Druid and can proc from spells, and Wrath never consumes it. [Nature's Grasp](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16689) is baseline at 10. [Faerie Fire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/9907) can be cast in Bear or Cat Form for free on a 6 second cooldown, and [Barkskin](https://foreverdb.net/spell/22812) works in form. Entangling Roots can be cast indoors, Hurricane lost its cooldown, and Thorns now scales with the caster's spell power.

Feral lost Tiger's Fury. Its replacement is the [Shifting Power](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1322605) talent, which turns mana into 40 Energy. [Furor](https://foreverdb.net/spell/17056) was reworked so that shifting back into Cat Form returns the Energy you had, not a free 40. Blizzard says this is meant to stop "bear-weaving", and that Shifting Power brings back powershifting in a simpler form. Bears gain Primal Bite (a tier 4 talent at 25), Berserk (the level 40 capstone) and Lacerate (trained at 42). Restoration gains Wild Growth, and [Swiftmend](https://foreverdb.net/spell/18562) no longer eats the heal over time it uses. Rejuvenation, Tranquility and Wild Growth can now crit ([ForeverDB outlook](https://foreverdb.net/article/outlook-druid)).

ForeverDB's [levelling guide](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-druid) adds what the client shows: Mark of the Wild lasts an hour, Bear Form gives far more health than in Classic, Enrage gives Rage instantly, and the early ranks of Wrath and Healing Touch get much more from spell power. Since the 1 October build every form is immune to Disarm and Bear Form gains 75% more Rage from crits. Blizzard's recap says Improved Mark of the Wild became baseline, but ForeverDB found nothing in the client showing the buff got stronger.

### Talents and respecs

You get one talent point per level from 10 to 60, so 11 at 20, 21 at 30 and 51 at 60. Each row of a tree needs five more points spent above it, and Blizzard adds a new milestone talent at 16 points alongside the old ones at 11, 21 and 31. You change talents at a class trainer in a major city, and Dual Specialization opens at 40 ([Blizzard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301515)). The Legacy perk Talented grants points up to five levels early ([ForeverDB talents](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents)). Wowhead's level 30 Balance guide notes that the beta's extra Legacy points won't be there at that level on launch.

Of the 52 talents in Forever's Druid tree, ForeverDB counts 14 new, 23 changed and 15 unchanged from Classic. Because first-row talents in other trees are cheap and strong, mixed builds are common; see [hybrid builds](#hybrid).

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | Role | Guides' short verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Balance | Ranged caster | Steady single-target damage and group buffs. Limited by mana, with no area spell before Hurricane at 40. |
| Feral | Cat damage and Bear tank | The flexible pick: one tree serves both roles, with strong mobility and an easy route into dungeons as a tank. |
| Restoration | Healer | Heals over time that need planning, strong mana regeneration, slow to level alone. |

Blizzard's deep dive is frank about the trade-off: Druids can fill any role but lack snares, silences, interrupts and reliable crowd control, and their healing leans on heals over time. The Bear's [Feral Charge](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1238122) talent does interrupt a cast, going by the client tooltip. Blizzard's stated target of about 5% less damage for hybrid DPS specs applies to Balance and Cat ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)).

### Which spec to level

- **Feral.** ForeverDB's pick for a first Druid: Bear Form is sturdy from 10, Cat Form with Feral Swiftness is fast from 20, and Rake at 24 makes Cat Form a good single-target killer. Icy Veins' [Feral levelling guide](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/feral-druid-leveling) calls it one of the best levelling specs, with slow stretches only before Bear Form at 10 and Cat Form at 20.
- **Balance.** Wowhead's [Balance overview](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/balance/overview-pve-dps) calls it a reasonably safe levelling choice. Its [level 20 guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/balance/level-20-dps-overview) adds that it's slower and more mana-hungry than in Classic, and rewards single pulls, a good caster weapon and consumables.
- **"Spellbear".** Wowhead's [level 30 Balance guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/balance/level-30-dps-overview) found this mixed build (spell power gear, Thorns, Swipe in Bear Form) the fastest it tried at 20 to 30.
- **Restoration.** It can level with Balance talents taken for damage, but ForeverDB and Icy Veins both treat it as slower solo.

Wowhead's [level 20 tank guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/druid/feral/level-20-tank-overview) warns that early form damage is weak. Before 20 most of your damage comes from auto-attacks and Thorns, so caster form with a good weapon can do as well. ForeverDB lists class quests for Bear Form, Cure Poison and Aquatic Form from Classic's data. Wowhead lists Skyborne versions, such as Strength and Mercy for Bear Form. Cat Form comes from The Great Cat Spirit at 20, a stealth quest that Wowhead places in a Moonglade cave and ForeverDB in the Stormrage Barrow Dens. Above level 30 everything is projection from client data: Ferocious Bite and Ravage at 32, Pounce at 36, and Dire Bear Form, Hurricane, Innervate and the 31-point capstones at 40.
