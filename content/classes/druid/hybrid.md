## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/druid-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Horde Druids can use Prowl, Travel Form and Bear durability to control whether an outdoor fight continues. Save enough mana to shift and heal if another player joins a pull.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Balance](ledger:classes/druid/balance/) | 38/0/13 | 38/0/13 is a caster group starting point built around Eclipse and Moonkin Form. Choose the 40s levelling route based on recovery and mobility if you are still spending most of your time outside dungeons. |
| [Feral](ledger:classes/druid/feral/) | 9/32/10 | Method’s Cat and main-tank Bear versions both use 9/32/10 with different talents. Its 8/33/10 option is for players who expect to change roles during the same fight. |
| [Restoration](ledger:classes/druid/restoration/) | 11/0/40 | 11/0/40 is the common healer template. The five-player variant trades Improved Tranquility points for Subtlety when threat matters more to your runs. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
