## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Proactive healing over time and mobility between heals.
- **Levels 40–59:** Take Wild Growth when you reach deep Restoration. Use Rejuvenation for damage you can anticipate and a direct heal for a missed spike. Use a second Feral build if solo combat is most of your time. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/druid-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 11/0/40 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Heal while moving where your spells permit, use roots or terrain to make space, and preserve enough mana for another shift. Wild Growth needs a safe cast window. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

11/0/40 is the common healer template. The five-player variant trades Improved Tranquility points for Subtlety when threat matters more to your runs. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/restoration) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Keep healing over time on the tank when it will be used, combine Swiftmend with its required effects and use Wild Growth for group damage when you can stand and cast. Reserve direct healing for damage that cannot wait. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/restoration) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Build a healing set and keep some mana-focused alternatives for longer fights. Test Wild Growth after the hotfix in a group that can actually benefit from it. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/restoration)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
