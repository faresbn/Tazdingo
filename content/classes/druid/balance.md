## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Ranged questing, roots and a caster role that can still shift or heal in an emergency.
- **Levels 40–59:** Moonkin Form and Hurricane are milestones around 40. Practise the Eclipse rhythm on enemies that will live for multiple casts, and keep shorter setups for fragile targets. Consider your next pull’s mana before opening with every spell. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/druid-leveling-guide-1-60)
- **At level 60:** 38/0/13 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep a line to terrain where you can root, shift and heal. Save a control tool for a player entering mid-pull. Use instant pressure while moving, and pick the form that helps you exit or counter. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

38/0/13 is a caster group starting point built around Eclipse and Moonkin Form. Choose the 40s levelling route based on recovery and mobility if you are still spending most of your time outside dungeons. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/balance) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Wrath to set up faster Starfires with Eclipse, and maintain Moonfire or Insect Swarm where the target’s lifetime justifies them. Use Hurricane when enemies are grouped and you can safely channel. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/balance) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-druid-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Build around spell damage and practical mana needs for the next dungeon. Keep enough mana to shift and escape on a contested levelling route. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/druid/balance)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
