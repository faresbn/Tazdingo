## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Ranged pressure, precise shot timing and damage without depending on a pet.
- **Levels 40–59:** Train the shots available to you and learn Sniper Shot when you reach 31 Marksmanship. Decide whether Lone Wolf’s damage is worth giving up a pet that can hold mobs or help you escape. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/hunter-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 5/41/5 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use range, Concussive Shot and traps to make opponents cross open ground. Leave a route around cover before committing to a hard cast, and consider a pet build for contested questing. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

5/41/5 is the deep ranged Lone Wolf build. The 5/33/13 variant invests in traps and the 16/35/0 alternative keeps a pet. [Current spec guide](https://thetirisfalen.com/wow-forever/guides/hunter-mm-bm-pvp/) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Work shot casts around Auto Shot, use Sniper Shot when it can land and use Multi-Shot only when nearby targets are safe to hit. Keep your planned distance and watch mana on successive pulls. [Spec guide](https://thetirisfalen.com/wow-forever/guides/hunter-mm-bm-pvp/) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Practise your timing with the ranged weapon you intend to keep. Compare ammo cost and time to finish a pull when deciding whether to trade away the pet. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/hunter/marksmanship)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
