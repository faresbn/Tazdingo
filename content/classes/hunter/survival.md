## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Traps, close-range control and switching between bow and melee where the fight allows it.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40, deep Survival can reach Lacerating Strikes. Practise entering melee for useful attacks and leaving between shots. A ranged approach is simpler if you cannot safely stand beside the target. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/hunter-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 0/20/31 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep a control trap ready when the route is busy. Wing Clip can buy space to resume ranged pressure. Avoid forcing melee range against someone who wants you there. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

0/20/31 is a two-handed weaving starting point. Compare 16/10/25 if you want a dedicated melee build and can reliably stay in reach. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/survival-hunter-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

On packs, plan Explosive Trap before movement scatters the enemies. Use Lacerate on targets that will live and weave melee attacks only where you can keep attacking safely. Treat pure melee as a separate setup. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/survival-hunter-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Compare the two-hander and ranged weapon together. Use the melee setup only when you can reach the target often enough to benefit from that weapon. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/survival-hunter-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
