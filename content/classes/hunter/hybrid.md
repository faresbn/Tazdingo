## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/hunter-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** When another player joins a pull, a live pet, a clear line to cover and a control trap give you ways to reset it. Think twice before switching to Lone Wolf if you still need your pet to tank.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Beast Mastery](ledger:classes/hunter/beast-mastery/) | 31/20/0 | 31/20/0 combines Bestial Wrath with ranged support. If the in-game tree locks Trueshot Aura behind more points, leave the extra point in Mortal Shots. |
| [Marksmanship](ledger:classes/hunter/marksmanship/) | 5/41/5 | 5/41/5 is the deep ranged Lone Wolf build. The 5/33/13 variant invests in traps and the 16/35/0 alternative keeps a pet. |
| [Survival](ledger:classes/hunter/survival/) | 0/20/31 | 0/20/31 is a two-handed weaving starting point. Compare 16/10/25 if you want a dedicated melee build and can reliably stay in reach. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
