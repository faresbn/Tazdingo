## At a glance

- **Why mixing is common:** One talent point per level from 10 to 60 (51 in all), and each row of a tree needs 5 more points spent in that tree. With 21 points at the level 30 beta cap, the cheap top-row talents of a second tree are tempting.
- **Milestones:** Blizzard's trees have one-point milestone talents at 11, 16 (new in Forever), 21 and 31 points.
- **Legacy points:** The account-wide Legacy perk Talented gives up to five extra points early ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents)), which most guides spend deeper in the main tree.
- **The usual borrowed talents:** Deadly Aspects (Beast Mastery tier 1), Lethal Attacks (Marksmanship tier 1, 5% crit on all attacks), Careful Aim (Marksmanship tier 2) and Improved Tracking (Survival tier 1).
- **Hybrid tax:** Doesn't apply. Blizzard's roughly 5% damage gap is aimed at hybrid classes, and Hunters are one of the four pure classes that set the bar.
- **Respecs:** 1 silver during the beta if you haven't reset recently (launch price unknown). Dual Specialization opens at level 40.

## What the guides say

### The hybrid tax, briefly

On the WoW: Forever podcast, Blizzard said it wants hybrid damage specs to deal about 5% less than Hunters, Mages, Rogues and Warlocks. It defined a hybrid as a class that can fill more than one role, which is why even Warriors count ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)). The rule is about classes, not about mixing talent trees, so a split Hunter build pays nothing for it. The 5% is a stated design target, not a number in the client.

### Levelling hybrids

**Wowhead's level 20 splits.** Wowhead's [Beast Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/beast-mastery/level-20-dps-overview) and [Marksmanship](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/marksmanship/level-20-dps-overview) pages both recommend 7/4/0 at level 20. That's Deadly Aspects 5 and Focused Fire 2 in Beast Mastery, plus 4 points of Lethal Attacks. Its reasoning is that the Marksmanship tree is awkward early, and a pet still beats Lone Wolf. The [Survival page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/survival/level-20-dps-overview) uses 0/4/7: Lethal Attacks 4, Improved Tracking 5 and Savage Strikes 2. By level 30 all three Wowhead builds are single-tree.

**Icy Veins' paths to 60.** Each Icy Veins levelling page finishes one tree's capstone, then moves into a second tree:

| Spec | Path | At 60 |
|---|---|---|
| [Beast Mastery](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling) | 31 points to Bestial Wrath at 40, then Lethal Attacks and Careful Aim, then Endurance Training, Spirit Bond and Hawk Eye | 39/12/0 |
| [Marksmanship](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/marksmanship-hunter-leveling) | Sniper Shot at 40, then Deadly Aspects, Focused Fire and Unleashed Fury up to Summon Hawk | 19/32/0 |
| [Survival](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/survival-hunter-leveling) | Lethal Attacks first (so Strider Kick waits until 35), Lacerating Strikes at 45, then Careful Aim, Lone Wolf, Improved Stings and Mortal Shots | 0/17/34 |

Icy Veins' Marksmanship page also says its 21-point build is for dungeons only. For general play at 30, it suggests the Beast Mastery build for Deadly Aspects and Pathfinding.

**ForeverDB stays in one tree to 30.** All of [ForeverDB's builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/builds) are single-tree at 20 and 30. Its Survival "hybrid melee" build is a hybrid of play style, ranged pulls into melee finishes, not of trees.

### Level 60 projections

The [WoWSims presets](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/hunter/dps/presets.ts) are described as the best build found for each tree and play style, using first-phase gear over a three-minute fight. Most of them mix trees. The talent strings below are decoded against the simulator's own Forever talent tree.

| Preset | Split | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SV Weave (default) | 0/20/31 | Lethal Attacks, Improved Stings, Careful Aim, Lone Wolf, Trueshot Aura, Mortal Shots; Survival to Lacerating Strikes; no pet |
| MM Weave | 0/26/25 | Marksmanship to Mortal Shots and a point of Ranged Weapon Specialization; Survival to Strider Kick and Expose Prey; no pet |
| BM and BM Weave | 31/16/4 | Bestial Wrath, plus Lethal Attacks, Careful Aim and Trueshot Aura; raptor pet with hawks |
| MM | 5/41/5 | Deadly Aspects and Improved Tracking around a deep Marksmanship tree without Sniper Shot; no pet |
| SV | 1/20/30 | Ranged Survival using Multi-Shot instead of Aimed Shot; no pet |
| SV Melee | 18/8/25 | Dual-wield melee with Deadly Aspects, Unleashed Fury and Summon Hawk for the raptor and hawks |

These are simulations with unconfirmed level 60 data, not results anyone has played. They show which cross-tree talents the simulator rates: Lethal Attacks and Careful Aim appear in nearly every build, and Trueshot Aura in most.

### Where the guides disagree

- **Split or stay pure early:** Wowhead splits at level 20; ForeverDB and Icy Veins' main guides stay in one tree until 30.
- **Lone Wolf:** the level 20–30 guides mostly keep the pet. Icy Veins takes Lone Wolf only for dungeons and Wowhead only in its Legacy-points variant, while Icy Veins' Survival path and most WoWSims presets take it by 60.
