## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Safe solo questing, pet threat and damage while you keep moving.
- **Levels 40–59:** Take Bestial Wrath at 31 Beast Mastery points. Keep your pet trained and fed, and make sure it can stay on what you send it to. Build into Marksmanship when your pet core is in place. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/hunter-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 31/20/0 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Intimidation for a stop or a retreat. Use a trap and range to make space, and manually recall your pet when an opponent is trying to pull it out of line of sight. An alive pet keeps your escape options open. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

31/20/0 combines Bestial Wrath with ranged support. If the in-game tree locks Trueshot Aura behind more points, leave the extra point in Mortal Shots. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/beast-mastery-hunter-guide-ludideo) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Send the pet in under control and let Auto Shot and useful instant shots do their work. On packs, check pet positioning and hold Multi-Shot if a target is trapped or the tank has yet to gather the pull. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/beast-mastery-hunter-guide-ludideo) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/)

### Pet tuning

The [November hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) brought the Beast Mastery pet health bonus down. Set up for the pulls your pet survives now, and use a second allocation for a distinct job before optimising for raid damage.

### Gear and practical checks

Replace a weak ranged weapon and train the pet abilities you use. Avoid judging two builds with pets at different levels or with different attack skills. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/beast-mastery-hunter-guide-ludideo)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
