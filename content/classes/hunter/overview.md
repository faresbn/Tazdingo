## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Beast Mastery for safe solo progress, Marksmanship for ranged pressure and Survival for traps and ranged-to-melee play.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40 compare Bestial Wrath, Sniper Shot and deep Survival. A current ranged weapon and usable pet skills can matter more to the next quest than chasing a full level-60 template. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/hunter-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** When another player joins a pull, a live pet, a clear line to cover and a control trap give you ways to reset it. Think twice before switching to Lone Wolf if you still need your pet to tank. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

The November hotfixes changed Beast Mastery pet health scaling and corrected Volley procs and movement. Use current pet and trap guidance rather than early launch assumptions. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Beast Mastery](ledger:classes/hunter/beast-mastery/) | 31/20/0 | Safe solo questing, pet threat and damage while you keep moving. |
| [Marksmanship](ledger:classes/hunter/marksmanship/) | 5/41/5 | Ranged pressure, precise shot timing and damage without depending on a pet. |
| [Survival](ledger:classes/hunter/survival/) | 0/20/31 | Traps, close-range control and switching between bow and melee where the fight allows it. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-hunter-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

When another player joins a pull, a live pet, a clear line to cover and a control trap give you ways to reset it. Think twice before switching to Lone Wolf if you still need your pet to tank. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
