## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** Cloth and leather from level 1, mail from 40 (above the beta cap). Bows, guns, crossbows and thrown weapons in the ranged slot; axes, swords, daggers, fist weapons, staves and (from 20) polearms in melee. No maces. Dual-wield from 20 ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-hunter)).
- **Roles:** Damage only. Blizzard describes the pet as a tank for solo and small-group play.
- **Resource:** Mana for shots, stings, traps and melee abilities. Auto Shot costs nothing. The pet spends its own Focus.
- **Pets:** Tamed at level 10 through the class quest. Every family now has a unique ability, Foxes are a new family, and pets scale with your gear instead of taking other players' buffs.
- **Specs:** Beast Mastery (pet and hawks), Marksmanship (your own shots, with an optional petless build) and Survival, which is now a melee spec from level 30.
- **Pure class:** Blizzard counts Hunters among the four pure damage classes that set the bar for its roughly 5% "hybrid tax", so no tax applies to them ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)).
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Night Elf and High Order Skyborne (Alliance); Orc, Tauren, Troll and Windshaper Skyborne (Horde). Human and Skyborne Hunters are new.

## What the guides say

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [class deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301515) keeps the Classic foundation but moves several talents into the baseline kit:

- [Aimed Shot](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19434) is a trainer ability at level 20 for every spec. It now shares a 6-second cooldown with Multi-Shot instead of Arcane Shot, has a 2-second cast and hits far less hard than Classic's talent version ([ForeverDB changes](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/changes)).
- Traps work in combat, but their cooldown rose to 30 seconds. Fire and Frost traps have separate cooldowns, and you can have one of each down.
- Aspect of the Beast adds melee attack power, Scorpid Sting now lowers the target's hit chance by 2%, and Disengage sheds twice the threat.
- Rapid Fire speeds up melee as well as ranged attacks. Multi-Shot is a single rank costing a share of base mana. Feign Death now kills you if you leave it running for its full 6 minutes.
- Melee, ranged and spell hit are one stat, which Blizzard says makes traps more reliable.

Early in the beta, Auto Shot fired without its 0.5-second wind-up. Blizzard called this a bug, and Wowhead's overview pages say the cast time is back. Plan around standing still briefly for each shot.

### Pets

Pets now inherit part of your stats. Wowhead lists the rules shown in the pet panel: 2 pet health per point of your Stamina, 30% of your armour, 10% of your highest attack power and all of your crit chance. Other players' buffs no longer apply to pets. You can no longer tame a beast above your own level ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-hunter)).

Icy Veins adds that pets are classed as Ferocity, Cunning or Tenacity with no built-in damage difference, and that higher ability ranks still come from taming a beast that knows them. ForeverDB says the client doesn't show how ranks unlock, so treat that as unconfirmed.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | What it does | Levelling verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Beast Mastery | Pet and hawks do much of the work | Icy Veins calls it the strongest leveller in the game; Wowhead calls it the easiest of the three |
| Marksmanship | Your own shots, mana tools, Trueshot Aura | Strong, but behind BM (Icy Veins); Wowhead finds the tree awkward before 30 |
| Survival | Melee strikes plus traps | The weakest leveller, especially for the first 40 levels (Icy Veins); a real melee spec only from 30 (ForeverDB) or about 32 (Wowhead) |

Every source agrees on keeping the pet out while levelling. ForeverDB's simulator puts the loss without one at roughly 20–27% of your damage at levels 20–30, whatever the spec. Talents cost 1 silver to reset during the beta; the launch price isn't known. Blizzard says Dual Specialization opens at level 40.

### Which spec to level

Beast Mastery is the consensus pick. The pet holds mobs, Summon Hawk arrives at 25 and Bestial Wrath at 40 ([Icy Veins levelling](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling)). Wowhead's level 20 Beast Mastery build borrows four points of Marksmanship's Lethal Attacks (see [Hybrid builds](hybrid.md)). Survival players can level at range like the other specs until the melee talents arrive.

### Races and professions

The guides don't agree on race. Icy Veins rates Tauren best overall for Plainsrunning's kiting speed, with Human ahead for the Alliance. Wowhead rates Night Elf (Elune's Light) and Orc (Blood Fury) highest, with Skyborne strongest against Elementals. Sources also describe the Human passive differently: Icy Veins says it needs a sword, and Wowhead's Hunter pages say it only helps melee. ForeverDB reports that Blizzard's 16 October build made Ruthless a flat 2% crit on all attacks, so the older descriptions may be out of date. Skinning with Leatherworking is the shared recommendation, since many strong low-level pieces are crafted. Wowhead also suggests Engineering.

### Looking ahead to 60

The [WoWSims presets](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/hunter/dps/presets.ts) model level 60 with first-phase gear that is still marked developer-only. They offer seven builds across all three trees, and the default is a Survival-Marksmanship build that walks into melee between Auto Shots. Treat it as a projection, not a tested result.
