## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Beast Mastery for solo levelling, the consensus pick: the pet holds mobs and hawks arrive at 25 ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling)). Marksmanship if your own shots should matter more. Survival only as a deliberate melee choice: the weakest leveller, with key tools from 30.
- **Key levels:** 10 first pet (class quest) · 20 [Aimed Shot](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19434), dual-wield and polearms · 25 Summon Hawk (Beast Mastery) · 30 Survival's melee kit · 40 mail, Dual Specialization and Bestial Wrath.
- **New in Forever:** Aimed Shot is baseline and shares a 6 sec cooldown with Multi-Shot; traps work in combat on a 30 sec cooldown; each pet family has a unique ability and pets scale with your gear; hit is one stat; Survival is melee ([Blizzard](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301515)).
- **Professions:** Skinning and Leatherworking for self-crafted levelling gear; Engineering for tools, at a material cost. Check whether a piece is bind-on-pickup before planning to buy it.
- **Race:** after the 26 October notes, Orc Command is 5% pet damage only (nothing for Lone Wolf), Savage is gone, Human is 1% crit and Tauren Plainsrunning is unmounted only ([Blizzard](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7)). Pet-heavy Orc builds gain; there is no universal winner.
- **Watch out:** keep the pet out while levelling; ForeverDB's sim puts losing it at 20–27% of your damage at 20–30. Don't read WoWSims' petless level-60 default as levelling advice. Auto Shot's 0.5 sec wind-up is back, so stand still briefly for each shot.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

**Beast Mastery with a pet is the default for solo levelling.** Marksmanship suits players who want their own shots to matter more, while Survival is a deliberate melee choice whose key tools arrive later. Do not turn a level-60 petless simulation into a recommendation to dismiss the levelling tank. Weapon upgrades, ammunition and pet care are more useful early priorities than chasing a projected raid ranking ([Beast Mastery](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/beast-mastery/level-30-dps-overview), [Marksmanship](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/hunter/marksmanship/level-30-dps-overview)).

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [class deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301515) keeps the Classic foundation but moves several talents into the baseline kit:

- [Aimed Shot](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19434) is a trainer ability at level 20 for every spec. It now shares a 6-second cooldown with Multi-Shot instead of Arcane Shot, has a 2-second cast and hits far less hard than Classic's talent version ([ForeverDB changes](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/changes)).
- Traps work in combat, but their cooldown rose to 30 seconds. Fire and Frost traps have separate cooldowns, and you can have one of each down.
- Aspect of the Beast adds melee attack power, Scorpid Sting now lowers the target's hit chance by 2%, and Disengage sheds twice the threat.
- Rapid Fire speeds up melee as well as ranged attacks. Multi-Shot is a single rank costing a share of base mana. Feign Death now kills you if you leave it running for its full 6 minutes.
- Melee, ranged and spell hit are one stat, which Blizzard says makes traps more reliable.

Early in the beta, Auto Shot fired without its 0.5-second wind-up. Blizzard called this a bug, and Wowhead's overview pages say the cast time is back. Plan around standing still briefly for each shot.

### Pets

Pets now inherit part of your stats. Wowhead lists the rules shown in the pet panel: 2 pet health per point of your Stamina, 30% of your armour, 10% of your highest attack power and all of your crit chance. Other players' buffs no longer apply to pets. You can no longer tame a beast above your own level ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-hunter)).

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | What it does | Levelling verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Beast Mastery | Pet and hawks do much of the work | Icy Veins calls it the strongest leveller in the game; Wowhead calls it the easiest of the three |
| Marksmanship | Your own shots, mana tools, Trueshot Aura | Strong, but behind BM (Icy Veins); Wowhead finds the tree awkward before 30 |
| Survival | Melee strikes plus traps | The weakest leveller, especially for the first 40 levels (Icy Veins); a real melee spec only from 30 (ForeverDB) or about 32 (Wowhead) |

Every source agrees on keeping the pet out while levelling. ForeverDB's simulator puts the loss without one at roughly 20–27% of your damage at levels 20–30, whatever the spec. Talents cost 1 silver to reset during the beta; the launch price isn't known. Blizzard says Dual Specialization opens at level 40.

### Which spec to level

Beast Mastery is the consensus pick. The pet holds mobs, Summon Hawk arrives at 25 and Bestial Wrath at 40 ([Icy Veins levelling](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/beast-mastery-hunter-leveling)). Wowhead's level 20 Beast Mastery build borrows four points of Marksmanship's Lethal Attacks (see [Hybrid builds](hybrid.md)). Survival players can level at range like the other specs until the melee talents arrive.

### Races and professions

**Race:** Blizzard's [26 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7) supersede the guides' older comparisons. Orc Command adds **5% pet damage**, not 5% total Hunter damage; its value depends on pet contribution and it does nothing for a petless Lone Wolf setup. Savage is gone. Human Ruthless is 1% crit, without the old sword-only wording. Tauren Plainsrunning now caps at 60% **unmounted** speed and gives no mounted bonus. These changes give pet-heavy Orc builds a stronger case, but do not establish a universal race winner across levelling, petless damage and PvP.

**Professions:** Skinning and Leatherworking support self-crafted levelling gear; Engineering offers tools at a material cost. Check whether a desired piece is bind-on-pickup before assuming it can be bought. A useful camp buff can be shared by another crafter and is not, by itself, a reason to take that profession.

### Looking ahead to 60

The [WoWSims presets](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/hunter/dps/presets.ts) model level 60 with first-phase gear that is still marked developer-only. They offer seven builds across all three trees, and the default is a Survival-Marksmanship build that walks into melee between Auto Shots. Treat it as a projection, not a tested result.
