## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Stealth lets you choose where to take a fight. Keep at least one reset tool ready, and use a dedicated PvP allocation when control and cooldowns matter more than a full damage cycle.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Assassination](ledger:classes/rogue/assassination/) | 31/19/1 | 31/19/1 is a poison-focused PvE starting point. The 33/18/0 alternative changes hit, weapon skill and utility picks. Check your actual daggers and group assignment before copying either. |
| [Combat](ledger:classes/rogue/combat/) | 16/35/0 | 16/35/0 is the straight Combat PvE choice. Compare the 21/30/0 Venom route if poison damage interests you and you are comfortable trading away Adrenaline Rush. |
| [Subtlety](ledger:classes/rogue/subtlety/) | 12/8/31 | The 12/8/31 guide build is for PvE bleeds and utility. Its Combat points can help hit; a PvP build puts a different value on Stealth, cooldowns and control. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
