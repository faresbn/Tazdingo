## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Reliable solo levelling, flexible weapons and two-target cleave.
- **Levels 40–59:** Get Adrenaline Rush at 31 Combat points if this is your main path. Keep your main-hand weapon current and fit the weapon talents to what you actually own. Use Blade Flurry when the second target is safe to hit. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 16/35/0 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Sprint ready for a reset and consider talents that improve its availability if open-world interruptions are common. Clear stray mobs before committing major cooldowns to an opponent. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

16/35/0 is the straight Combat PvE choice. Compare the 21/30/0 Venom route if poison damage interests you and you are comfortable trading away Adrenaline Rush. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/combat-rogue-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Fit Slice and Dice to the pull’s length, then use your builder and a finisher that will get value before the target dies. Pair Adrenaline Rush with a pull where you can keep attacking. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/combat-rogue-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Base weapon talents on your current main hand and choose a suitable off hand. Carry poisons for both group damage and slows outdoors. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/combat-rogue-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
