## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Stealth openers, burst and bleeds, playable from any side with Hemorrhage at 30; strong in PvP, underwhelming at 20 (Wowhead).
- **Levelling build:** at 30, ForeverDB and Icy Veins: Opportunity 2, Camouflage 4, Improved Ambush 3, Setup 3, Ghostly Strike, Initiative 3, Premeditation, Serrated Blades 3, [Hemorrhage](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16511). Wowhead swaps Ghostly Strike and Improved Ambush for Master of Deception, Elusiveness and Dirty Tricks ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/rogue/subtlety/level-30-dps-overview)).
- **Build at 60:** projection only: WoWSims' "Subtlety Hemo" (20/0/31) takes Quietus and Thousand Cuts plus Assassination's Malice, Ruthlessness, Relentless Strikes, Lethality and Cold Blood; "Subtlety BS" (12/8/31) is the dagger version, with Cutthroat, Thousand Cuts and Combat's Puncturing Wounds ([WoWSims](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/rogue/dps/presets.ts)).
- **Priority:** at 30, open from Stealth with Garrote (Wowhead) or Ambush (Icy Veins, ForeverDB), build with Hemorrhage, and finish with Rupture on long fights, Eviscerate on short ones; Vanish and Preparation reopen ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/rogue/subtlety/level-30-dps-overview)). At 60 (WoWSims), Garrote opener, Ambush on Cutthroat procs, Rupture at 3+ points.
- **Stats:** main-hand dagger damage for Ambush and Backstab, then hit and expertise, Agility and crit. WoWSims' level-60 weights: expertise 2.42, hit 2.12, Agility 1.93, crit 1.63, attack power 1 ([WoWSims](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/rogue/dps/presets.ts)).
- **Key levels:** 18 Ambush · 20 Poisons class quest · 30 Hemorrhage · 35–40 [Quietus](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310728), [Cutthroat](https://foreverdb.net/spell/462708) and [Thousand Cuts](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310721).
- **Gear and consumables:** Icy Veins at 30: Torturing Poker main hand, Serrated Raptor Claw or Subdued Dragon's Fang off hand; Wowhead prefers the Ruby-Adorned Blade from The Eye of Bhossca. Instant Poison on both weapons while levelling. ForeverDB's sim: Elixir of Agility and Tender Wolf Steak, about 3.3% each.
- **Watch out:** Icy Veins' Subtlety pages date from 8 October and still mention a level 20 cap in places; it says poisons last an hour, where ForeverDB reads 30 minutes from the client ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/subtlety-rogue-leveling)). Backstab is hard to land solo before Hemorrhage; bleed-immune enemies blunt the spec.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Choose the opener for the fight: **Ambush for immediate dagger burst, Garrote for a target that will live through its bleed, Cheap Shot when control is worth more than damage**. Those are genuine alternatives, not a factual disagreement. At 30, Hemorrhage makes front-facing solo play easier; use Rupture only where it can tick and the enemy is susceptible ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/rogue/subtlety/level-30-dps-overview)). Weapon swapping adds complexity and can affect swing timing; learn a consistent basic setup first. Keep Vanish available for survival when spending it on another opener would be unsafe.

### Talents by level

ForeverDB's levelling build and Icy Veins' level 30 build are point-for-point the same. Wowhead takes a different route.

| Level | ForeverDB levelling build and Icy Veins | Wowhead |
|---|---|---|
| 10–20 | Opportunity 2, Camouflage 3, Improved Ambush 3, Setup 2, Ghostly Strike | Dagger build 0/9/2 (Combat's Improved Eviscerate, Improved Sinister Strike, Puncturing Wounds, Precision, plus Opportunity 2), or an Ambush build: Malice 1, Improved Sinister Strike 2, Master of Deception 3, Opportunity 2, Improved Ambush 3 |
| 21–30 | Setup 3, Initiative 3, Premeditation, Serrated Blades 3, Camouflage 4, Hemorrhage | Master of Deception 3, Opportunity 2, Camouflage 1, Setup 3, Elusiveness 2, Dirty Tricks 2, Initiative 3, Premeditation, Serrated Blades 3, Hemorrhage |

The 21–30 row for ForeverDB and Icy Veins lists the points added after 20; Wowhead's level 30 build is shown in full.

Icy Veins' [levelling page](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/subtlety-rogue-leveling) swaps the fourth Camouflage point for Elusiveness, then takes Preparation and a second Elusiveness point before moving into Assassination (Remorseless Attacks, Malice, Ruthlessness, Improved Slice and Dice, Relentless Strikes, Lethality) and Combat (Improved Eviscerate, Lightning Reflexes, Precision). That ends at 19/9/23. It says there's little reason to go deeper in Subtlety while levelling once you have Hemorrhage. For Legacy points, Icy Veins suggests Preparation and Remorseless Attacks; Wowhead suggests Dirty Deeds, Preparation and Ghostly Strike. [ForeverDB's group build](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/builds) trades Setup for Dirty Tricks and Elusiveness.

[WoWSims](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/rogue/dps/presets.ts) has two level 60 Subtlety builds. "Subtlety Hemo" (20/0/31) takes Quietus and Thousand Cuts with Assassination's Malice, Ruthlessness, Murder, Improved Slice and Dice, Relentless Strikes, Lethality, Cold Blood and Improved Poisons. "Subtlety BS" (12/8/31) takes Cutthroat and Thousand Cuts for a dagger build, with Puncturing Wounds from Combat.

### Rotation and priorities

- **Wowhead ([level 30](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/rogue/subtlety/level-30-dps-overview)):** Always open with Garrote from Stealth, and use Vanish and Preparation to reapply Garrote once it has run out. Keep Slice and Dice up. Expose Armor for physical groups. Spend spare points on Eviscerate against low armour and Rupture against high armour, and otherwise press Hemorrhage.
- **Icy Veins:** Always open with Ambush. Before 30, build with Backstab from behind or Sinister Strike from the front. From 30, use Hemorrhage for every combo point. Use Rupture on targets that will live its full duration; Icy Veins rates it above Slice and Dice at 30. Pull threat? Evasion (Setup turns the dodges into combo points) or Vanish.
- **ForeverDB:** Premeditation then Ambush for 4 combo points, or Premeditation then Cheap Shot for 5 on a dangerous mob. Build with Hemorrhage and use Ghostly Strike when ready. Eviscerate on short fights, Rupture on long ones. In groups, keep Hemorrhage's debuff on the boss and use 5-point Ruptures ([builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/builds)).
- **WoWSims, level 60 ([default APL](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/rogue/dps/apls/default.apl.json)):** Open with Garrote, use Vanish to open again and Preparation once Vanish is on cooldown. Premeditation at 3 points or fewer. Ambush whenever Cutthroat procs. Keep Slice and Dice up. Rupture at 3 or more points when Hemorrhage's debuff is on the target or Thousand Cuts is talented. Eviscerate at 5 points, but with Thousand Cuts only while Rupture has at least 4 seconds left. For builders, Ghostly Strike comes first. The Hemorrhage build then presses Hemorrhage, while the Backstab build uses Hemorrhage only to keep its debuff up and fills with Backstab.

### Levelling

Stealth does little until Ambush at 18, and Backstab is hard to land solo before Hemorrhage ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/subtlety-rogue-leveling)). Icy Veins offers weapon-swap macros: open with a dagger, then swap to a slow main hand for Sinister Strike (see [Macros](macros.md)). The other option is Gouge, then step behind for Backstab. Icy Veins also suggests Master of Deception if mobs keep spotting you, particularly for the level 20 class quest. Wowhead's levelling advice for all Rogue specs is to keep the main-hand weapon up to date.

### Gear and consumables

Icy Veins' level 30 list matches its Assassination list: Torturing Poker main hand, Serrated Raptor Claw or Subdued Dragon's Fang off hand, Brawler's and Skulker's Leatherworking pieces, Triprunner Dungarees, Ironspine's Eye and the Pyrewood Signet Ring. Wowhead recommends the Ruby-Adorned Blade from The Eye of Bhossca at 30. Use Instant Poison on both weapons while levelling; Icy Veins says Deadly Poison is better in long fights. [Elixir of Agility](https://foreverdb.net/item/8949) and [Tender Wolf Steak](https://foreverdb.net/item/18045) are worth about 3.3% each in ForeverDB's simulator.

### Where the guides disagree

- **Opener:** Garrote (Wowhead, the WoWSims default) or Ambush (Icy Veins, ForeverDB).
- **Level 20 build:** Subtlety from the start (ForeverDB, Icy Veins) or a Combat dagger split (Wowhead).
- **Ghostly Strike and Improved Ambush:** in ForeverDB's and Icy Veins' level 30 builds, but not in Wowhead's.
- **Poison duration:** Icy Veins says one hour per coat; ForeverDB reads 30 minutes from the client's item data.
- **Age:** Icy Veins' Subtlety pages were last updated on 8 October and still mention a level 20 cap in places.
