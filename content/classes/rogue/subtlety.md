## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Choosing fights from stealth, controlling enemies and using bleeds on targets that survive them.
- **Levels 40–59:** Build around your stealth and bleed tools if you value contested levelling. Test the same three or four mobs on a Combat setup if time per kill matters more than being able to skip or reset. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 12/8/31 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Lead with control when you need it, use Stealth before crossing exposed ground, and reserve a second stop for the opponent’s response. Perplexity’s 60 PvP guide covers a dedicated allocation. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

The 12/8/31 guide build is for PvE bleeds and utility. Its Combat points can help hit; a PvP build puts a different value on Stealth, cooldowns and control. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-pvp-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Keep bleeds on durable targets where appropriate and use Death Mark when enough of your damage will land during its window. On short pulls, use the damage you can deliver before target switching breaks your setup. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-pvp-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Death Mark and target switches

The [level-60 allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/) uses Death Mark alongside Boot Knife and Dirty Deeds. [Perplexity’s 23 November PvP guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-pvp-guide) covers separate talent choices for controlling an opponent or escaping. The [November fixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) changed several Shank interactions, so use a current priority when switching targets.

### Gear and practical checks

Carry gear that supports the opener you actually use. A setup for sustained bleeds on bosses may need to give up some offensive points for control outdoors. [Level-60 talent comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
