## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Poison-heavy dagger damage, quick combo points and short-range burst.
- **Levels 40–59:** Once your daggers and poison supply are up to date, build around Mutilate and Venom. If your weapons are much stronger outside daggers, Combat is an easier progression change than forcing a weak Mutilate set. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 31/19/1 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Carry Crippling Poison when you expect to be chased or kited. Open only when you can finish the trade or escape it, and keep Blind and Vanish for the opponent rather than the mob. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

31/19/1 is a poison-focused PvE starting point. The 33/18/0 alternative changes hit, weapon skill and utility picks. Check your actual daggers and group assignment before copying either. [Current spec guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/assassination-rogue/) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Build points with Mutilate against poisoned targets, then fit Slice and Dice and finishers to target lifetime. Use a shorter setup on trash that will die before Deadly Poison or longer effects earn the time. [Spec guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/assassination-rogue/) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-rogue-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Look for good daggers and keep poison ranks and supplies current. An old dagger can make an otherwise viable talent test misleading. [Spec and gearing guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/assassination-rogue/)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
