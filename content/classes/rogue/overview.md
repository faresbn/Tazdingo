## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Combat for repeatable quest kills, Assassination with strong daggers and poisons, Subtlety for choosing fights and controlling them.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40 compare Adrenaline Rush, Venom or Death Mark as appropriate to your talents. Keep weapons and poisons current, then judge whether your damage setup earns its time on real dungeon trash. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/rogue-leveling-guide)

Keep at least one reset tool ready, and use a dedicated PvP allocation when control and cooldowns matter more than a full damage cycle. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

Stealth lets you choose where to take a fight. Keep at least one reset tool ready, and use a dedicated PvP allocation when control and cooldowns matter more than a full damage cycle. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

### Rogue client build 1.60.1.70790 reshuffled talents (testing, 5 Nov 2026)

Read in [ForeverDB's 4 November build comparison](https://foreverdb.net/changelog/1.60.1.70790) and its [client-data explanation](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70790-game-files). Compared with 1.60.1.70755, the client files contain extensive Rogue talent movement and renaming, including Mutilate moving to tier 7 (level 40), new Relentless Strikes, and the new Shank ability at level 24. The earlier level-30 builds and key-level recommendations on this page need rechecking against the changed talent layout. This is a source-derived client comparison, not a verified in-game damage test or evidence about server-only behaviour.
