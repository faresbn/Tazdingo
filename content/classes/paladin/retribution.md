## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Two-handed solo play, burst and support for a levelling partner.
- **Levels 40–59:** Work towards the deep Retribution capstone Unsealed Wrath if you want to play around Seal changes. Seek a current two-hander and learn the new skill ranks as you go. Keep healing and threat tools within reach even while doing damage. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/paladin-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 12/8/31 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Reserve Hammer of Justice for a kill or escape. Blessing of Freedom, Cleanse and an emergency heal can save a partner or let you reset behind cover. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

12/8/31 is a level-60 group starting point for Unsealed Wrath and paladin utility. Keep pursuit or defensive talents if your regular play includes more contested travel than stationary bosses. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/retribution-paladin-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Keep a useful Seal active and use Judgement and your weapon strikes. With Unsealed Wrath, use Seal changes to release stored copies at an appropriate time. On short pulls, stop setting up once the enemy would die to a simpler attack. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/retribution-paladin-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Start with a current two-hander and keep a shield or healing weapon in your bags for emergencies. Ask if Judgement of Command is part of the group setup before tuning around its procs. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/retribution-paladin-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
