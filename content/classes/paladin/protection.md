## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Shield tanking, controlling melee packs and helping a group recover from loose enemies.
- **Levels 40–59:** Around 40, bring your shield, Righteous Fury and the available 31-point tools into dungeon pulls. Begin with manageable packs. Caster-heavy pulls need corners and line of sight more than another Consecration. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/paladin-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 0/35/16 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Carry a shield outdoors. Use Hammer of Justice or Blessing of Freedom to disengage and Cleanse to help a companion. Leave space to get away while several mobs are still on you. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

0/35/16 is a dedicated tank starting point. The guide comparison lists a different dungeon option; choose extra damage or sustain only once missed casts and loose enemies are under control. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/profile/deadly-storm-fto57c/classes/protection-paladin-leveling-guide-level-1-60-talents-rotations-races-and-more) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Pull from range when the room allows, use your tank seal and Judgement to recover a loose enemy, and establish threat with Consecration and shield abilities. Adjust Consecration use to fight length and mana. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/profile/deadly-storm-fto57c/classes/protection-paladin-leveling-guide-level-1-60-talents-rotations-races-and-more) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Carry a current shield and avoid evaluating tank gear just by Consecration damage. Check durability when threat is already holding. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/profile/deadly-storm-fto57c/classes/protection-paladin-leveling-guide-level-1-60-talents-rotations-races-and-more)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
