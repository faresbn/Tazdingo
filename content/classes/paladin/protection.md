## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** a Mana tank that holds packs with Consecration and Retribution Aura; also safe solo levelling with sword and board after 20.
- **Levelling build:** at 30, Wowhead takes Redoubt 5, Precision 3, Anticipation 2, Improved Seal of Fury, Improved Righteous Fury, Shield Specialization 3, Sacred Duty 2, One-Handed Weapon Specialization 3 and Templar's Bulwark ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/level-30-tank-overview)); ForeverDB's dungeon build trades the weapon talent for Improved Righteous Fury 3 and Swift Judgement.
- **Build at 60:** projection only: WoWSims' default is 34 Protection (Swift Judgement, Templar's Bulwark, Reckoning, Iron Creed, Holy Shield), 8 Holy (Divine Strength, Improved Seals) and 9 Retribution (Deflection, Improved Judgement, Holy Conduit) ([WoWSims](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/paladin/protection/presets.ts)).
- **Priority:** at 30: Righteous Fury, then Seal of Fury, Judgement, Holy Strike, Exorcism on Undead and Demons, and Consecration when Mana allows (first on packs) ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/level-30-tank-overview)). Icy Veins saves Judgement to taunt loose enemies at first. At 60 WoWSims adds Swift Judgement and Holy Shield.
- **Stats:** ForeverDB: Defense, Stamina, armour, block value, then hit, Strength and spell power; Icy Veins puts spell power second, after Stamina ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-paladin-leveling)). Take survival when pulls are dangerous, threat when enemies escape.
- **Key levels:** 16 Righteous Fury and Retribution Aura (two-hander until then) · 20 Consecration · 25 [Swift Judgement](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310994) (16 points) · 30 [Templar's Bulwark](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1311015) · 35 Iron Creed · 40 Holy Shield and Hammer of the Righteous (above 30 is projection).
- **Gear and consumables:** at 30 Wowhead picks Painwalker Buckler and Scorn's Icy Choker (Scarlet Monastery), Worgenbane Talisman and Blacksmithing's Crusader's chain set. No spell power weapons before 20 (about 25% less melee damage). Engineering for ranged pulls; Icy Veins adds Lesser Arcane Elixir.
- **Watch out:** ForeverDB's PvE, builds and levelling pages still give Righteous Fury 60% threat; it is 90%. Shield Specialization is 4% Mana per block, not 6% ([Blizzard](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6)). Templar's Bulwark is an absorb, not immunity. Check Iron Creed's tooltip at launch.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Choose **survival talents when dangerous pulls are the constraint**, or more threat when the group is safe but enemies escape. Keep Righteous Fury and the appropriate Seal active, use controlled pulls, and conserve mana for the next pack. Saving Judgement for an imminent loose enemy can be worth more than pressing it on cooldown for damage ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/level-30-tank-overview)). Templar's Bulwark is a temporary absorb, not immunity; large or badly positioned pulls remain dangerous. The [later official notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6) settle Righteous Fury at 90% added Holy threat and Shield Specialization at 4% mana per block with a 3-second internal cooldown.

Iron Creed (35), Holy Shield and Hammer of the Righteous (40) were out of reach in the beta; their value is projection.

### What's different in Forever

From Blizzard's [deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099):

- **Seal of Fury** adds the same Holy damage to every swing, whatever the weapon speed, and with a shield turns half of it into an absorb. Its Judgement deals Holy damage and taunts for 4 sec.
- **Swift Judgement** (16 points) finishes Judgement's cooldown for a second taunt.
- **Templar's Bulwark** (21 points) absorbs damage equal to your maximum health for 8 sec, on a 5 min cooldown. It shares Forbearance with Divine Protection.
- **Shield Specialization** blocks restore Mana, and **Improved Seal of Fury** returns Mana when an absorb is used up (more against higher-level enemies).
- **Improved Righteous Fury** now cuts damage taken by 6% instead of adding threat. **Reckoning** can also trigger from blocks.
- **Consecration** is baseline at 20 and only hits four enemies hard. Holy Shield (40) now requires Templar's Bulwark.

The 15 October build made Seal of Fury survive Power Word: Shield. It also put Righteous Fury back to 90% Holy threat ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/paladin-deep-dive-seals-judgements-holy-strike-protection-retribution)).

### Threat and priorities

- **ForeverDB (30):** Righteous Fury before the pull, then Seal of Fury, Judgement, Holy Strike, and Exorcism on Undead and Demons. Use Retribution Aura on multi-enemy pulls and Devotion Aura on single enemies. Consecration only on real packs, as it is expensive.
- **Wowhead (30):** one target: Seal of Fury, Judgement, Exorcism, Holy Strike, then Consecration if Mana allows. On packs Consecration moves to the front.
- **Icy Veins:** Seal of Fury, Consecration as long as Mana allows, Judgement, Holy Strike, Exorcism. While learning, hold Judgement back for taunting enemies you have lost.

The [WoWSims APL](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/paladin/protection/apls/default.apl.json), built for level 60, prepares Righteous Fury, Retribution Aura and Seal of the Crusader before the pull. It then runs:

1. Judgement
2. Swift Judgement
3. Seal of Fury, if missing
4. Consecration
5. Holy Shock, if talented
6. Holy Shield
7. Holy Strike
8. Exorcism

Opening with a Crusader Judgement before switching to Seal of Fury also appears in Wowhead's advice for Undead and Demons.

### Levelling

Wowhead says the road to 16 is rough, and a two-hander is better until Righteous Fury and Retribution Aura arrive. After 20, sword and board handles the open world better than Retribution. Templar's Bulwark at 30 adds a strong temporary absorb, but its size, duration and cooldown do not make dungeon pulls risk-free. ForeverDB's levelling build (Redoubt, Precision, Benediction, then Shield Specialization and Swift Judgement) rarely needs to drink and can tank as it is. Icy Veins starts in Retribution for Pursuit of Justice and cheaper spells. Wowhead notes a beta bug where Consecration sometimes dealt less than full damage at its edge; moving enemies fixed it.

### Gear and consumables

Wowhead's level 30 picks: Painwalker Buckler and Scorn's Icy Choker (Scarlet Monastery), Worgenbane Talisman (Shadowfang Keep), Blacksmithing's Crusader's chain set, and the Enchanting libram Tenets of the Silver Hand. ForeverDB adds Forever's Protector's Silvered Chain Shirt and Leggings and Warder's Belt. Wowhead warns against spell power weapons before 20, as they carry about a 25% melee damage penalty. Icy Veins recommends Lesser Arcane Elixir. WoWSims' level 60 defaults use Flask of Supreme Power, Elixir of Holy Power, Greater Stoneshield Potion and Brilliant Wizard Oil. Icy Veins prefers Dwarf for Stoneform; WoWSims assumes Human.

### Where guides disagree

- **Righteous Fury's threat:** current client data and Blizzard's 15 October notes give 90%. ForeverDB's PvE, builds and levelling pages, checked against an older build, still say 60%. Wowhead says 90%.
- **Judgement:** ForeverDB and WoWSims press it on cooldown; Icy Veins suggests saving it for taunts at first.
- **Damage or survival in row 3–4:** Wowhead takes One-Handed Weapon Specialization for threat. ForeverDB now prefers Improved Righteous Fury, arguing Righteous Fury already covers threat.
- **Talent numbers:** the [later official notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/6) confirm Shield Specialization at 4% mana per block with a 3-second internal cooldown, superseding 6%. Iron Creed still needs a current tooltip check before using either the older 15% or client-reported 25% figure in a threat calculation.
