## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Keeping a focused target alive while bringing blessings, cleanses and emergency saves.
- **Levels 40–59:** Build around Holy Shock and add Light’s Vigil at 31 Holy points. Heal through a few dungeon pulls before using the same talents for solo levelling. A second Retribution build is worth considering if you spend most of the week outdoors. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/paladin-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 32/7/12 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** In a Horde group, play close enough to heal but far enough to make opponents come to you. Keep Blessing of Freedom and Cleanse available for the ally being focused, and save a defensive cooldown for interrupts and stuns. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

32/7/12 is a practical healing starting point. The source versions choose between Guardian’s Favor and Sacred Duty. Take the former if you value more frequent utility and compare the latter for durability. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/paladin/holy/rotation) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use efficient direct heals on predictable incoming damage, Holy Shock when you need to move and Light’s Vigil for appropriate group damage. Maintain your blessing assignment and leave mana for the next pull. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/paladin/holy/rotation) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Build a healing and mana set alongside your solo gear. Compare efficient heals per pull before prioritising bigger short bursts. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/paladin/holy/rotation)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
