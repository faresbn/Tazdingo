## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Retribution for solo progress, Protection for regular tanking and Holy for a steady healing group.
- **Levels 40–59:** Around 40, decide whether most of your time will be spent using a two-hander, tanking with a shield or healing. Use the secondary allocation to cover the role you play second most. All three should keep key blessings, Cleanse and emergency support accessible. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/paladin-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** On Horde PvP, keep a way to free a partner or yourself from movement control. Use line of sight while healing and choose a safe place before committing to a long pull. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

Judgement keeps the Seal active. The 13 November fix gives Judgement of Command melee attack treatment for on-hit effects. Other Judgements retain ranged attack treatment. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Holy](ledger:classes/paladin/holy/) | 32/7/12 | Keeping a focused target alive while bringing blessings, cleanses and emergency saves. |
| [Protection](ledger:classes/paladin/protection/) | 0/35/16 | Shield tanking, controlling melee packs and helping a group recover from loose enemies. |
| [Retribution](ledger:classes/paladin/retribution/) | 12/8/31 | Two-handed solo play, burst and support for a levelling partner. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

On Horde PvP, keep a way to free a partner or yourself from movement control. Use line of sight while healing and choose a safe place before committing to a long pull. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
