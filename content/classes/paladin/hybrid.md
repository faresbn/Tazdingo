## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/paladin-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** On Horde PvP, keep a way to free a partner or yourself from movement control. Use line of sight while healing and choose a safe place before committing to a long pull.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Holy](ledger:classes/paladin/holy/) | 32/7/12 | 32/7/12 is a practical healing starting point. The source versions choose between Guardian’s Favor and Sacred Duty. Take the former if you value more frequent utility and compare the latter for durability. |
| [Protection](ledger:classes/paladin/protection/) | 0/35/16 | 0/35/16 is a dedicated tank starting point. The guide comparison lists a different dungeon option; choose extra damage or sustain only once missed casts and loose enemies are under control. |
| [Retribution](ledger:classes/paladin/retribution/) | 12/8/31 | 12/8/31 is a level-60 group starting point for Unsealed Wrath and paladin utility. Keep pursuit or defensive talents if your regular play includes more contested travel than stationary bosses. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-paladin-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
