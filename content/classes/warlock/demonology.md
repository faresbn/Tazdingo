## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Pet-led solo levelling with Soul Link and a choice between Fire and Shadow damage.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 44 you can have Demonic Pact and four points outside Demonology. Use Soul Link and practise Searing Pain into Demonic Brand with your pet already attacking. A Felhunter sacrifice can suit long solo sessions if you keep a different demon active. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warlock-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 2/31/18 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep Soul Link available and choose your demon for the opponent. Use a Felhunter against dangerous casts, a Voidwalker for a defensive shield, or a Sayaad for a control option. Keep the demon alive instead of spending its utility on routine mobs. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

2/31/18 is a framework with different pet and Destruction talents for Fire and Shadow. Sacrifice a Sayaad and summon an Imp for Fire, or sacrifice an Imp and summon a Sayaad for Shadow. Pact preserves the buff only with a different demon. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/demonology-warlock-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Searing Pain and an Imp for Fire, or Shadow Bolt and a Sayaad for Shadow, with Searing Pain to renew Demonic Brand. Crix uses Decimation Soul Fire below 35% in Shadow; Warcraft Tavern keeps Shadow Bolt. Compare under your own buffs and gear. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/demonology-warlock-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)

### Pet swaps and November fixes

The [live Demonology guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warlock/demonology/overview-pve-dps) distinguishes a fresh summon of the sacrificed demon from swapping to a different demon. The [20 November fix](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) removes the Demonic Aegis armour snapshot when you unlearn it. If you switch builds at 40–60, equip the buff and pet setup for your new talents.

### Gear and practical checks

Use the same gear and demon for each trial before comparing Fire and Shadow. When tanking with a Voidwalker, measure time to kill and time to recover together. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/demonology-warlock-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
