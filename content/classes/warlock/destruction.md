## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Direct-cast burst and cleave when you can control positioning and mana.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 44 you can already have Incinerate and Shadow and Flame. Build around Immolate and Incinerate, and use instant finishers when a cast would land too late. If several short kills make you stop to recover after each pull, Affliction or Demonology is the better solo comparison. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warlock-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 5/11/35 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep a demon for contested questing. Use Shadowburn or Conflagrate to apply pressure while moving, and save Fear, an interrupt from a Felhunter, or a defensive shield for surviving the opponent’s response. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

5/11/35 is the Fire sacrifice build with Suppression. 7/11/33 keeps a sacrifice and adds faster Corruption and Life Tap talents. Warcraft Tavern and Method also describe pet-based approaches when you want utility or independent damage. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/destruction-warlock-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Maintain Immolate where you intend to use Incinerate. Use Conflagrate and Shadowburn according to talents and shard supply. Use Bane of Havoc on a second durable target and allow for its global cooldown. Use Rain of Fire on safe, grouped pulls. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/destruction-warlock-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/)

### Two live changes to build around

[Blizzard’s hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) restored Incinerate to Destruction and fixed Intensity’s interaction with it on 23 November. Havoc uses a global cooldown, so start it early enough to repay an action. [Method’s rotation](https://www.method.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/destruction-warlock/rotation-and-playstyle) describes a Sayaad setup; that setup and a Demonic Sacrifice setup solve different group needs.

### Gear and practical checks

Compare Fire damage on your Immolate and Incinerate setup and account for Shadow casts separately. Check if your friend needs Blood Pact or a pet interrupt before choosing a sacrifice build. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/destruction-warlock-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
