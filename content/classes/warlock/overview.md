## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** Cloth. Staves, daggers, one-handed swords and wands, according to the Forever client ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-warlock)).
- **Role:** Ranged damage only. Blizzard counts the Warlock as a pure damage class.
- **Resources:** Mana, which [Life Tap](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1454) tops up from your health (it now scales with Spirit), and [Soul Shards](https://foreverdb.net/item/6265) from [Drain Soul](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1120).
- **Races:** Human and Gnome for the Alliance; Orc, Undead and Troll for the Horde. Troll Warlocks are new.
- **Demons:** Imp from level 1, Voidwalker at 10, Succubus or Incubus at 20, Felhunter at 30. Blizzard says pets now scale with your stats.
- **New in Forever:** Curse of Agony and Curse of Doom are now Banes, so a Bane and a Curse can share a target. New capstones are [Wrack](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1316697), [Demonic Pact](https://foreverdb.net/spell/425464) and [Incinerate](https://foreverdb.net/spell/412758), and [Bane of Havoc](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1225228) arrives at 30. Firestones and Spellstones are now weapon imbues.
- **Levelling pick:** ForeverDB and Icy Veins favour Affliction, with Demonology as the tough option. Wowhead calls Destruction the slowest solo.
- **Utility:** Soulstone, Healthstone, Ritual of Summoning, Curse of the Elements and Curse of Recklessness, Banish and Fear.

## What the guides say

### How Forever changed the Warlock

From Blizzard's [Rogue and Warlock deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.com/en-us/news/24310968) and the beta client:

- **Banes and Curses.** [Bane of Agony](https://foreverdb.net/spell/980) and Bane of Doom are no longer Curses. Each Warlock can keep one Bane and one Curse on a target, so your damage over time no longer competes with the raid's [Curse of the Elements](https://foreverdb.net/spell/440892), which now covers every magic school. Curse of Shadows is gone.
- **Stones.** Firestone (Fire damage and crit) and Spellstone (Shadow damage and spell haste) become weapon imbues that stack with Wizard Oil.
- **Life Tap and Drain Soul.** Life Tap scales with Spirit. Drain Soul has a rising chance to give a Soul Shard on each tick and always gives one when a mob dies during the channel.
- **Pets.** Demons scale from your stats. Stay is replaced by "Move To", which sends your demon to a spot up to 100 yards away (Icy Veins, Wowhead). Wowhead says demons take 50% less damage from boss AoE.
- **Crits.** Damage over time spells can now crit (Wowhead, Icy Veins), and talents such as Pandemic and Malevolence build on it.
- **Spell power.** Shadow Bolt rank 1 takes 0.486 of your spell power in the client, up from 0.14 in Classic, and Corruption and Immolate gained similarly ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-warlock)).
- **Talent tree.** Soul Link drops to the 21-point slot, Conflagrate to the 16-point slot, and Ruin becomes a 5-point row 3 talent that Blizzard says is meant to be easier for Demonology and hybrid builds to reach. Late changes on 16 October renamed Malediction to Grim Reach, now a range talent ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70425-game-files)).

### Soul Shards

[Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/warlock-class-overview) says Soul Shards now count as reagents, with a dedicated reagent bag slot for your shard bag. They still don't stack. Its guides list them for summoning demons (the Imp costs only mana), Healthstones, Soulstones, Ritual of Summoning and Shadowburn. [Shadowburn](https://foreverdb.net/spell/17877) refunds its shard if the target dies within 8 seconds, up from 5 in Classic. Decimation lets Soul Fire skip the shard for 10 seconds on low-health targets.

ForeverDB adds a caution: the client's tooltips don't list reagents, so which spells consume shards isn't confirmed from the files. It suggests keeping a few spare by finishing mobs with Drain Soul now and then. The WoWSims presets assume 32 shards, a full Felcloth Bag.

### Demons

| Demon | From | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| Imp | Level 1 | Ranged Firebolt, Blood Pact for party Stamina and Fire Shield on the tank. Fragile and short of mana; the usual group pet |
| Voidwalker | Level 10 | The tank pet: Torment, a Sacrifice shield, Consume Shadows to heal. Most levelling builds use it; Icy Veins says its threat can rival a real tank |
| Succubus or Incubus | Level 20 | Melee damage with Lash of Pain, and Seduction from 26. Icy Veins measured it at roughly a fifth to a quarter of total damage in the beta |
| Felhunter | Level 30 | Devour Magic at 30 and Spell Lock at 36, bought as a Grimoire. The anti-caster pet |

Demon abilities come from Grimoires sold by the Demon Trainer, which Icy Veins says makes Warlocks spend more gold than most classes. Summons take 10 seconds in the client, so summon between fights (ForeverDB). [Demonic Sacrifice](https://foreverdb.net/spell/18788) now lasts 2 hours: Imp gives 15% Shadow damage, Voidwalker restores 2% mana every 4 seconds, Succubus or Incubus gives 15% Fire damage and Felhunter restores 3% health every 4 seconds. Demonic Pact keeps that buff while a different demon is out.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | Playstyle | Levelling verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Affliction | DoTs and drains, little downtime | ForeverDB's first pick; Icy Veins calls it a top-tier leveller; Wowhead pulls two mobs at once and Fears one |
| Demonology | Your demon does much of the damage; Soul Link from 30 | ForeverDB suggests it if you die a lot; Wowhead says it plays like a weaker Affliction until Demonic Brand at 25 |
| Destruction | Direct hits and burst, Incinerate at 40 | ForeverDB says it has the most personal damage at 20; Wowhead calls it the slowest solo but strong in dungeons |

Shared levelling advice: keep the Voidwalker out from 10 for solo play and bring the Imp to dungeons ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-warlock)). Life Tap for mana, Drain Life for health, and expect to eat more than you drink. Use Curse of Weakness solo against melee mobs and Curse of the Elements in groups. Tailoring and Enchanting are the usual professions, with First Aid for bandages after a Life Tap (Icy Veins, ForeverDB).

### What the simulators assume at 60

WoWSims Forever ships five Warlock builds at 60: two Affliction (Shadow Bolt with Ruin, or Wrack), two Demonology (Fire or Shadow) and one Destruction. All of them mix trees, and all default to Curse of the Elements ([presets](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/warlock/dps/presets.ts)). Read them as projections: the beta stopped at 30.

### Where the guides disagree

- **Spirit:** Icy Veins calls it important now that Life Tap scales with it; Wowhead's Affliction overview says it does little beyond a slightly bigger Life Tap.
- **Felsteed:** Wowhead lists it as a trainer spell at 40; Icy Veins calls it a class quest at 40.
- **Racials:** Icy Veins' Warlock pages say Eureka! cuts mana costs by 50%, while Wowhead and ForeverDB say 10%. Icy Veins gives Touch of the Grave a 10% chance; Wowhead gives 30% with a 25 second cooldown. Shatter Curse's magic damage reduction is 15% in Icy Veins and 5% in Wowhead's tooltip.
- **Pet scaling:** Blizzard and Icy Veins say demons scale with your stats; ForeverDB notes the client doesn't show how much of your gear carries over, and the character sheet shows demon stats wrongly for now.
