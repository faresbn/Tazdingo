## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Affliction for low-downtime questing, Demonology for pet-led safety, Destruction for direct burst and planned cleave.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 44 normal progression gives 35 talent points. A deep build can already have Wrack, Demonic Pact or Incinerate. Compare how long targets live, how much channel time you get and how often you stop to recover before deciding to respec. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/warlock-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Keep a demon and a few shards when questing in contested zones. Choose Spell Lock, a Sacrifice shield or Seduction according to the likely threat. An imp-sacrifice raid setup is a different job from surviving a player who joins your pull. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

The 10 November hotfix reworked Wrack and Soul Siphon and buffed demon health. Bane of Havoc now uses a global cooldown. On 23 November Blizzard fixed Intensity for Incinerate. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-warlock-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Group practice in the 40s

At 44, Krol’dok Stronghold or an early Zul’Farrak group can help you compare immediate damage with longer damage-over-time setups. Watch which targets survive your first few casts, and note whether the group benefits more from a pet interrupt or an imp’s Stamina. [Dungeon list](https://tierhall.app/wow/dungeons)

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

Keep a demon and a few shards when questing in contested zones. Choose Spell Lock, a Sacrifice shield or Seduction according to the likely threat. An imp-sacrifice raid setup is a different job from surviving a player who joins your pull. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
