## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Ranged Lightning Bolt damage, Purge and burst when a cast window opens.
- **Levels 40–59:** Build around Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning and shocks. Use Call of Thunder and work towards Lightning Mastery. Press Chain Lightning for real cleave with enough mana to get through the next pull. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/shaman-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 35/5/11 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use Purge and Earth Shock to take away an advantage or stop a cast. Use Grounding Totem or line of sight to make a cast window. Lightning Mastery burst can wait until the enemy has used a defensive. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

35/5/11 adds range and threat support to the lightning core. The 31/9/11 alternative trades some of it for Intellect and less expensive totems. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/elemental-shaman-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Lightning Bolt on ordinary single targets and Chain Lightning when the extra targets justify the mana. Fit shocks around movement, range and the job you need done. Assign totems to the party composition. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/elemental-shaman-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Lightning changes at launch

[Blizzard’s final tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/) replaced Lava Burst with Call of Thunder and renamed Elemental Mastery to Lightning Mastery. The [live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) also corrected Lightning Overload damage. Use those notes when comparing cast sequences from a launch-week guide.

### Gear and practical checks

Compare a spell-damage and mana package on repeat pulls and bring a shield where safe casts matter. Watch how often Chain Lightning saves a cast or drains the mana you need next. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/elemental-shaman-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
