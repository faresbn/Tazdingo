## Cheatsheet

- **Pick a spec:** Enhancement for weapon-led levelling, Elemental for lightning damage and Purge, Restoration for group healing.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40 choose based on how you fight most often. Enhancement needs a current weapon, Elemental needs enough mana and room to cast, and Restoration gets more from levelling with a group. Keep totems relevant to the pull and group. [Current levelling guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/shaman-leveling-guide)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Purge, Grounding Totem and an interrupt change what you can do against a player. Use Earthbind or Frost Shock to help a companion disengage, and leave time to move between casts. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)
- **Level-60 dungeons:** Check the build and rotation for your role below, then adjust to the pulls you actually run.

### Current class changes

Elemental’s current build uses Call of Thunder in place of Lava Burst and Lightning Mastery. November tuning also reworked Enhancement around Windfury and Maelstrom. [Blizzard’s live hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) · [Final class tuning notes](https://www.bluetracker.gg/wow/topic/us-en/2360696-wow-forever-beta-development-notes-updated-november-2/)

### Level-60 starting points

| Spec | Example PvE split | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Elemental](ledger:classes/shaman/elemental/) | 35/5/11 | Ranged Lightning Bolt damage, Purge and burst when a cast window opens. |
| [Enhancement](ledger:classes/shaman/enhancement/) | 12/35/4 | Weapon-led solo damage, quick support and melee pressure in a levelling pair. |
| [Restoration](ledger:classes/shaman/restoration/) | 0/10/41 | Healing clustered allies, removing harmful effects and supporting a dungeon party with totems. |

Choose a full allocation from the [level-60 guide comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/) or the spec page. The [first three raids](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) are scheduled for 9 December. Dungeon play can tell you about damage, mana and utility in the meantime.

### Horde PvP and secondary talents

Purge, Grounding Totem and an interrupt change what you can do against a player. Use Earthbind or Frost Shock to help a companion disengage, and leave time to move between casts. The secondary talent allocation unlocks at level 40. Pair your regular dungeon role with a questing or PvP setup if the two jobs want different tools. [Secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/) · [Horde PvP rules](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## Field findings

### Shaman client build 1.60.1.70790 revised spells and totems (testing, 5 Nov 2026)

Read in [ForeverDB's 4 November client comparison](https://foreverdb.net/changelog/1.60.1.70790) and its [source-based explanation](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70790-game-files). Lightning Bolt, shocks and Chain Lightning list increased damage, while Chain Lightning lists a 2.5-second cast instead of 2 seconds; Stormstrike is listed at 150% weapon damage with a 6-second cooldown, and Flurry reaches 30%. The old Totemic Projection was removed and Totemic Call is listed at level 22. Earlier talent and key-level advice on this page should be checked before use. These are changes in client-file values, not measured release-server performance.
