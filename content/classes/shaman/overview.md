## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** cloth, leather and shields, with mail from level 40. Weapons: one-handed maces and axes, daggers, fist weapons and staves. Blizzard says two-handed axes and maces are now baseline. Shamans can't dual wield in Forever.
- **Roles:** ranged damage (Elemental), melee damage (Enhancement) or healer (Restoration). Blizzard says Enhancement can briefly tank in small groups.
- **Resource:** Mana, for spells, shocks and totems.
- **Both factions:** Shamans are open to the Alliance for the first time, but only as Dwarves. The Horde has Orc, Tauren, Troll and Windshaper Skyborne.
- **Totems:** one per element at a time. Most buff totems now last 5 min and reach 30 yards.
- **What's new:** [Call of the Elements](https://foreverdb.net/spell/66842) drops four totems in one cast, [Totemic Recall](https://foreverdb.net/spell/36936) refunds mana and [Fire Nova](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408341) is now a spell. [Lava Burst](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408490), [Riptide](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408521), [Water Shield](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408510) and [Maelstrom Weapon](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408498) are talents.
- **Levelling:** Enhancement is the usual recommendation.
- **Hybrid status:** Elemental and Enhancement fall under Blizzard's target of about 5% less damage than pure classes (see the hybrid page).

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

For mostly solo questing, start with **Enhancement**, keep the weapon current and spend mana selectively. Choose **Elemental** for the caster playstyle or a regular group that can support its mana use; choose **Restoration** when healing a regular party is the job. A damage build can carry a healing set, but that is not a promise that every dungeon pull is safe without healing talents. These are level-30 Beta recommendations, not measured level-60 rankings ([Enhancement guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/enhancement/level-30-dps-overview), [Restoration guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/restoration/level-30-healer-overview)).

The beta stopped at level 30. Chain Lightning (32), Windfury Totem (32), mail (40) and Chain Heal (40) all come later and are untested.

### Both factions

Three sources confirm that both factions can play Shaman. Blizzard's [race and class article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24304075) lists Dwarf Shaman among the new combinations, and says the Horde-aligned Windshaper Skyborne can be Shaman. [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-shaman) lists five Shaman races and notes Dwarves are the only Alliance option. The Alliance's High Order Skyborne can't be Shamans. Wowhead's spec guides also call Dwarf the only Alliance choice.

Dwarves start in Coldridge Valley ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/dwarf-shaman)). Totem spells need the matching totem item in your bags, earned from the Earth, Fire, Water and Air class quests at 4, 10, 20 and 30. ForeverDB couldn't confirm from the client how Dwarves and Skyborne get these quests. Wowhead's level 30 guides list Dwarf and Skyborne versions of Call of Earth, and Alliance versions of the other three.

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [Mage and Shaman deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302097) aims to cut repeated setup in combat:

- **Totems:** a 1 sec global cooldown, 5 min durations for buff totems and a tracking bar where a right-click dismisses a totem. Resistance totems cover the whole raid.
- **Weapon imbues:** they last 60 min and can sit alongside sharpening stones and wizard oils.
- **Fire Nova:** a spell that erupts from your active Fire totem. The old Fire Nova Totem is gone.
- **Windfury and Flametongue Totems:** now plain group buffs. You don't benefit from one if your own weapon carries the matching imbue. Blizzard's 16 October notes made Windfury, Flametongue, Strength of Earth and Grace of Air Totems raid-wide. They also let Grace of Air run alongside the other two, though Windfury and Flametongue still exclude each other ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/beta-development-notes-october-16)).
- **New totem tools:** [Totemic Projection](https://foreverdb.net/spell/437009) at 22 moves your totems. Call spells hold preset four-totem sets. Totemic Recall returns 25% of their mana.
- **Baseline talents:** Two-Handed Axes and Maces, Enhancing Totems, Weapon Mastery and Improved Weapon Totems are no longer talents. Blizzard says every Shaman now has them.
- **Spell scaling:** Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning cast faster but start lower, gaining more from spell power. [Lightning Bolt](https://foreverdb.net/spell/403) rank 1 went from 0.123 to 0.429 ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/outlook-shaman)).

The 16 October build then reshuffled the Elemental tree ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/build-1-60-1-70425-game-files)). Lava Burst moved up to tier 5, reachable at level 30. A redesigned [Elemental Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16166) returned as the tier 7 talent, even though Blizzard's deep dive had said it was removed. Chain Lightning and Fire Nova also became cheaper. One more mismatch: the deep dive puts Call of the Spirits at 30, but the client teaches [Call of the Ancestors](https://foreverdb.net/spell/66843) at 30 and [Call of the Spirits](https://foreverdb.net/spell/66844) at 40.

### Choosing a spec

- **Elemental:** a ranged caster with strong area damage from Fire Nova and Magma Totem. Wowhead finds its single-target damage weak at low levels, and Icy Veins sums up its level 30 damage as weak.
- **Enhancement:** melee with a slow two-hander, Windfury Weapon and Stormstrike. Wowhead calls it easy to learn, and says every melee group will want a Shaman for Windfury Totem. Icy Veins warns that mana runs out fast.
- **Restoration:** Healing Wave, then Chain Heal and Riptide at 40. Its healing talents suit a regular group, at the cost of slower solo kills.

Blizzard's own summary calls Shamans among the sturdiest classes, but short on reliable crowd control.

### Levelling

ForeverDB and Wowhead both point new Shamans to Enhancement, and Icy Veins rates Elemental's damage weak at 30. As Enhancement you fight with a big weapon, heal between pulls, and from level 20 [Improved Ghost Wolf](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16262) makes Ghost Wolf instant and usable indoors. Wowhead's Restoration guide says to level as Enhancement and keep Restoration for Dual Specialization, which unlocks at 40. Wowhead's Elemental guide goes further, calling Elemental a poor choice below 40 and suggesting a melee-caster hybrid instead.

The level 30 Call of Air chain rewards a choice of weapon: Lightning's Grasp, Rage of the Storm or Tidebringer's Claw, plus Elementalist's Guard ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/shaman/elemental/level-30-dps-overview)). Icy Veins recommends Lightning's Grasp with the shield for Elemental, and Tidebringer's Claw for Restoration.

### Race and profession picks

- **Race:** older guides favour Orc or Skyborne, but their racial comparisons predate the 26 October changes below. The Alliance has only Dwarf.
- **Professions:** ForeverDB suggests Leatherworking and Skinning. Wowhead's Elemental and Restoration guides and WoWSims suggest Leatherworking and Enchanting; Enchanting makes the [Polished Driftwood Icon](https://foreverdb.net/item/249398) relic.

### Where the guides disagree

**The old racial disagreement is resolved:** Blizzard's [26 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7) set Skyborne Wind-Blessed to **2% haste**. Orc Savage was removed and Command restored; its pet-damage bonus does not benefit a Shaman. Tauren Endurance is now only 5% health. Earlier Orc-for-every-spec rankings and 1%/3% Skyborne figures are not a current comparison. Choose race for its actual toolkit and preferences; there is no launch-tested universal winner here.
