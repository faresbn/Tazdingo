## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Weapon-led solo damage, quick support and melee pressure in a levelling pair.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40, build around the current Stormstrike and Windfury interaction. Look for a weapon upgrade over minor stat reshuffles. Fit Maelstrom casts to the fight and use Ghost Wolf to shorten time travelling. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/shaman-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 12/35/4 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use Frost Shock or Earthbind to make space and Purge before committing to burst. Save a Maelstrom heal or Grounding Totem if a second enemy can join the fight. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

12/35/4 is Warcraft Tavern’s PvE alternative to the popular 8/39/4. The former puts more points into Elemental damage and Clearcasting, the latter into Enhancement. [Current spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/shaman/enhancement/talents) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Use Stormstrike as the melee anchor, fit shocks and totems to the pull and spend useful Maelstrom procs on damage or a heal. Judge your build on the live Windfury and Maelstrom behaviour. [Spec guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/shaman/enhancement/talents) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Tuning that changes gear and priority

[Blizzard’s November hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-23-2026) changed Stormstrike to 100% weapon damage on an 8-second cooldown and revised Maelstrom Weapon. The notes explain the renewed emphasis on Windfury for gearing and fix procs and extra attacks across later days. Treat an older build’s spell-power emphasis as a different tuning context.

### Gear and practical checks

Upgrade the melee weapon that powers your Stormstrike and Windfury tests. Keep a shield option when casting through incoming pressure matters. [Spec and gearing guide](https://wowforeverclasses.com/classes/shaman/enhancement/talents)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
