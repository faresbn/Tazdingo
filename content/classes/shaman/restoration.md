## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** Healing clustered allies, removing harmful effects and supporting a dungeon party with totems.
- **Levels 40–59:** At 40, add your newly available Chain Heal and talented Riptide. Use efficient Healing Wave casts on steady tank damage and instant support when you have to move. Use a second damage build if questing as a healer is slow. [Level-by-level class guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/shaman-leveling-guide)
- **At level 60:** 0/10/41 as a starting point. [Full talent allocation](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Use Grounding Totem and terrain to protect a heal. Use shocks and Purge for critical utility when it is safe to spend the action, and reposition before an opponent can lock you down. [Ruleset details](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302070/choose-your-ruleset-in-world-of-warcraft-forever)

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

0/10/41 is a common level-60 Chain Heal and Riptide build. A deeper Restoration allocation can trade utility or critical chance for different totem and healing priorities. [Current spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/restoration-shaman-guide) · [Level-60 alternatives](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Dungeon priority

Plan Chain Heal around allies close enough to benefit, use Riptide to stabilise a target, and time Mana Tide for a fight long enough that the group can use it. Keep Water Shield up if talented. [Spec guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/restoration-shaman-guide) · [Level-60 build comparison](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)

### Gear and practical checks

Prioritise a healing set that leaves enough mana for consecutive pulls. Check whether healing throughput or spell timing is what made the last pull difficult. [Spec and gearing guide](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/restoration-shaman-guide)

### What level-60 evidence covers

Use this build for open level-60 content or to prepare for a group role. Rankings for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair need evidence from those raids. Blizzard announced 9 December for their opening. [Launch and raid schedule](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live)
