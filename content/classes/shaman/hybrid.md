## Cheatsheet

- **Levelling:** Pick the defining talent for your main role. [Levelling route](https://mobalytics.gg/wow-forever/classes/shaman-leveling-guide)
- **Level 60:** Use the build comparison below to choose your cross-tree points. [Full point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/)
- **Horde PvP ruleset:** Purge, Grounding Totem and an interrupt change what you can do against a player. Use Earthbind or Frost Shock to help a companion disengage, and leave time to move between casts.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Reach the talent you want for your main role, then add points from another tree for a job you actually do. Secondary talents at 40 let you keep a dedicated dungeon or outdoor build. [Level 40 secondary talents](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-talents-explained/)

### Level-60 builds that cross trees

| Spec | Example PvE split | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| [Elemental](ledger:classes/shaman/elemental/) | 35/5/11 | 35/5/11 adds range and threat support to the lightning core. The 31/9/11 alternative trades some of it for Intellect and less expensive totems. |
| [Enhancement](ledger:classes/shaman/enhancement/) | 12/35/4 | 12/35/4 is Warcraft Tavern’s PvE alternative to the popular 8/39/4. The former puts more points into Elemental damage and Clearcasting, the latter into Enhancement. |
| [Restoration](ledger:classes/shaman/restoration/) | 0/10/41 | 0/10/41 is a common level-60 Chain Heal and Riptide build. A deeper Restoration allocation can trade utility or critical chance for different totem and healing priorities. |

See the [complete point allocations](https://www.wofwforever.com/en/guides/wow-forever-shaman-level-60-talents/) before buying a respec. Point totals can hide differences between allocations with the same split. Use the current spec page for contested-zone priorities and [Blizzard’s raid calendar](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-now-live) for the content these builds are preparing for.
