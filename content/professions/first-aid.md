## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** every character, most of all those who can't heal themselves ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/first-aid/overview)); it's secondary, and in Forever it makes the healing potions.
- **Pairs with:** nothing it strictly needs; cloth drops for everyone. The potions use herbs, so Herbalism, or a friend who has it, helps.
- **Levelling route:** guides split: 1–75 Linen Bandages (Wowhead) or Minor Healing Potions ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/first-aid)) · 75–150 Wool Bandages or Lesser and Healing Potions · 150–225 Silk to Mageweave Bandages · 225–300 Mageweave to Heavy Runecloth (Icy Veins; untested in the beta). Wowhead finds bandages usually cheapest.
- **Money makers:** anyone can drink the healing potions, so they suit Auction House buyers while levelling (Icy Veins); some new Tailoring recipes use potions (Wowhead). Anti-venoms are the only in-combat poison cure. No prices are established; value the materials, the heal isn't free.
- **Camp items:** [First Aid Kit](https://foreverdb.net/item/279968) (20; Stamina, doesn't stack with Power Word: Fortitude) · [Toxin Study](https://foreverdb.net/item/279940) (140; also stocks healing potions and anti-venom) · [Plague Doctor's Laboratory](https://foreverdb.net/item/279951) (300; healing potions and poultices).
- **Trainers and ranks:** Expert from the tradeable book Under Wraps (Deneb Walker, Stromgarde; Balai Lok'Wein, Brackenwall) · Artisan from the quest Triage (level 35, skill 225; Theramore or Hammerfall). Major Healing Potion manual: Pexmit in Hyjal (Icy Veins); [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/first-aid-leveling-guide) says no vendor does.
- **Watch out:** hand in Triage the moment you finish, before patients die. Poultices and tourniquets work out of combat only, up to a set effect level. Mageweave was scarce in the beta. Don't buy a costly manual until its source and use are confirmed.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

**Learn First Aid alongside either pair of primary professions**, especially on characters with weak self-healing. For levelling it, compare the total cost of a bandage route with the herb-and-vial potion route; keep useful output for personal use, but value the materials rather than calling the heal free. The [guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/first-aid-leveling-guide) allows either early route and warns that yellow crafts give uncertain skill gains. Bandages require a safe channel and cures have effect-level limits. Do not buy an expensive high-skill manual until its source, required rank and use are confirmed.

### What changed in Forever

**Healing potions moved here.** Every source agrees that the plain healing potions are now First Aid recipes:

| Potion | Skill to learn | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Minor, Lesser and Healing Potion | 1, 55 and 110 | First Aid trainers |
| Greater Healing Potion | 155 | A manual, which [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/first-aid) says drops from Syndicate Mercenaries in Arathi |
| Superior Healing Potion | 215 | A manual |
| Major Healing Potion | 275 | A manual. Icy Veins says Pexmit in Mount Hyjal sells it; [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/first-aid-leveling-guide) says no vendor does |

Anyone can drink them. They share a 2-minute cooldown, and the higher ones need a character level (Icy Veins). Alchemy keeps only its "Discolored" healing potions, which heal more but hurt you afterwards ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions)).

**Cures for poison, disease and bleeds** ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/first-aid/overview), Icy Veins):

- **Anti-venoms (poisons):** the only cure you can use in combat, which makes them valuable where poisons are common. A new Potent Anti-Venom joins the line at 215.
- **Poultices (diseases):** Simple, Clever, Superior and Powerful, from skill 90.
- **Tourniquets (bleeds):** Woolen, Leather and Surgical, from skill 120.

Poultices and tourniquets work only out of combat. Each cure removes effects up to a set level: the [Simple Poultice](https://foreverdb.net/item/255716) handles diseases up to level 25 and the [Woolen Tourniquet](https://foreverdb.net/item/255720) bleeds up to level 30 (ForeverDB). All the cures share a 1-minute cooldown.

**Bandages work as before.** You channel them on yourself or an ally, and the target then can't take another for a minute. Crafting and using can need different skill: Heavy Runecloth Bandage takes 290 to make but 225 to use (Icy Veins). The Field Medicine Legacy perk shortens the Recently Bandaged debuff outside dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.

### Levelling, in brief

This is where the guides disagree most: bandages or potions.

| Skill | Wowhead | Icy Veins |
|---|---|---|
| 1–75 | Linen, then Heavy Linen Bandages | Minor Healing Potions |
| 75–150 | Wool, then Heavy Wool Bandages | Lesser Healing Potions to 110, then Healing Potions |
| 150–225 | Silk, Heavy Silk, then Mageweave Bandages | The same bandages |
| 225–300 | Not covered | Mageweave, Heavy Mageweave, Runecloth, then Heavy Runecloth Bandages |

The other guides split the difference:

- **Wowhead:** bandages are usually the cheapest way.
- **wow-professions.com:** either bandages or Minor Healing Potions to 75, then bandages. Its untested 225–300 plan slots in Superior and Major Healing Potions.
- **[wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/first-aid/),** priced from Auction House scans: mixes potions, Simple Poultices (about 90–125) and bandages.

wow-professions.com adds that First Aid kept every skill range from Classic. Mageweave Cloth was almost unobtainable in the beta, so it stayed on Heavy Silk Bandages to 225.

Ranks:

- **Apprentice and Journeyman:** from trainers.
- **Expert:** from the book Expert First Aid – Under Wraps, sold by Deneb Walker in Stromgarde Keep, Arathi (Alliance) or Balai Lok'Wein in Brackenwall Village, Dustwallow Marsh (Horde). The same vendors sell the Heavy Silk and Mageweave bandage manuals, and all three can be traded.
- **Artisan:** from the quest Triage, which needs level 35 and skill 225. It's given by Doctor Gustaf VanHowzen in Theramore (Alliance) or Doctor Gregory Victor in Hammerfall (Horde). You save 15 patients with the doctor's bandages. wow-professions.com advises handing it in the moment you finish, before more patients die and the quest fails.

### What it's worth

- **Healing potions:** Icy Veins notes that anyone can drink them, so they suit players buying consumables on the Auction House, especially while levelling.
- **Tailoring demand:** Wowhead's Tailoring guide mentions that some new cloth recipes use health or mana potions, and suggests First Aid as a source.
- **Your own survival:** bandages provide healing between fights at the cost of cloth and a safe channel.
