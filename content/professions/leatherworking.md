## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** self-crafted upgrades while levelling for Druids, Hunters, Rogues and Shamans, when the specific pieces justify the cost ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/leatherworking)).
- **Pairs with:** Skinning, for leather, hides, scales and the new Pristine and Fel materials; buying leather can still be the better use of time.
- **Levelling route:** wow-professions.com, tested to 225: 1–55 Light Leather, Cured Light Hide · 55–130 Embossed Leather Gloves, Fine and Dark Leather Belts, Heavy Leather · 130–210 Cured Heavy Hide, Hillman's and Guardian Gloves, Nightscape Headbands · 210–225 Nightscape Pants (Artisan trainers only) · 225–300 untested ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/leatherworking-leveling-guide)).
- **Money makers:** guides value it mostly for your own gear. Icy Veins rates armour kits for levelling and mentions rare creations without naming any; every Hunter wants a quiver or pouch ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions)). Bind-on-pickup pieces can't be sold.
- **Camp items:** [Camp Tent](https://foreverdb.net/item/279978) (20), tops rested experience up to 5% of a level · [Tanning Rack](https://foreverdb.net/item/279941) (140), blueprint from Razorclaw the Butcher in Shadowfang Keep · [Sewing Machine](https://foreverdb.net/item/279945) (300), source unknown. Many recipes need the two stations.
- **Trainers and ranks:** Expert from Una (Thunder Bluff) or Telonis (Darnassus) · Artisan at skill 200 and level 35 from Hahrana Ironhide in Feralas (Horde) or Drakk Stonehand in Aerie Peak (Alliance) (wow-professions.com). Role-themed patterns sell for Merchant's Favor at your trade camp.
- **Watch out:** past 225 many recipes turn grey about 25 points earlier than in Classic. Dragonscale, Elemental and Tribal are unconfirmed in Forever. Price Favor and station access before comparing a craft with a dungeon reward.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Choose **Leatherworking when its specific self-crafted upgrades justify the investment**, especially for leather/mail characters. Skinning is a convenient supply partner; buying leather can still be the better use of time. A bind-on-pickup item is a personal benefit, not an Auction House product. Price the complete recipe and Favor/station access before comparing it with a dungeon or quest reward. The [levelling guide](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/leatherworking-leveling-guide) offers alternatives and estimated materials, not a cheapest route for every server. Yellow crafts need a buffer, and the 225–300 route is not established.

### What changed in Forever

**Role-themed sets.** The trade-camp vendors sell a run of new patterns for Merchant's Favor ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/azeroth-commerce-authority-reputation-professions)):

- **Skill 60–110:** belts, boots, tunics, gloves, legs and hoods named Brawler's, Defender's, Stormrider's, Totemic, Trapper's and Wisdom's.
- **Skill 200–220:** gloves, shoulders and bracers named Mender's, Prowler's, Skulker's, Skycaller's, Stalker's and Warden's, some in leather and some in mail.

[ForeverDB's](https://foreverdb.net/profession/leatherworking) gear data shows which spec each name serves, and its list of candidate crafts within the level-30 bracket is long:

- **Wisdom's:** Restoration Druids and Shamans.
- **Stormrider's:** Balance Druids and Elemental Shamans.
- **Trapper's:** Hunters.
- **Brawler's and Defender's:** Rogues, Enhancement Shamans and Feral Druids. The [Defender's Leather Tunic](https://foreverdb.net/item/252450) (skill 110) is an example.

Icy Veins adds that some of this gear binds on pickup, so a leatherworker can make healing pieces that nobody else could hand over.

**Other new items:**

- The blue Black Whelp Slippers and Dark Leather Boots for level 10, from patterns at skill 35.
- A 6-slot [Light Leather Reagent Bag](https://foreverdb.net/item/277715) for Forever's new reagent bag slot.
- Armour kits with new stat lines: Icy Veins describes Forceful kits that add Attack Power and Mystic kits that add Spell Power, each alongside armour.

The early quiver and ammo pouch raise ranged attack speed by 10% (ForeverDB). Endgame recipes such as Warbear Helm and Tranquil Wristguards use Skinning's new Fel Leather, Fel Hide and Pristine materials ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/skinning)). Blizzard's 1 October notes added Leatherworking recipes as random dungeon drops.

**Stations come from camps.** Blizzard says the Tanning Rack is needed for more advanced Leatherworking recipes ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/camping-system-how-campfires-and-profession-buffs-work)). [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide) says both the Rack and the Sewing Machine are needed for many recipes, and that it found such stations only at camps. The Tanning Rack blueprint drops from Razorclaw the Butcher in Shadowfang Keep; the Sewing Machine's source is still unknown.

**Skill ranges moved.** wow-professions.com warns against using a Classic guide past 225: many recipes there turn grey about 25 points earlier in Forever.

### Levelling, in brief

From [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/leatherworking-leveling-guide), tested to 225:

| Skill | What the guide makes |
|---|---|
| 1–55 | Light Leather from scraps (or Light Armor Kits), then Cured Light Hide |
| 55–130 | Embossed Leather Gloves, Medium Leather, Cured Medium Hide, Fine Leather Belts, Light Leather Pants, Dark Leather Belts, then Heavy Leather |
| 130–210 | Cured Heavy Hide, Hillman's Leather Gloves, Barbaric Shoulders or Leggings, Guardian Gloves, then Nightscape Headbands |
| 210–225 | Nightscape Pants, a recipe only the Artisan trainers teach |
| 225–300 | Not yet tested |

[wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/leatherworking/) prices a similar route from Auction House scans, through Light Armor Kits, Embossed Gloves, Hillman's Gloves and Guardian Gloves to about 193.

The trainers, per wow-professions.com:

- **Expert:** Una in Thunder Bluff (Horde) or Telonis in Darnassus (Alliance).
- **Artisan:** needs skill 200 and level 35. Hahrana Ironhide in Camp Mojache, Feralas (Horde), or Drakk Stonehand in Aerie Peak, The Hinterlands (Alliance).

### What's worth making

- **Your own gear:** guides value Leatherworking mostly for this. ForeverDB's [best professions](https://foreverdb.net/professions/best) tool picks Leatherworking and Skinning for Hunters, Rogues, Shamans and Druids.
- **Armour kits:** Icy Veins rates them for levelling, since they add a mix of defensive and offensive stats.
- **Quivers and pouches:** every Hunter wants one (ForeverDB).
- **Selling:** Icy Veins mentions profit from rare creations, without naming items.
- **Title:** at 300 you can buy a Leatherworking Certification and the title "the Leatherworker" for 1,000 Merchant's Favor (Wowhead).
