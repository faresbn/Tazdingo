## At a glance

- **Makes:** cloth armour and cloaks, bags (now including reagent bags), shirts and cosmetic clothing.
- **Suits:** Mages, Priests and Warlocks, who can fill weak slots with their own crafts as they level ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/tailoring)). Bags help everyone.
- **Pairs with:** Enchanting. You disenchant the green items you make, and cloth drops from humanoids, so neither profession needs a gatherer.
- **New in Forever:** cloth sets themed by spell school and role from early levels, more reagent bags for a new bag slot, Skyborne reputation patterns, and new endgame sets.
- **Camp feature:** the [Faction Banner](https://foreverdb.net/item/279973) (skill 20) gives Spirit to members of your faction sitting nearby, and doesn't stack with Divine Spirit. Alliance and Horde each have their own banner. The [Spinning Wheel](https://foreverdb.net/item/279943) (140) makes certain reagents, and the [Loom](https://foreverdb.net/item/279959) (300) is the station some recipes require. Both keep the Banner's buff.

## What the guides say

### What changed in Forever

[Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/tailoring-overview-trainer-locations-recipes) puts the main change simply: in Classic you could level without professions, but in Forever you'll want one going early. Tailoring now lets you craft whole sets for yourself while levelling, and they bind on pickup.

**Spell-school sets.** From Wowhead's pattern tables:

- **Skill 60–125:** six early sets, each tied to a school or role: Flame (fire), Pearly (arcane), Pristine (healing), Shadow (shadow), Shining (holy) and Silky (frost). The trade-camp vendors sell the patterns for Merchant's Favor. Stronger Filigreed versions of some pieces appear to drop from dungeon bosses.
- **Skill 140–235:** a second tier named Black, Fiery, Frothing, Gilded, Golden and Radiant. These need a Spinning Wheel to craft.
- **Skill 195–205:** Nether-named shoulders and cuffs, with only a couple of pieces found so far.

ForeverDB's list of best-in-slot crafts at level 30 and below is full of these pieces:

- [Gilded Slippers](https://foreverdb.net/item/254001) (skill 140): most healers.
- [Shadow Gown](https://foreverdb.net/item/253967) (skill 110): Warlocks and Shadow Priests.
- [Pristine Leggings](https://foreverdb.net/item/253987) (skill 125): healers.

**Other new patterns:**

- **Skyborne reputation:** the Azure and Cloudy Stormsewn patterns, sold on Zephras Isle. Wowhead says the items bind on equip, are weak, and cost a lot for a new character.
- **Level-10 sashes:** Wowhead describes level-10 waist pieces that the trainer teaches at Tailoring 35, made from linen bolts plus health or mana potions. [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions) lists the blue Novice Arcanist's and Novice Ardent's Sash for level 10, from patterns at Tailoring 50, and so does Wowhead's own pattern list. They look like the same items, but the skill is unclear.
- **Endgame:** new sets at 285–300, such as Manaflare, Conviction and Demonheart from a vendor in Mount Hyjal, and Venomspew and Swarmtender from Silithus.

**Reagent bags.** Forever adds a dedicated slot for a reagent bag alongside your five normal bags ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/professions/), Icy Veins). Tailors make the [Linen Reagent Bag](https://foreverdb.net/item/253664) from skill 5, then Mageweave and Runecloth versions, up to a Bottomless Reagent Bag at 300.

**Stations come from camps.** The Spinning Wheel blueprint drops from the Unstable Sentinel in the new City of Dalaran dungeon. The Loom's source isn't known yet ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide)).

**Skill ranges moved.** wow-professions.com warns that past 225, many recipes turn grey 25 to 45 points earlier than in Classic, so a Classic guide will mislead you.

### Levelling, in brief

From [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/tailoring-leveling-guide), tested to 225:

| Skill | What the guide makes |
|---|---|
| 1–55 | Bolts of Linen Cloth, then Linen Belts |
| 55–125 | Bolts of Woolen Cloth, Woolen Capes, Double-stitched Woolen Shoulders, Bolts of Silk Cloth, then Gloves of Meditation (needs Elixir of Wisdom from Alchemy) |
| 125–150 | More Gloves of Meditation, then Bolts of Mageweave or Red Swashbuckler's Shirts |
| 150–225 | Crimson Silk Vests and Pantaloons, Black Mageweave Gloves, then Mageweave Bags |
| 225–300 | Not yet tested |

[wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/tailoring/) prices a close variant from Auction House scans, ending with Crimson Silk Pantaloons and Gloves at about 200. wow-professions.com notes that Mageweave Cloth only drops from level-40 mobs, so it was scarce and dear in the beta. The guide will revisit this step once it sees how fast players reach 40.

Trainers:

- **Expert:** Josef Gregorian (Undercity) or Georgio Bolero (Stormwind), per wow-professions.com and wowtbc.gg.
- **Artisan:** needs skill 200 and level 35. wow-professions.com sends you to Daryl Stack in Tarren Mill (Horde) or Timothy Worthington in Theramore (Alliance).

Wowhead's trainer table, last updated 12 October, lists Gregorian and Bolero as Artisan trainers and Daryl Stack as "Master". Check in game. Wowhead also found that, in the beta, an Expert trainer can teach the lower ranks too.

### What sells

- **Bags:** Icy Veins calls them very profitable, since every character wants bigger bags early.
- **Early gold:** Wowhead says Tailoring has always made good gold, especially early. Cloth is cheap because everyone loots it, so you can craft gear simply to disenchant.
- **Bolts:** crafted bolts also fill the Fabrics version of the Waylaid Crates, which you hand in at your faction's trade camp for Merchant's Favor ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/merchants-favor-durotar-supply-azeroth-commerce)).
- **Title:** at 300 you can buy a Tailoring Certification and the title "the Tailor" for 1,000 Merchant's Favor (Wowhead).
