## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** specific self-crafted mail and plate, mainly for Warriors and Paladins, rather than a promised gold income ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/professions/best)).
- **Pairs with:** Mining, for ore, bars and stone. wow-professions.com warns that buying every bar costs a lot; mining still costs time and gives up ore sales.
- **Levelling route:** the routes differ; compare before buying. wow-professions.com: 1–75 stones, Runed Copper Gauntlets · 75–150 Rough Bronze Leggings, Patterned Bronze Bracers · 150–225 Green Iron Bracers, Golden Scale Bracers, Mithril Plate Pants · 225–300 untested ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/blacksmithing-leveling-guide)). wowtbc.gg runs Copper Chain Belts and Runed Copper pieces.
- **Money makers:** guides say little. Icy Veins mentions Auction House sales and the rods every Enchanter needs ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/blacksmithing)); ForeverDB notes weapon stones help any melee class. The role-themed gear is mainly for yourself.
- **Camp items:** [Sharpening Wheel](https://foreverdb.net/item/279944) (20), a Strength buff that doesn't stack with Strength of Earth Totem · [Anvil](https://foreverdb.net/item/279988) (140), blueprint from Gilnid in the Deadmines · [Master Forge](https://foreverdb.net/item/279955) (300), needed for many recipes, blueprint source unknown.
- **Trainers and ranks:** Expert from Saru Steelfury (Orgrimmar) or Bengus Deepforge (Ironforge) · Artisan at skill 200 and level 35 from Brikk Keencraft in Booty Bay, both factions ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/blacksmithing-leveling-guide)). Role-themed plans sell for Merchant's Favor at your trade camp.
- **Watch out:** past 225 many recipes turn grey about 25 points earlier than in Classic, so don't follow a Classic guide. Check binding and station before spending Favor: Sentinel's Sabatons bind on pickup. Armorsmithing and Weaponsmithing are unconfirmed in Forever.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Choose **Blacksmithing for specific self-crafted equipment or tools**, not a promised general gold income. Check the binding and required station before spending Favor on a plan. Mining reduces purchased inputs but costs time and gives up ore sales; it does not make the armour free. Compare the [alternative levelling recipes](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/blacksmithing-leveling-guide) at current realm/faction prices, including a yellow recipe's extra attempts. A higher material cost can be worthwhile when the output is a real upgrade. The route above 225 remains untested; do not substitute Classic skill ranges where Forever changed them.

### What changed in Forever

**Gear for yourself while you level.** [wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/blacksmithing/) sums up the shift: professions now aim to give you crafted upgrades often as you level. Blacksmithing gets new sets for each role:

- **Chain, skill 45–125:** belts, gloves, boots, helms, shirts and leggings named Acolyte's, Crusader's, Guard's, Protector's and Veteran's.
- **Plate, skill 175–250:** gauntlets, pauldrons, wristguards, sabatons and waistguards named Justicar's, Officer's, Prefect's, Sentinel's and Warder's.
- **Skill 300:** an Enriched Thorium Breastplate, Helm and Leggings, and the Legionite Glaive.

All of these plans are sold for Merchant's Favor at your faction's trade camp ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/azeroth-commerce-authority-reputation-professions)).

[ForeverDB's](https://foreverdb.net/profession/blacksmithing) gear data shows which role each name serves. It identifies Acolyte's pieces for Holy Paladins and Protector's for Protection Paladins; these are candidate upgrades for the reported bracket, not a verified launch best-in-slot list. Guard's suit both Protection specs, and Veteran's suit Arms, Fury and Retribution. Its list of best-in-slot crafted items at level 30 and below is long, from [Veteran's Silvered Chain Leggings](https://foreverdb.net/item/250523) (skill 125) to [Officer's Belt](https://foreverdb.net/item/250556) (150). Icy Veins adds that some of this gear binds on pickup, such as Sentinel's Sabatons, so only a Blacksmith can wear it.

Other changes:

- Rough Copper Vest is now a green item.
- New blue Gemmed and [Glowing Copper Boots](https://foreverdb.net/item/250482) come from plans at skill 35.
- Green Skyforged mail for levels 5 to 7 is taught by plans at skill 50 (ForeverDB).
- Blizzard's 1 October notes added Blacksmithing recipes as random dungeon drops.

**Stations come from camps.** The Anvil blueprint drops from Gilnid in the Deadmines. [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide) says the Master Forge is needed for many Blacksmithing recipes, and that it found such stations only at camps, never out in the world. Where the Master Forge blueprint drops isn't known yet.

**Skill ranges moved.** wow-professions.com warns against following a Classic guide past 225: many recipes there turn grey about 25 points earlier in Forever.

### Levelling, in brief

The two published routes take different paths, so compare them before you buy materials. Both stop around the beta's 225 ceiling.

| Skill | wow-professions.com | wowtbc.gg (priced from Auction House scans) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–75 | Sharpening and grinding stones, then Runed Copper Gauntlets | Sharpening and grinding stones, then Copper Chain Belts |
| 75–150 | Rough Bronze Leggings, Patterned Bronze Bracers, Heavy Grinding Stones, Bronze Shortswords, Bronze Warhammers | Runed Copper Bracers and Belts, Iron Buckles, Bronze Shortswords |
| 150–225 | Green Iron Bracers, Golden Scale Bracers, Mithril Plate Pants (or steel armour if mithril is dear), Heavy Mithril Breastplate | Green Iron Bracers, Solid Sharpening Stones, Truesilver Rods, Golden Scale Bracers (to about 190) |
| 225–300 | Not yet tested | Not covered |

wow-professions.com warns that buying every bar from the Auction House costs a lot, which is why it recommends Mining. Its Expert trainers are Saru Steelfury (Orgrimmar) and Bengus Deepforge (Ironforge). Artisan, which needs skill 200 and level 35, comes from Brikk Keencraft in Booty Bay for both factions.

### What's worth making

- **Your own gear:** most guides value Blacksmithing for this. ForeverDB's [best professions](https://foreverdb.net/professions/best) tool picks Blacksmithing and Mining for both Warriors and Paladins, scoring heavily for crafted gear that binds on pickup.
- **Selling:** guides say little. Icy Veins mentions selling your work on the Auction House. Blacksmiths also make the rods every Enchanter needs (Icy Veins) and weapon stones that help any melee class (ForeverDB).
- **Title:** at 300 you can buy a Blacksmithing Certification and the title "the Blacksmith" for 1,000 Merchant's Favor (Wowhead).
