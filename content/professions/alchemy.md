## At a glance

- **Makes:** elixirs, potions, flasks, oils, dyes and transmutes. It no longer makes the plain healing potions.
- **Suits:** every class. Its consumables help everyone, while levelling and at 60 ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/alchemy-overview-trainer-locations-recipes)).
- **Pairs with:** Herbalism. Nearly every recipe uses herbs.
- **New in Forever:** dozens of new recipes, many of them low-level, which makes Alchemy worth taking while you level. They include new elixir lines, short boost potions, dyes, new flasks and a new transmute. Many are sold for Merchant's Favor at your faction's trade camp.
- **Camp feature:** the [Mana Well](https://foreverdb.net/item/279956) (skill 20) restores mana to everyone sitting nearby and doesn't stack with Blessing of Wisdom. The [Fermenter](https://foreverdb.net/item/279970) (140) makes certain reagents. The [Alchemy Laboratory](https://foreverdb.net/item/279990) (300) is the station some recipes require. Both keep the Mana Well's buff.
- **Tools:** vials in four tiers, and a Philosopher's Stone for transmutes.

## What the guides say

### What changed in Forever

**Healing potions moved to First Aid.** All the sources agree that the plain line, from Minor to Major Healing Potion, is now a First Aid recipe. Alchemy keeps a "Discolored" line instead. [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions) quotes the tooltip of the [Minor Discolored Healing Potion](https://foreverdb.net/item/4596): it heals more, but you then lose 75% of that amount over time as a poison or disease. Wowhead says these are taught by special trainers, Odd Child or Apothecary Renferrel depending on faction. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/alchemy) warns returning players not to look for healing potions in their Alchemy list.

**New recipe families.** From Wowhead's recipe tables and ForeverDB:

- **Mageblood and Cleric's elixirs:** two new elixir lines from skill 25. The [Minor Mageblood Elixir](https://foreverdb.net/item/250339) restores mana over 30 minutes.
- **Mender's, Spellblasting and Frenzy potions:** short boosts to healing, spell damage or Attack Power for a hard fight, from Minor up to Major. The Minor ones last 30 seconds (ForeverDB).
- **Other new potions:** Disorienting Smog, Caustic Smog, Dragonfire Potion, Potion of Venomous Blood and Potion of Elemental Purging.
- **New elixirs for Friendly and Honored standing** with the trade camps: Elixir of the Phalanx, Whale, Owl, Grizzly and Nature Power, among others.
- **Reagents for other crafts:** Cerulean, Magenta and Viridian Dye from Herbalism's new Scarce materials, and Sulfuric Acid from Mining's Pyrite (Icy Veins).
- **Endgame:** four new flasks at 300 (Natural Accuracy, Aggression, Precision and Swiftness), using the new herbs, and Transmute: Legionite, which turns Heavy Thorium Bars into Legionite Bars. Where these recipes come from isn't known yet.

Many of the new recipes are sold by Nina Surefire (Alliance, Three Corners in Redridge) or Apothecary Durelle (Horde, outside the Crossroads) for Merchant's Favor, and the better ones need Friendly or Honored standing (Wowhead). Some recipes can only be made next to a Fermenter or an Alchemy Laboratory. The Fermenter blueprint drops from Rath'mael in the new Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon; the Laboratory's source is still unknown (Wowhead, [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide)).

### Levelling, in brief

[wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/alchemy-leveling-guide) says Alchemy's skill ranges are the same as Classic's, minus the healing potions. Its route, summarised:

| Skill | What the guide makes |
|---|---|
| 1–55 | Minor Arcane Elixir or Elixir of Minor Force (both new), then Elixir of Minor Defense |
| 55–150 | Minor Mana Potions, Elixir of Minor Fortitude, Elixir of Wisdom, Lesser Mana Potions, then Fire Oil (or more mana potions if Firefin Snapper is dear) |
| 150–225 | Elixir of Fire Power, Mana Potions, then Nature Protection Potions from a vendor recipe that binds on pickup |
| 225–300 | Untested at the beta cap: Classic's route without the healing potions. For the last points it suggests the Major Frenzy Potion, sold for Merchant's Favor |

[wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/alchemy/), which prices its route from Auction House scans, takes a different path: Elixir of Minor Defense to 75, then Blackmouth Oil, Fire Oil and Free Action Potions, with Mana Potions to about 200. Both stop near the beta's 225 ceiling.

Ranks need skill 50 and level 10 (Journeyman), 125 and level 20 (Expert), and 200 and level 35 (Artisan), per Icy Veins and wow-professions.com. For Artisan, wow-professions.com sends Horde to Rogvar in Stonard and Alliance to Kylanna Windwhisper in Feathermoon Stronghold; Wowhead also lists Kylanna Windwhisper.

### What's worth making

- **Transmutes:** Wowhead calls them an easy profit. Transmute: Arcanite and the like are long-cooldown spells that turn cheap materials into dear ones.
- **Consumables:** Wowhead expects steady demand because so many players buy them, though the best sellers will shift with the market.
- **Elixirs:** Icy Veins notes that elixirs stack with each other, which makes them sought after at endgame.
- **Reagents:** Icy Veins calls the oils, dyes and other reagents a strong income, because other professions need them for their own gear.
- **Live prices:** the site's own price tables show today's margins.
