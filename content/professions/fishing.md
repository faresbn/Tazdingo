## At a glance

- **Gathers:** fish for Cooking and for Alchemy's oils, plus junk, trunks and other loot from pools and floating wreckage.
- **Suits:** anyone; it's a secondary profession and needs no materials. [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/fishing/overview-leveling) picks out Shamans (Far Sight and Water Walking help reach schools) and Hunters (raw fish feeds pets).
- **Pairs with:** Cooking, so you level both from the same catch. Alchemists also want a few fish.
- **New in Forever:** camp items, older fish dishes reworked to raise Fishing skill, more winners at the Stranglethorn tournament, a new fish (Whimsyfin) and a Find Fish tracking ability.
- **Camp feature:** the [Fish Bowl](https://foreverdb.net/item/279967) (skill 20) gives 8% to all stats and doesn't stack with Blessing of Kings. Blizzard's post calls it a "Fishing Bowl". The [Fishing Rack](https://foreverdb.net/item/279965) (140) lets you catch uncommon fish for an hour and hands out lures. The [Fishing Hut](https://foreverdb.net/item/279966) (300) does the same for rare fish. Both keep the Bowl's buff.
- **Tools:** a fishing pole, and lures when fish keep getting away.

## What the guides say

### What changed in Forever

Wowhead says Fishing itself barely changed. What changed is around it:

- Many fish recipes in Cooking were buffed or added, and most cooked food now grants extra experience from kills.
- Fishing makes its own camp items. The Rack blueprint drops from Gelihast in Blackfathom Deeps (Wowhead); where the Hut's blueprint drops is unknown. The Hut uses Seasonal Fish Steaks, prepared from Raw Winter Squid or Raw Summer Bass ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fishing)).
- Some Classic fish dishes now raise Fishing skill for 15 minutes, from Loch Frenzy Delight (+3, level 5) up to Filet of Redgill and Spinefin Halibut (+18).
- The Stranglethorn Fishing Extravaganza now has 50 winners instead of one. Every winner gets an Arcanite Fishing Pole and an Extravagant Extravaganza Coin. The coin buys one of several rewards, such as the Hook of the Master Angler, which turns you into a fish while swimming. Icy Veins says the tournament runs every Sunday, 2 to 4 p.m. server time, and that 20 Speckled Tastyfish buy a Message in a Bottle, which can hold rare endgame items.
- [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions) notes a Find Fish ability that shows fishing pools on the minimap.
- Icy Veins adds Whimsyfin, caught east of Lake Everstill in Redridge, for the new Savory Whimsyfin Delight.

**How skill works.** Wowhead and Icy Veins agree that you gain Fishing skill per catch, and a harder zone doesn't give points faster. Fish where you can land fish reliably and move only for the catch you want. Your effective skill rises with:

- **Poles:** a Strong Fishing Pole from vendors, the Big Iron Fishing Pole from shellfish traps off Desolace, a Horde-only pole from a Hinterlands quest, and the Arcanite Fishing Pole.
- **Lures:** Shiny Bauble up to Aquadynamic Fish Attractor.
- **Gear:** a hat, boots and a line from the tournament's rare fish, plus a glove enchant.
- **Food:** the dishes above.

The Luremaster Legacy perk gives a chance to catch an extra fish while a lure is active.

### Levelling, in brief

The guides largely agree on the route.

| Skill | Where to fish | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–75 | Any starting zone | Use a Shiny Bauble until about 25 |
| 75–150 | A capital city | Wowhead says you won't need a lure here |
| 150–225 | Dustwallow Marsh or Stranglethorn Vale | Expert comes from a book, Expert Fishing – The Bass and You, sold by Old Man Heming in Booty Bay. Wowhead suggests Dustwallow for Alliance and Stranglethorn for Horde; use lures until 225 |
| 225–300 | Tanaris (Wowhead) or Feralas, Felwood, The Hinterlands, Un'Goro and Western Plaguelands (wow-professions.com) | Artisan first, and keep using lures |

Artisan comes from the quest Nat Pagle, Angler Extreme, from Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh. It needs level 35 and Fishing 225, and asks for four quest-only fish from Feralas, Swamp of Sorrows, Desolace and Stranglethorn ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/fishing-leveling-guide), Wowhead).

### Which catches are worth keeping

- **Cost:** Wowhead points out that Fishing costs essentially nothing, so every catch is profit, and the right fish can bring steady gold.
- **Oil fish:** Icy Veins lists Oily Blackmouth, Firefin Snapper and Stonescale Eel, which Alchemists turn into Blackmouth, Fire and Stonescale Oil.
- **Buff-food fish:** Raw Sunscale Salmon and Large Raw Mightfish (Attack Power), Raw Winter Squid and Raw Summer Bass (crit), Raw Whitescale Salmon and Darkclaw Lobster (spell damage), and Raw Plated Armorfish (armour).
- **Rack and Hut fish:** the uncommon and rare catches these unlock haven't been listed yet.

### Where guides disagree

- **Rank levels:** Icy Veins and wow-professions.com say Journeyman needs level 10 and the Expert book level 20. ForeverDB found that the Journeyman and Expert rank spells carry level 1 in the beta client.
- **Late zones:** they suggest different zones for 225–300, though both say skill gains don't depend on the zone.
