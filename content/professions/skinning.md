## At a glance

- **Gathers:** leather, hides and scales from looted beasts, plus new Pristine materials.
- **Suits:** leather and mail wearers (Druids, Hunters, Rogues, Shamans), who often pair it with Leatherworking to make their own gear. [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/skinning/overview-leveling) adds that Dwarves and Trolls do 5% more damage to Beasts, and Hunters can track them.
- **Pairs with:** Leatherworking, or Mining or Herbalism for a two-gatherer character.
- **New in Forever:** Pristine Leather, Hide and Scale as extra drops, Fel Leather and Fel Hide for endgame recipes, its own camp items, and beasts to skin from the starting areas on.
- **Camp feature:** the [Camp Chair](https://foreverdb.net/item/279979) (skill 20) gives 2% critical strike chance, which doesn't stack with Moonkin Aura. The [Field Guide](https://foreverdb.net/item/279969) (140) teaches Track Beasts to anyone who reads it. The [Trapper's Workbench](https://foreverdb.net/item/279938) (300) holds one trap; [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/skinning) says the trap holds a skinnable beast.
- **Tools:** a [Skinning Knife](https://foreverdb.net/item/7005) in your bags. You don't need to equip it.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

**Skin eligible beasts you are already killing**, especially when supplying Leatherworking. A dedicated beast circuit is worthwhile only if leather proceeds and skill gains repay the added kills and travel. [Wowhead's route](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/skinning/overview-leveling) is a starting point, not proof that every listed zone beats the quest route on a crowded realm. Corpses must be looted and each can be skinned once. Check the corpse tooltip rather than relying on an unresolved skill formula. Pristine and endgame leather scarcity may create demand, but neither its drop rate nor a profitable launch price is established.

### What changed in Forever

Like the other gatherers, Skinning now has a crafting menu, and its only recipes are its three camp items (Wowhead). The Chair comes from the trainer. The Field Guide blueprint drops from Lady Anacondra in Wailing Caverns (Wowhead, [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide)).

New materials, per Icy Veins:

- **Pristine Leather, Pristine Hide and Pristine Scale:** Scarce drops that go into new Leatherworking, Blacksmithing and Tailoring recipes, bags among them. The odds and skill needed aren't known yet (Wowhead).
- **Fel Leather and Fel Hide:** for new endgame items such as Warbear Helm, Tranquil Wristguards and Mystic Rugged Armor Kit. Where to skin them is unknown.

Rules worth knowing:

- Each corpse can be skinned once. Blizzard restored this with the 1 October build, and a skinned corpse now disappears ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions)). Icy Veins adds that this holds even when several group members can skin.
- You can skin other players' kills once their loot has been taken (Wowhead).
- You can learn Apprentice Skinning in your starting zone, from a trainer or by picking Pelt Collecting for Beginners as an early quest reward (Wowhead).
- Skinning has no Legacy challenges. Bountiful Harvest raises your Scarce finds.

### How much skill a beast needs

Wowhead gives this formula: beasts up to level 10 need skill 1, beasts from 11 to 20 need (level − 10) × 10, and above that it's level × 5. [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/profession/skinning) uses the same formula, but labels it Classic's rule and an assumption for Forever. Icy Veins says Forever made the requirements "much more friendly" for a smoother climb, without giving numbers. Some endgame beasts need more than 300 skill, so the boosts matter: the Keen Skinning Knife (+3, from the Horde quest Unwelcome Guests) and two glove enchants.

### Levelling, in brief

If you skin what you kill while questing, your skill mostly keeps up with your level. The zones below are for a dedicated session. Wowhead covers 1–225 (the beta's ceiling). The last row comes from ForeverDB's Classic-data route, and the leather tiers come from Icy Veins.

| Skill | Where guides send you | Typical leather |
|---|---|---|
| 1–75 | Any starting zone, such as Durotar or Elwynn Forest | Ruined Leather Scraps, Light Leather |
| 75–125 | The Barrens (north to south), Loch Modan, Darkshore | Light Leather and Hide |
| 125–175 | Ashenvale, Duskwood, Hillsbrad Foothills, Wetlands | Medium Leather and Hide |
| 175–225 | Arathi Highlands (raptors near Refuge Pointe), Stranglethorn Vale, Thousand Needles | Heavy Leather and Hide |
| 225–300 | Tanaris, Dustwallow Marsh, Feralas | Thick Leather; Rugged comes later, in Winterspring, Felwood and the Plaguelands |

Special leathers come from particular creatures: whelps, deviate beasts in Wailing Caverns, turtles, scorpids, dragonkin and devilsaurs. Icy Veins lists where to find each.

### Ranks

Wowhead's route trains Journeyman from Thuwd in Orgrimmar and Expert from Dranh in Camp Taurajo, and says Artisan is available at skill 200. Icy Veins agrees on 50, 125 and 200 skill. The character level each rank needs is disputed. Icy Veins' [overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/professions-overview) gives gathering ranks lower levels (none, 10, 25). ForeverDB's levelling page gives 10, 20 and 35, from Classic.

### Making money

- Wowhead rates Skinning one of the easiest gold-makers, because it adds semi-passive income to kills you were making anyway. It suggests favouring quests that send you after beasts and skinning corpses other players leave.
- Icy Veins notes that exotic leathers fetch high prices and that the new Pristine materials feed several professions.
- [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/making-money) gives vendor floors: Light Hide sells for 50 copper and Medium Hide for 1 silver 25 copper. Leather itself sells for little to a vendor.
- Leather also fills Waylaid Crates, which you trade at your faction's camp for Merchant's Favor ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/merchants-favor-durotar-supply-azeroth-commerce)).
