## Cheatsheet

- **Pick it for:** access to enchants and early crafts, especially wands for Mages, Priests and Warlocks; don't count on Tailoring to finance it.
- **Pairs with:** Tailoring, whose greens you disenchant; neither needs a gatherer. Icy Veins calls Enchanting expensive if you buy everything and Tailoring cheap to level ([Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/enchanting)). A crafted green isn't free material.
- **Levelling route:** wow-professions.com, tested to 225: 1–70 Runed Copper Rod, bracer enchants with Motes of Magic · 70–150 minor chest, bracer, cloak and boot enchants, Silver Rod at 110 · 150–225 Golden Rod, Minor Mana Oil, Lesser Agility boots, Truesilver Rod at 200 · 225–300 untested ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide)).
- **Money makers:** Icy Veins says rarer enchants fetch the higher prices, but selling one needs customers and the formula. Since 1 October crafted common and uncommon wands vendor for 1 copper, so make them to use ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions)).
- **Camp items:** [Enchanted Lute](https://foreverdb.net/item/279976) (20), armour, stats and resistances, doesn't stack with Mark of the Wild · [Arcane Salvager](https://foreverdb.net/item/279985) (140), more efficient disenchanting by an unstated amount · [Arcane Forge](https://foreverdb.net/item/279987) (300), a station, blueprint source unknown.
- **Trainers and ranks:** Expert from Hgarth at Sun Rock Retreat (Horde) or Kitta Firewind at the Tower of Azora (Alliance) · Artisan at skill 200 and level 35 only from Annora inside Uldaman, who alone teaches several recipes from 230 ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide)).
- **Watch out:** starting-zone trainers have no supply vendor (except Zephras Isle), so buy Motes and your first rod in a capital. Disenchant is now in the Professions window. Learn Annora's recipes before leaving Uldaman.

## What the guides say

### Tazdingo recommendation

Choose **Enchanting for access to its crafts and enchants**, especially early wands, rather than assuming Tailoring will finance it. Compare buying a wand with training and crafting one. For disenchanting, compare expected material sale value with the item's vendor or Auction House value; do not count a crafted green as free material or assume a guaranteed dust yield. The [route](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide) uses cheaper starter recipes but still has training, rod and recipe costs. Selling a rare enchant depends on finding customers and acquiring the formula, not just its material margin. The Arcane Salvager's unquantified benefit is not a guaranteed return.

### What changed in Forever

**A cheaper start.** [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/enchanting-leveling-guide) says a few new low-level recipes make the first 70 points cheaper than in Classic. They use the [Mote of Magic](https://foreverdb.net/item/247786), which Enchanting Supply vendors sell. The new [Dust to Motes](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1245320) recipe splits one Strange Dust into three Motes (ForeverDB). Starting-zone trainers have no supply vendor beside them, except on Zephras Isle, so you buy Motes and your first rod in a capital. Disenchant now lives in the Professions window rather than your spellbook.

**More things to make.** The trade-camp vendors sell formulas for Merchant's Favor ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/azeroth-commerce-authority-reputation-professions)), including staves, orbs and relics, which Wowhead and Icy Veins present as new work for enchanters:

- **Staves:** Glimmering Staff, Soulstaff, Dreamstaff and Radiant Staff.
- **Off-hand orbs:** [Orb of Souls](https://foreverdb.net/item/249395) and Orb of Mystic Insight.
- **Class relics:** for example Tenets of the Silver Hand, Libram of Invocation, Totem of Ancestral Protection and Idol of the Dream.
- **Necklace enchants** at skill 210: Agility, Strength, Spell Power, Healing Power and Deflection.
- **At skill 300:** two-hand Mighty Spell Power, Mighty Healing Power and Spellblasting enchants, plus several glove, bracer and cloak enchants.

The same vendors sell the Brilliant Wand (skill 275), which [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/enchanting) notes binds on pickup.

ForeverDB lists two new Lesser two-hand enchants, Agility and Strength at +15 each, against +5 for the older Lesser ones. It also flags two crafted items as notable crafts within the level-30 bracket: the [Lesser Magic Wand](https://foreverdb.net/item/11287) for most casters, and the Orb of Souls for Holy and Discipline Priests and Restoration Druids.

Gatherers will want the gathering-glove enchants. Icy Veins lists +2 and +5 versions for Herbalism and Mining, and +5 and +10 for Skinning. The Lotus Claw glove enchant gives a chance at Death Lotus when picking herbs in Hyjal ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/herbalism/overview-leveling)).

**Skill ranges barely moved.** Unlike Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring, almost every Enchanting skill range is unchanged from Classic, according to wow-professions.com.

### Levelling, in brief

From wow-professions.com's route, which is tested to 225:

| Skill | What guides make |
|---|---|
| 1–70 | Runed Copper Rod, then bracer enchants with Motes of Magic, then cheap chest or bracer enchants with Strange Dust or Lesser Magic Essence |
| 70–150 | Minor chest, bracer, cloak and boot enchants. Runed Silver Rod at 110. Enchant 2H Weapon – Lesser Intellect if you buy the formula |
| 150–225 | Runed Golden Rod, Minor Mana Oil, boots with Lesser Agility (Soul Dust), Vision Dust enchants, Runed Truesilver Rod at 200, then Lesser Wizard Oil |
| 225–300 | Untested: Artisan, then Dream Dust and Illusion Dust enchants, with a Runed Arcanite Rod near 295 |

[wowtbc.gg](https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/professions/enchanting/) prices a simpler route from Auction House scans. It makes stamina and protection enchants on bracers, chest, cloak and boots, then boots with Lesser Agility to about 200.

The trainers are unusual:

- **Expert:** one trainer per faction. Horde train with Hgarth at Sun Rock Retreat in Stonetalon; Alliance train with Kitta Firewind at the Tower of Azora in Elwynn.
- **Artisan:** needs level 35 and skill 200, and only Annora inside Uldaman teaches it. She appears once her area is cleared, and she alone teaches several recipes from 230 up, so learn them before you leave (wow-professions.com).

### Where the gold is

- **Enchanting for others:** Icy Veins says the rarer enchants command the higher prices.
- **Disenchanting your own crafts:** Tailoring's green items keep you in materials. Icy Veins warns that Enchanting is expensive if you buy everything, while Tailoring is cheap to level.
- **Strange Dust:** it comes mainly from disenchanting greens from about levels 5 to 25, and vendors stock only small amounts (ForeverDB).
- **Wands:** since the 1 October build, crafted common and uncommon wands sell to vendors for 1 copper, so make them to use, not to sell (ForeverDB).
- **Title:** at 300 you can buy an Enchanting Certification and the title "the Enchanter" for 1,000 Merchant's Favor (Wowhead).

### Open questions

- Where the Arcane Forge blueprint drops. The Arcane Salvager blueprint drops from the Shade of the Archmage in the City of Dalaran ([wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide)).
- How much "more efficient" the Salvager makes disenchanting. No source gives a number.
