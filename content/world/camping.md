## At a glance

- **What "camping" means here:** Forever's campfire system, a shared campsite that gives buffs and services. Waiting at a rare's spawn point is covered on [Rare spawns](ledger:world/rares/).
- **How a camp starts:** a cook places a campfire, and anyone nearby can add one profession-made camp feature to it.
- **The reward:** sit or craft at the camp for one minute, then get one combined buff that lasts an hour.
- **Camp size:** a Basic Campfire holds 3 features, a Journeyman one 5 and an Expert one 10.
- **No double-dipping:** each camp buff is a weaker version of a class buff and doesn't stack with it.
- **Unlocking:** a quest around level 5 introduces camping; each profession's first feature comes at skill 20, and the better ones need Blueprints, many from dungeon bosses.
- **Cooldowns:** one feature per player, on a shared one-hour cooldown; a campfire burns for about 10 minutes and has a 5-minute cooldown.

## What the guides say

### How a camp works

Blizzard's [camping article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301516) (21 October) describes the system: Cooking's old Cooking Fire becomes a Basic Campfire, and players nearby each add one crafted object. Everyone who sits or crafts at the camp for a minute gains the benefits of the other objects there. Each benefit replaces a class buff rather than stacking with it.

[Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/camping) adds the detail: sitting down gives a one-minute *Welcoming Campfire* countdown, after which every camp benefit lasts an hour. [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/camping/) says the effects merge into one *Camp Benefits* buff, and that sitting again picks up newly placed features without losing the old ones. A beta build in September let players buff, cast and craft while waiting, and counts sitting in a chair, sleeping, lying down or kneeling as sitting ([Blizzard's development notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696)).

You can camp almost anywhere outdoors except in major cities, with a minimum distance between fires (Icy Veins). Blizzard warns that a camp is riskier on a PvP ruleset, where enemy players can find it.

### Campfire sizes

The kits are single-use. [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide) suggests crafting a stack of Basic Campfire Kits at home, since Simple Wood is cheap from trade and general goods vendors.

| Campfire | Learn at | Use at | Features | Made from | Recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 1 | 1 | 3 | Simple Wood | Comes with Cooking |
| Journeyman | 90 | 140 | 5 | Star Wood | Blueprint from Lord Serpentis, Wailing Caverns |
| Expert | 200 | 220 | 10 | Thick Logs | Blueprint, source not found yet |

Sources quote different skill numbers. [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/camping-overview-unlock-rewards) and Icy Veins give 90 and 200; [ForeverDB's client data](https://foreverdb.net/camping) and Warcraft Tavern give 140 and 220. wow-professions.com explains the gap: you learn the Blueprint at the lower skill but can only use the kit at the higher one, which it suspects is a bug.

### What each profession adds

Every profession has three features, at skill 20, 140 and 300. Cooking's first "feature" is the campfire itself. The higher tiers keep the tier 1 buff, so a Master Forge still gives the Sharpening Wheel's Strength (ForeverDB). Whether tier 3 also keeps the tier 2 station isn't confirmed (wow-professions.com).

| Profession | Skill 20 | Skill 140 | Skill 300 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alchemy | Mana Well: mana every 5 sec (not with Blessing of Wisdom) | Fermenter: reagent station | Alchemy Laboratory |
| Blacksmithing | Sharpening Wheel: Strength (not with Strength of Earth Totem) | Anvil | Master Forge |
| Enchanting | Enchanted Lute: armour, all stats, resistances (not with Mark of the Wild) | Arcane Salvager: better disenchanting | Arcane Forge |
| Engineering | Reagent Bot: a reagent vendor | Repair Bot: adds repairs | Anarchist's Workbench |
| Herbalism | Incense Candle: Intellect (not with Arcane Intellect) | Greenhouse: grows herbs from seeds | Seed Hybridizer |
| Leatherworking | Camp Tent: rested XP up to 5% of a level | Tanning Rack | Sewing Machine |
| Mining | Lodestone: melee attack power (not with Blessing of Might) | Rock Garden: spawns a common ore node | Molten Foundry |
| Skinning | Camp Chair: +2% crit (not with Moonkin Aura) | Field Guide: read it to gain Track Beasts | Trapper's Workbench: one trap |
| Tailoring | Faction Banner: Spirit for your faction (not with Divine Spirit) | Spinning Wheel | Loom |
| Cooking | (the campfires) | Cookie's Feast: Stamina food | Iron Oven |
| First Aid | First Aid Kit: Stamina (not with Power Word: Fortitude) | Toxin Study: healing potions and anti-venom | Plague Doctor's Laboratory |
| Fishing | Fish Bowl: +8% all stats (not with Blessing of Kings) | Fishing Rack: uncommon fish for an hour, lures | Fishing Hut: rare fish for an hour, lures |

Most buffs scale with your level. The Lodestone gives 12 to 90 attack power and the First Aid Kit 3 to 56 Stamina, for example (ForeverDB). The Camp Tent works once an hour, tracked by a *Boosted Rest* debuff (Warcraft Tavern). Two small disagreements: Blizzard's article table leaves out Mining's Lodestone, though the client has it, and Blizzard's September Deep Dive recap said the Sharpening Wheel raised attack power, while the later article and the client say Strength.

The Engineering bot sells class reagents and profession supplies. wow-professions.com found no Tanning Rack, Loom or similar station anywhere in the world, so many higher-level recipes will need a camp that has one.

### Unlocking features

Around level 5, the quest *The Great Outdoors* asks you to `/sit` at a campfire, then sends you to learn Cooking (Wowhead). At skill 20, each profession's first feature comes from the trainer or a starter-zone quest, *Camping 101*; both quests are optional (wow-professions.com). Blizzard says items needing skill 140 or more drop in dungeons and raids. The tier 2 Blueprints found in the beta:

| Blueprint | Drops from |
|---|---|
| Journeyman Campfire | Lord Serpentis, Wailing Caverns |
| Fermenter | Rath'mael, Ruins of Lordaeron |
| Anvil | Gilnid, Deadmines |
| Arcane Salvager | Rath'mael, Ruins of Lordaeron (Icy Veins); Shade of the Archmage, City of Dalaran (wow-professions.com) |
| Repair Bot | Sneed, Deadmines |
| Greenhouse | Shadetooth, Excavation Site: Wetlands |
| Tanning Rack | Razorclaw the Butcher, Shadowfang Keep |
| Rock Garden | Relic Guardian, Excavation Site: Wetlands |
| Field Guide | Lady Anacondra, Wailing Caverns |

| Cookie's Feast | Cookie, Deadmines |
| Toxin Study | Commander Springvale, Shadowfang Keep |
| Fishing Rack | Gelihast, Blackfathom Deeps |

Where the tier 3 Blueprints and the Expert Campfire drop is unknown: they sit above the beta's level 30 cap. Camp items can be traded and sold on the Auction House, but placing one needs the skill (Icy Veins).

### Legacy perks and other extras

- **Field Guide** (Adventure tree) cuts the feature cooldown by 8/17/25% (ForeverDB; Icy Veins lists 8/16/25%).
- **Permanence** (Resourcefulness) makes camp benefits last 50/100% longer.
- **Reagent Economy**: Blizzard and Wowhead say it also makes tier 1 camp features free to craft; the client tooltip on ForeverDB mentions only class reagents.
- **Cozy Sleeping Bag:** a quest chain from level 14 gives a bag that adds 1% experience per minute slept, up to 3%, and stacks with camp buffs and cooked food (Warcraft Tavern).
- **Addons:** AzerothArchive Campsite Tracker shares the camps you find with your group and guild; ForeverArtisan covers camping alongside all twelve professions ([Addons](ledger:more/addons/)).
