## At a glance

- **Rares, not campfires:** this page covers rare spawns. In Forever, "camping" is the campfire system; see [Camping](ledger:world/camping/).
- **Mostly unconfirmed:** ForeverDB lists 354 rares, but only 19 had been seen in Forever by 24 October. The rest come from Classic data.
- **New loot:** Blizzard says "hundreds of new rare-creature drops have been added". Beta players found set pieces, rings and companion pets.
- **New rares:** foreverchanges.pro counted 42 rares that Forever adds, eight of them on Zephras Isle.
- **Respawn timers:** the published timers are Classic's and haven't been measured in Forever.
- **Tracking:** RareScanner and SilverDragon have Forever downloads; ForeverMapNotes and ForeverDB's addon also map rares.
- **Above level 30, little is known:** the beta stopped at 30.

## What the guides say

### Forever data versus Classic data

[ForeverDB's rare list](https://foreverdb.net/rares) holds 354 rares and rare elites in 38 zones and 10 dungeons, 149 of them at or below level 30. Only 19 have been seen in Forever, through the game's cache and players' ForeverDB addons. The rest, and every respawn timer, come from Classic's data (the CMaNGOS project) and are labelled that way. Blizzard's [Deep Dive recap](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24303313) promises many new rare drops, so Classic loot tables are a poor guide.

The 19 seen in Forever, as of 24 October:

- **Dun Morogh:** Timber
- **Durotar:** Warlord Kolkanis, Felweaver Scornn
- **Elwynn Forest:** Mother Fang, Thuros Lightfingers
- **Silverpine Forest:** Gorefang
- **The Barrens:** Stonearm, Rocklance, Snort the Heckler, Humar the Pridelord, Brontus, and the four Alliance Outrunners (Aean Swiftriver, Thora Feathermoon, Hannah Bladeleaf, Marcus Bel)
- **Wetlands:** Ma'ruk Wyrmscale
- **Scarlet Monastery (Graveyard):** Azshir the Sleepless, Fallen Champion, Ironspine

### Notable rares and drops from the beta

[foreverchanges.pro](https://foreverchanges.pro/rares) collects players' and readers' reports (last updated 13 October). These are single reports, not Blizzard data.

|---|---|---|

| The Alliance Outrunners | Southern Barrens, 24–25 elites | Four scouts riding together; each can drop its own weapon. ForeverDB gives them a respawn of about 7 minutes |
| Nightveiled Rotheap | Wetlands, 31–32, new elite | Comes ashore at night; Alliance players trade its Rotheap Innards to Rethiel the Greenwarden for the Malignant Root ring |
| Heartrazor | Thousand Needles, 32 | Wyvern Heart Band |
| Taskmaster Whipfang | Stonetalon Mountains, 22 | Whipfang's Skinsearer dagger |
| Foreman Rigger | Stonetalon Mountains, 24 | Foreman's Helm, with +5 Mining |
| Dro'zem the Blasphemous | Redridge Mountains, 23 | Dro'zem's Tunic |
| Elmpaw | Elwynn Forest, 12, new elite | A bear whose head starts an Alliance quest |

The client also holds a *Rider of the Plaguelands* set (faster riding in the Plaguelands), but nobody has found its pieces; foreverchanges.pro guesses Plaguelands rares drop them, unconfirmed.

Some rares now drop companion pets ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/collections/pets-locations-sources)): the Sewer Beast in Stormwind (Baby Crocolisk), Lord Condar in Loch Modan (Shaking Egg) and the Strider Clutchmother in Darkshore (Tallstrider Hatchling).

### Rares in the new zones

foreverchanges.pro lists eight rares on Zephras Isle: Den'dralass, Fernfeather, Galemender Delanea, Mystmane, Slydris, Snapbeak the Quick (Thendal Watcher's Boots), Tel'daeor the Stormspeaker (Scout Ranger's Leggings) and The Lost One. It has one on Mount Hyjal, Pexmit. Nothing is reported yet for the Riverglades or Shen'dralas, which were above the beta cap. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/news/wow-forever-is-hiding-instance-portals-nobody-can-enter-yet/) also notes a level 60 Mistmantle Prowler that troubled low-level players in Duskwood during the beta.

### Dungeon rares

Icy Veins' dungeon guides list a rare in most early dungeons: the Deviate Faerie Dragon in Wailing Caverns, Miner Johnson in the Deadmines, the Deathsworn Captain in Shadowfang Keep, Bruegal Ironknuckle in the Stockade, Earthcaller Halmgar and the Blind Hunter in Razorfen Kraul, three in the Scarlet Monastery Graveyard, and the Lordaeron Captain in the new Ruins of Lordaeron. See [Dungeons and raids](ledger:world/dungeons-and-raids/).

### Respawns and tracking

ForeverDB's timers range from minutes to days, but they are Classic's, so treat them as rough. Its rare pages show when players last saw each one. Addons with a Forever download on CurseForge (checked 26 October):

- [RareScanner](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarescanner): alerts for rares, treasures and events on your minimap
- [SilverDragon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/silver-dragon): rare scanning with drop information
- [ForeverMapNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forevermapnotes-bysiberiano12): map notes for rares with drops filtered for your class, plus ore, herbs, chests and flight paths
- [HandyNotes: WoW Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes-wow-forever): map notes for things to find in Forever

For Hunters, ForeverDB lists 63 tameable rares. Forever adds two pet families, Core Hound and Fox ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/veterans)). On Zephras Isle, Mystmane (a bear) and Fernfeather (a tallstrider) can be tamed (foreverchanges.pro).
