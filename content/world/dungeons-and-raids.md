## At a glance

- **35 dungeons in 28 instances,** from level 13 to 60 (Icy Veins).
- **Nine are new:** Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site, City of Dalaran, the Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and the Shaper's Terrace.
- **Only four of the new ones were playable** in the level 30 beta, so guides for the rest are still to come.
- **Raids open on 9 December:** Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia's Lair (40, needs the Drakefire Amulet).
- **Raid rules:** weekly resets planned; no heroic modes or flexible sizes; world buffs removed on entry (Icy Veins).
- **Dungeon quests** give half the bonus experience they did early in the beta.
- **Camping Blueprints** drop from many dungeon bosses ([Camping](ledger:world/camping/)).

Notes on the table:

- **New dungeon descriptions** follow [Blizzard's dungeon overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301455). The Level 30 beta build listed Excavation Site at 26–31 ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/launch/level-30)), a third range.
- **City of Dalaran attunement:** Alliance players start with *An Alarming Request* from Emissary Jacques in Southshore; Horde players with *Key to the City* and *Prison Break-In* from Magus Wordeen Voidglare in Tarren Mill (Blizzard). Icy Veins says the Horde need the resulting Dalaran Sewer Key, while the Alliance can reach the sewers from inside the city.
- **Boss names differ** between sources for two new dungeons: Icy Veins names Magmatus and The Baron where ForeverDB's client data has Infurnus and The Butcher, and Icy Veins lists three Excavation Site bosses where the client has four.
- **Not on the list:** the client holds a new instance, Manor Mistmantle in Duskwood, that Blizzard hasn't announced, and several Season of Discovery leftovers such as Karazhan Crypts ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/dungeons)).

### The raids

All three open on Wednesday 9 December for level 60 characters, and all are planned to reset weekly ([Blizzard's raid overview](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24303009)).

| Raid | Players | Bosses | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barrow Deeps | 10 | 8 | Beneath Mount Hyjal, the prison that held Illidan; entrances from Mount Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold |
| Hyjal Summit | 20 | 13 | The summit near Nordrassil; opens with an ancient guardian, then branches into several paths |
| Onyxia's Lair | 40 | 1 | Dustwallow Marsh; a three-phase fire fight, so bring fire resistance |

Boss lists from the client's Legacy challenges ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/legacy)):

- **Barrow Deeps:** Chillhowl, Elder Tangleclaw, Khalith the Dreadspinner, Well of Sorrow, Amethrax, Del'lynar Songwood, Ravus and Darlissa, Sonya Darkhallow. [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/raids/) has a Deepscar Matriarch in place of Chillhowl.
- **Hyjal Summit:** Bandalar, Ancient of Decay, Time-Lost Battalion, Sylvestris Dusksong, Old Gloomlurker, Gharalis the Abyssal, Kathris the Haunted, Anara Chillwind, Elder Minderel, Tracker Stillwind, Council of Thorns, Nythus the Dreambound and the Wild King.

[Icy Veins' raid overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/raid-overview) adds the design rules: tier pieces drop in all three raid sizes, every boss has rare "chase" items, item stats stay hidden until someone loots them, and power gains between tiers are kept small. There are no heroic or mythic modes and no scaling, so a 20-player raid needs exactly 20. Blizzard has promised a legendary tied to the first raids; Icy Veins says it comes from Hyjal Summit. No attunement has been announced for the Barrow Deeps or Hyjal Summit. The client has a Brooch of the Wardens that grants access to a prison wing inside the Barrow Deeps, but what it's for isn't known ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/lore/place/mount-hyjal)).

Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Zul'Gurub, both Ahn'Qiraj raids and Naxxramas are in the client, but Blizzard hasn't dated them. On its podcast, a developer said older raids like Molten Core will bring mechanics players haven't seen ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/podcast-molten-core-new-mechanics-raid-difficulty)).

### Onyxia attunement

Every raider needs the Drakefire Amulet in their bags; it doesn't have to be worn. Blizzard advises starting early, bringing friends for the elite quests and expecting several high-level dungeon runs.

**Alliance:**

1. In the Burning Steppes, take *Dragonkin Menace* from Helendis Riverhorn, then follow the six *The True Masters* quests.
2. Marshal Maxwell at Morgan's Vigil sends you into Blackrock Depths after Marshal Windsor (*Marshal Windsor*, then *Abandoned Hope*).
3. Loot *A Crumpled Up Note* from Blackrock Depths enemies and take it to Windsor in his cell.
4. In *A Shred of Hope*, recover his papers from Golem Lord Argelmach and General Angerforge, then free him in *Jail Break!*.
5. Go to Stormwind (*Stormwind Rendezvous*), see *The Great Masquerade* through with a group, and take *The Dragon's Eye* from Highlord Bolvar Fordragon.
6. Finish *Drakefire Amulet* with the Blood of the Black Dragon Champion from General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire.

**Horde:**

1. In Kargath (Badlands), take *Blood and Thunder* from Warlord Goretooth, then *Warlord's Command* in Lower Blackrock Spire.
2. Report to Eitrigg and Thrall in Orgrimmar, then bring back Warchief Rend Blackhand's head in *For the Horde!*. Blizzard's guide places Rend in Lower Blackrock Spire; in Classic he is fought in the upper spire, so check in game.
3. After *What the Wind Carries*, find Rexxar roaming Desolace (*The Champion of the Horde*), carry his testament to Myranda the Hag in the Western Plaguelands, and gather Black Dragonspawn Eyes in *Oculus Illusions*.
4. Use the Amulet of Draconic Subversion to find Emberstrife in Dustwallow Marsh, and pass his four *The Test of Skulls* quests (Scryer, Somnus, Chronalis and Axtroz).
5. Take the Dull Drakefire Amulet (*Ascension...*) back to Rexxar, then bring him the Blood of the Black Dragon Champion from General Drakkisath in Upper Blackrock Spire.
