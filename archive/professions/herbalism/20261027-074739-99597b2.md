## At a glance

- **Gathers:** herbs, plus herb seeds and three new "Scarce" materials that sometimes drop alongside the herb.
- **Suits:** anyone who wants gold early. [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/herbalism/overview-leveling) singles out Druids (Travel Form, Prowl) and pet classes, whose pets can hold a mob while they pick.
- **Pairs with:** Alchemy, which uses herbs in most recipes, or Mining for a two-gatherer character. In Forever, First Aid also uses herbs for its healing potions.
- **New in Forever:** a crafting menu of its own (camp items only), three new endgame herbs, seeds, Scarce materials for dyes, and a reworked Tauren Cultivation.
- **Camp feature:** the [Incense Candle](https://foreverdb.net/item/279962) (skill 20) gives an Intellect buff that doesn't stack with Arcane Intellect. It upgrades to the [Greenhouse](https://foreverdb.net/item/279964) (140), which grows herbs from planted seeds, and the [Seed Hybridizer](https://foreverdb.net/item/279947) (300), which multiplies seeds or combines them into rarer ones.
- **Tools:** none. Turn on Find Herbs; only one kind of tracking works at a time.

## What the guides say

### What changed in Forever

Gathering professions now have recipes. Herbalism's are its three camp items, and [Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/professions/herbalism/overview-leveling) says they are the only things a herbalist crafts. The Candle is taught by the trainer; Wowhead and [wow-professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/camping-guide) say the Greenhouse blueprint drops from Shadetooth in the new Excavation Site: Wetlands dungeon. Where the Seed Hybridizer blueprint drops isn't known yet.

New materials:

- **Endgame herbs:** Marefoil, Demonsage and Death Lotus. Neither Wowhead nor [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/herbalism) knows where they grow. Icy Veins ties them to four new Alchemy flasks (Natural Accuracy, Aggression, Precision and Swiftness) and to new enchants.
- **Seeds:** Commonplace, Uncommon, Unusual and Scarce Herb Seeds, for the Greenhouse and Hybridizer. Which herbs drop which seeds is still unclear (Wowhead).
- **Scarce materials:** Frilled Lichen, Rosecap and Stranglevine. Icy Veins says Alchemists turn them into Cerulean, Magenta and Viridian Dye, which other crafts use. The Bountiful Harvest Legacy perk raises how many you find (Wowhead).

Other changes:

- You can learn Apprentice Herbalism in your starting zone, from a trainer or by picking Wild Harvest as the reward from an early quest. Starting areas even have small low-skill nodes (Wilted Peacebloom, Stunted Silverleaf), per Wowhead.
- Tauren [Cultivation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20552) now grows a harvestable copy of a nearby herb, and you don't need Herbalism to pick it. [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions) adds that the 24 September beta notes gave it a level requirement: the herb's skill divided by 5. [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/professions/) points out that this doubles a Tauren herbalist's yield.
- Gathering earns no Legacy Points. Wowhead lists no Herbalism challenges, and ForeverDB says the tradeskill challenges (150, 225, 300) count only crafting professions.

### Ranks

The skill caps are 75, 150, 225 and 300, and Icy Veins gives 50, 125 and 200 as the skill needed to train the next rank. The character level is less clear. Icy Veins' Herbalism page gives 10, 20 and 35, as does ForeverDB's levelling page, which takes them from Classic. Icy Veins' own [professions overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/professions-overview) gives gathering professions lower ones: none for Journeyman, 10 for Expert and 25 for Artisan. Treat this as unconfirmed until launch.

### Levelling, in brief

Herbalism levels by picking anything that isn't grey. Follow your questing route and the skill mostly keeps pace. Wowhead's route stops at 225, the beta's ceiling. The last row comes from ForeverDB's route, which uses Classic spawn data.

| Skill | Where guides send you | Main herbs |
|---|---|---|
| 1–70 | Any starting zone | Peacebloom and Silverleaf, then Earthroot from 15 |
| 70–115 | Darkshore, Loch Modan, Silverpine Forest | Mageroyal, Briarthorn, Stranglekelp (it grows underwater) |
| 115–170 | Hillsbrad Foothills, Wetlands, Stonetalon Mountains | Bruiseweed, Wild Steelbloom, Kingsblood, Liferoot |
| 170–225 | Stranglethorn Vale and Arathi, then Alterac Mountains, Tanaris, Searing Gorge | Fadeleaf, Goldthorn, Khadgar's Whisker, Wintersbite (Alterac only), Firebloom, Purple Lotus |
| 225–300 | Felwood, Feralas, Azshara, the Plaguelands, Winterspring | Arthas' Tears up to Black Lotus |

Skill boosts stack and let you pick higher herbs early: Herbalist's Gloves, two glove enchants and [Goldthorn Tea](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/herbalism), a Cooking recipe worth +15 skill for 15 minutes.

### Making money

- Wowhead calls Herbalism a strong gold-maker while you level. Herbs sell well because many recipes use them, and in Forever more professions do.
- With Alchemy as your second profession, you can gather nearly everything you need yourself (Wowhead).
- Icy Veins notes that most endgame recipes use high-level herbs, and that rare herbs fetch high prices.
- A vendor pays almost nothing for herbs (Peacebloom sells for 10 copper, per [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/professions)), so their value is on the Auction House.
- Herbs also fill Waylaid Crates, which you hand in to your faction's trade camp for [Merchant's Favor](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever/merchants-favor-durotar-supply-azeroth-commerce). Other professions spend that currency on recipes, so it gives a gatherer something to trade for.
