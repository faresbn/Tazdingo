## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** cloth, leather and mail from level 1, plate from 40, and shields. Almost every melee weapon, with polearms and dual-wielding from level 20. Bows, guns, crossbows and thrown weapons go in the ranged slot for pulling ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-warrior)).
- **Roles:** tank as Protection, melee damage as Arms (two-hander) or Fury (two weapons).
- **Resource:** Rage. It fills as you hit and get hit, and drains away out of combat.
- **Races:** every race can be a Warrior, including both Skyborne races.
- **What's new:** [Tactical Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310185) is baseline at 14, [Victory Rush](https://foreverdb.net/spell/402927) arrives at 20, and [Slam](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1240193) is a level 20 strike with an 18 sec cooldown. Rage generation was rebuilt.
- **Talents:** one point per level from 10, so 21 at the beta cap of 30 and 51 at 60. The capstones [Mortal Strike](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12294), [Bloodthirst](https://foreverdb.net/spell/23881) and [Shield Slam](https://foreverdb.net/spell/23922) need level 40.
- **Hybrid status:** Blizzard counts Warriors as hybrids because they can tank, so Arms and Fury are aimed at about 5% less damage than pure classes (see the hybrid page).

## What the guides say

### Race choice after the 26 October changes

[Blizzard's 26 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7) remove Orc Savage, set Human Ruthless to 1% crit and Skyborne Wind-Blessed to 2% haste, replace Dwarf Earth Fury with 1% hit, and leave Tauren Endurance at 5% health only. Do not use older weapon-specialisation or critical-damage bonuses to choose a race. Pet Command does not boost Warrior attacks; tank survival, movement and control can justify a choice beyond modelled damage.

### Tazdingo recommendation

For a first solo Warrior, **Protection is a strong default once Revenge and its shield talents are available**, especially if dungeon tanking is welcome. Arms is the simpler two-handed alternative; Fury asks for two useful weapons and receives key tools later. That explains why guides disagree about levelling speed: safer recovery and fewer deaths can beat faster individual kills ([Protection](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/protection/level-30-tank-overview), [Arms](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/arms/level-30-dps-overview)). Keep weapons trained and current, avoid over-levelled enemies and compare whole-route pace. No level-30 result proves the level-60 spec order.

The Beta stopped at level 30. That is the available play bracket, not proof that every setup within it was tested. Above-30 advice comes from files and design notes.

### How Forever changed the class

**Rage.** Blizzard's [Priest and Warrior deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24301514) changed how Rage is earned. Rage from being hit ignores your actual armour and absorbs. The original 50% assumed mitigation was superseded by [2 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/5), which use level-dependent expected values in the 20–40% range. Those notes explicitly cover both Warriors and Druids. Rage from dealing damage is normalised to the speed of the weapon that hit, so bigger hits no longer give more Rage. Critical strikes were set to give 75% more Rage, and Blizzard's [2 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/warrior-updates-in-todays-beta-build/2369360) raised that to 100% after a Dual Wield Specialization bug skewed its tests. Wowhead's Fury overview still says crits give no extra Rage; that text predates the change.

**Stances.** Defensive Stance comes from a level 10 class quest and Berserker Stance from a level 30 one. Tactical Mastery keeps 10 Rage when you switch, and the Arms talent [Improved Tactical Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12295) keeps up to 15 more ([ForeverDB talents](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/talents)).

**Baseline changes** from the deep dive and [ForeverDB's changes page](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/changes):

- Victory Rush becomes usable for 20 sec after you kill an enemy worth experience. It heals 10% of your maximum health on a 30 sec cooldown.
- [Thunder Clap](https://foreverdb.net/spell/6343) works in Defensive Stance, slows attacks by 20% (10% in Classic) and has a 6 sec cooldown. It and [Rend](https://foreverdb.net/spell/772) now scale with attack power.
- [Shield Wall](https://foreverdb.net/spell/871) cuts damage by 60% for 12 sec on a 15 min cooldown. It, [Retaliation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20230) and Recklessness no longer share a cooldown.
- [Shield Block](https://foreverdb.net/spell/2565) blocks two attacks over 7 sec. Taunt has an 8 sec cooldown.
- [Battle Shout](https://foreverdb.net/spell/6673) lasts 3 min and reaches the whole raid since the 16 October build, but gives less attack power per rank.
- Rend and Sunder Armor tag an enemy to you as soon as they land.

**Talents.** The Axe, Mace, Sword and Polearm specialisations merged into one Arms talent, [Weaponmaster](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1290261). [Improved Slam](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12862) moved to Arms and stops Slam delaying your swing. Toughness, Improved Cleave and Precision are gone. ForeverDB counts 10 new and 24 changed talents. Blizzard says one-point milestone talents sit at 11, 16, 21 and 31 points, talents are changed at a capital-city trainer, and Dual Specialization opens at level 40.

Blizzard's stated identity for the class is sustained damage against several melee targets. It wants Warriors to be "the kings of cleave damage" (WoW: Forever Podcast, via [ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)). The deep dive adds that Warriors lack tools to recover once they have taken too much damage.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | Weapon | What the guides say |
|---|---|---|
| Arms | Two-hander | Icy Veins rates it weak solo but strong in groups. Wowhead calls it a good pick for new players thanks to the new built-in swing timer and Improved Slam. ForeverDB sees Anger Management as the most direct fix for an empty Rage bar. |
| Fury | Two one-handers from 20 | Wowhead says it is not the strongest leveller and is best with other players around. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/fury-warrior-leveling) says it only comes together with Flurry (35) and Bloodthirst (40). |
| Protection | One-hander and shield | The tank. Shield Specialization gives 5 Rage per block and Master of Defense 5 per dodge or parry, which fixes Warrior Rage problems. |

### Which spec for levelling

The guides lean towards Protection. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/protection-warrior-leveling) calls it the fastest Warrior levelling spec and the one it recommends. It credits stronger Revenge, a shield that cuts downtime, and talents that both add Rage and make abilities cheaper. Wowhead's [Level 30 Protection page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/protection/level-30-tank-overview) agrees that once Revenge arrives at 14, sword-and-board levels as well as a two-hander or better. ForeverDB's [levelling guide](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-warrior) is more even-handed: Arms for a simple start, Fury for crits and passive talents, Protection if you die too often or want to tank, accepting slower kills.

Advice shared across all three guides:

- Weapon upgrades matter more than anything else for Arms and Fury, so take quest weapons and run dungeons for them.
- Victory Rush at 20 makes solo questing much smoother. Wowhead suggests sticking to quests and enemies of your level or lower.
- Mining and Blacksmithing pair well while levelling, since crafted mail rates highly at 20 and 30. Engineering is the usual endgame pick.

### Class quests

The Berserker Stance quest starts at any capital Warrior trainer at 30 (except Darnassus). It leads to a gauntlet on Fray Island that ends with Big Will, a level 33 enemy. Klannoc Macleod then starts the long Whirlwind Weapon chain, which ends against Cyclonian, a level 40 elite ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/arms/level-30-dps-overview)). The Brutal Hauberk chain can start at 20 but runs through Razorfen Kraul, so most players do it later ([Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/warrior/protection/level-20-tank-overview)).
