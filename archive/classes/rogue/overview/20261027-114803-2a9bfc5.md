## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** Leather and cloth. Daggers, one-handed swords, maces, fist weapons and, new in Forever, one-handed axes. An off-hand weapon from level 10, and a bow, crossbow, gun or thrown weapon for pulling ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-rogue)).
- **Roles:** Melee damage only. Blizzard calls the Rogue the class with the highest potential single-target melee damage in the game.
- **Resource:** Energy and combo points. Wowhead and Icy Veins say Energy now refills smoothly, at about 10 per second, instead of in 2-second ticks, and that combo points stay on a target when you switch away.
- **Signature tools:** Stealth, poisons (from a level 20 class quest), Sap, which no longer breaks Stealth, Kick, Gouge, Vanish and, from 26 and 30, Cheap Shot and Kidney Shot.
- **Specs:** Assassination (daggers and poisons, Mutilate at 30), Combat (any weapon, sustained damage and Blade Flurry) and Subtlety (openers, bleeds and burst, Hemorrhage at 30).
- **Pure class:** Rogues are one of the four pure damage classes Blizzard uses as the bar for its roughly 5% "hybrid tax", so the tax doesn't apply to them ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)).
- **Races:** Human, Dwarf, Night Elf, Gnome and High Order Skyborne (Alliance); Orc, Undead, Troll and Windshaper Skyborne (Horde). Tauren can't be Rogues. Skyborne Rogues are new.

## What the guides say

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [class deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24310968) keeps the Classic kit and changes how it fits together:

- Poisons have more charges and can sit on a weapon alongside a sharpening stone or weightstone.
- [Sap](https://foreverdb.net/spell/6770) no longer breaks Stealth, so you can Sap one mob and still open on another. Blizzard wants it used as group crowd control.
- Feint sheds twice the threat. Distract also lowers enemies' Stealth detection for 10 seconds, though enemy players can tell a Rogue is near.
- Expose Armor now matches five stacks of Sunder Armor and no longer stops Warriors from using Sunder.
- Rogues can learn one-handed axes. Combat's old weapon specialisations are merged into one talent, Hack and Slash, that rewards whichever weapon you hold.
- New talents include [Mutilate](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310707) (Assassination tier 5), [Venom](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310703) (Assassination capstone), Puncturing Wounds and Flawless Execution (Combat), and Quietus, Cutthroat and [Thousand Cuts](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310721) (Subtlety). [Coup de Grace](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310709) is a new trainer passive at 30 that makes Eviscerate stronger against targets with your Deadly Poison.

The client shows some numbers moving down as well as up. Rupture deals far less than in Classic, Backstab's flat bonus is lower, and Dual Wield Specialization gives half the off-hand bonus ([ForeverDB changes](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/changes)). Wowhead adds that poisons now scale with attack power and that missed abilities refund some Energy. ForeverDB says poison scaling is a server rule it can't confirm from the client.

### Poisons

You learn Poisons at level 20 from a class quest: Klaven's Tower for the Alliance, Hinott's Assistance for the Horde. You then craft them from vendor reagents ([ForeverDB poisons](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/poisons)). Instant Poison and Crippling Poison come at 20, Mind-numbing Poison at 24 and Deadly Poison at 30. Icy Veins recommends Instant Poison on both weapons while levelling. ForeverDB's Assassination group build pairs Deadly Poison with Instant Poison for long fights.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | What it does | Levelling verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Combat | Sinister Strike or Backstab, Slice and Dice, Eviscerate, Blade Flurry | The most forgiving: any one-handed weapon, no need to stand behind the mob (ForeverDB). Wowhead calls it the most complete spec at 20 and 30 |
| Assassination | Mutilate, poisons, Cold Blood; Venom at 40 | Wowhead finds it underwhelming at 20, where it plays like Combat. It spikes at 30 with Mutilate, which needs a dagger in each hand |
| Subtlety | Stealth openers, Hemorrhage, Rupture | Also underwhelming at 20 (Wowhead). Hemorrhage at 30 is the reason to level it (Icy Veins) |

A dagger Backstab build works in any spec. Puncturing Wounds sits in Combat's second tier, and Wowhead's level 20 pages recommend the same dagger split (Combat with two points of Subtlety's Opportunity) for all three specs (see [Hybrid builds](hybrid.md)).

### Which spec to level

Combat is the safe recommendation: ForeverDB names it the most forgiving, and Icy Veins' Combat levelling guide builds around Riposte at 20 and Blade Flurry at 30. Assassination gets a big power spike at 30 with Mutilate, as long as you have two daggers (Icy Veins, Wowhead). Combat also has the fewest weaknesses later on, because it doesn't rely on poisons or bleeds, which some enemies are immune to (Wowhead). ForeverDB's simulator shows auto attacks making up 43–50% of a Rogue's damage at levels 20–30 in every spec, so a strong main-hand weapon matters more than any single stat.

### Races and professions

Advice differs. Icy Veins' Assassination and Combat guides pick Gnome (Eureka! and Escape Artist) and Orc (Blood Fury, Savage and Hardiness). Its Subtlety guide prefers Human or Night Elf, and Orc for PvP or Troll for PvE. Wowhead rates Human, Skyborne and Gnome about equal for the Alliance, Orc or Skyborne for the Horde, and Troll as nearly unmatched for levelling, thanks to its health regeneration. Several racials changed on 16 October: Human Ruthless and Orc Savage became flat crit bonuses, and Wowhead reports Skyborne's Wind Blessed haste rose to 3%. Skinning with Leatherworking is the common pick, with First Aid for bandages, since Rogues can't heal themselves.

### Class quests worth doing

Wowhead and Icy Veins point to two Stealth quests: The Horn of Xelthos at 20 (rewarding the Pyrewood Signet Ring) and The Eye of Bhossca at 30 (the Ruby-Adorned Blade, which Wowhead rates the best weapon at 30 for Combat and Subtlety).

### Looking ahead to 60

The [WoWSims presets](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/ui/specs/rogue/dps/presets.ts) model level 60 with gear still marked developer-only. They default to a Combat Sinister Strike build (18/33/0) with Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off hand. Treat this as a projection.
