## At a glance

- **Armour and weapons:** cloth, leather and mail from level 1, plate from 40, and shields. One- and two-handed axes, maces and swords, plus polearms from 20. A libram goes in the ranged slot; no bows, guns or thrown weapons ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-paladin)).
- **Roles:** healer as Holy, tank as Protection, melee damage as Retribution.
- **Resource:** Mana. Most early downtime is drinking, not eating.
- **Races:** Human and Dwarf (Alliance), Undead (Horde). Undead Paladins are new in Forever and have their own storyline.
- **What's new:** [Holy Strike](https://foreverdb.net/spell/679) at 6, a tanking seal, [Seal of Fury](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1311649), at 10, and [Judgement](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20271) no longer uses up your Seal. [Consecration](https://foreverdb.net/spell/26573) and [Blessing of Kings](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20217) are trainer spells at 20.
- **Limits:** Blizzard says Paladins still have no interrupt, no way to slow enemies and little range. They are strong against Undead and Demons.
- **Hybrid status:** Retribution is a hybrid damage spec, aimed at about 5% less damage than pure classes (see the hybrid page).

## What the guides say

### Race choice after the 26 October changes

[Blizzard's 26 October notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696/7) set Human Ruthless to 1% crit and replace Dwarf Earth Fury with 1% hit. Hit helps attacks and harmful spells, not healing spells landing on friendly targets. Older critical-damage/healing or weapon-specialisation comparisons are obsolete. Undead remains the Horde option; a simulated Alliance race is not a requirement for the class to work.

### Tazdingo recommendation

For mainly solo questing, **Retribution is the straightforward two-handed choice**. Protection becomes attractive for pack control and regular dungeon tanking; Holy makes most sense when healing a regular party. Claims that Protection is faster than Retribution describe particular Beta gear, pulls and spell ranks, not every route ([Protection](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/level-30-tank-overview), [Retribution](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/retribution/level-30-dps-overview)). Carry role-appropriate gear and count drinking, deaths and group availability when comparing. A strong defensive cooldown does not make a pull risk-free.

The beta stopped at level 30. Holy Shock is the deepest talent anyone could reach; Light's Vigil, Twist of Light and Holy Shield (all level 40) are untested.

### How Forever changed the class

Blizzard's [Paladin deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099) keeps Seals and Judgements at the centre and gives each spec a clearer job. Blizzard sums up the result as effective tanks, competitive damage and "the strongest single-target healers in the game". The main baseline changes:

- **Judgement** leaves your Seal active. It has a 10 sec cooldown and 10 yard range, and doesn't trigger the global cooldown, so you Judge every time it is ready ([ForeverDB macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/macros)).
- **Holy Strike** is an instant strike for part of your weapon damage plus Holy damage, on a 10 sec cooldown. Every spec has a talent built around it.
- **Seal of Fury** adds the same Holy damage to every swing whatever your weapon speed, which favours fast weapons. With a shield it also gives small absorbs. Its Judgement taunts from 10 yards, against 5 for a Warrior's Taunt.
- **Consecration** hits the first four enemies properly and everything else lightly, to match Thunder Clap.
- **Lay on Hands** is on a 20 min cooldown instead of an hour. Blessings last an hour.
- **Salvation** is no longer a Blessing. Since the 15 October build it is a separate spell, with **Revoke** to remove it.
- **Seal twisting** is gone as a baseline trick and returns as the Retribution capstone [Twist of Light](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310735).
- **[Righteous Fury](https://foreverdb.net/spell/25780)** went from 90% to 60% Holy threat on 24 September and back to 90% on 15 October ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/article/paladin-deep-dive-seals-judgements-holy-strike-protection-retribution)). Some ForeverDB guide pages still say 60%.

Most of a Paladin's damage now scales with spell power: Seals, Judgement, Holy Strike, Consecration and Retribution Aura. Wowhead says spell power gear competes with Strength even on Retribution, and calls this a divisive change among players. ForeverDB notes that melee and spell hit are a single stat in Forever.

The talent tree is one tree with three sections ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-paladin)). It has 18 new talents. Holy Shock moved to the 21-point slot (level 30), with a 10 sec cooldown. Blizzard says Dual Specialization opens at 40. The client also lists [Hammer of the Righteous](https://foreverdb.net/spell/407632), a Season of Discovery rune, as a trainer spell at 40.

### Choosing a spec

| Spec | What the guides say |
|---|---|
| Holy | Blizzard rates it the best single-target healer. Healing a whole party needs Light's Vigil at 40 and planning. Solo it kills slowly, so both Icy Veins and ForeverDB offer levelling builds that still fight in melee. |
| Protection | A Mana tank that holds packs with Consecration and Retribution Aura and taunts with Judgement. Wowhead says it holds threat reliably from 16, once Righteous Fury and Retribution Aura arrive. |
| Retribution | Two-handed melee with Seal of Command or Seal of Righteousness. Icy Veins calls it very efficient for levelling. Wowhead reports beta testers thought it overtuned at level 20. |

### Which spec for levelling

The guides lean towards Retribution. ForeverDB calls it the fastest-killing levelling build and the only one that reaches row 5 by 30. [Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/retribution-paladin-leveling) praises its damage, self-healing and escape tools. Even Icy Veins' Holy and Protection levelling plans spend their first 10 or so points in Retribution, for Benediction, Holy Conduit and Pursuit of Justice (cheaper spells and faster movement).

Protection is the safe alternative. ForeverDB says it rarely has to stop, and Wowhead's [Level 30 Protection page](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/classes/paladin/protection/level-30-tank-overview) argues sword and board handles the open world better than Retribution between 20 and 30. Holy levels slowest but can heal dungeons along the way.

Shared advice:

- Use a slow two-hander for Retribution and a shield for Protection.
- Heal yourself with spare Mana instead of eating.
- Mining with Blacksmithing suits the class; Blacksmithing's crafted mail sets rank well at 20 and 30.

### Class quests

Wowhead finds no Paladin class quest at 30. The Alliance chain that starts with Return to Delgren (from 22) ends in Cloak of the Divine Storm, a strong cloak for the level. Icy Veins points Alliance players to Verigan's Fist and Horde players to Wolfsbane, weapon quest chains that start at 20. Blizzard says Undead Paladins get their own epic mount quest at 60.
