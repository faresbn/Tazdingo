## At a glance

- **How mixing works:** one tree with three sections and one point per level from 10. Each row needs 5 more points in its own section, so 21 points at 30 reach row 5 of one section.
- **Most borrowed talents:** Retribution's [Benediction](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20101), [Holy Conduit](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1237268) and [Pursuit of Justice](https://foreverdb.net/spell/26022), for cheaper spells and faster movement, and Holy's [Improved Seals](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20224) and [Divine Intellect](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20257).
- **Levelling hybrids:** Icy Veins starts both its Holy and Protection levelling plans with about 11 points in Retribution. Wowhead's level 20 tank build is all Retribution.
- **The Shockadin:** Blizzard placed Holy Shock as an alternative to Twist of Light so damage Paladins can build into Holy instead.
- **Hybrid tax:** Retribution is aimed at about 5% less damage than pure classes. It is a design target, not a number in the game files.
- **Dual roles:** Dual Specialization opens at 40. Before that, the guides offer builds that level in one role and still fill another.

## What the guides say

### How mixing works

The Paladin talent window is a single tree with Holy, Protection and Retribution sections ([ForeverDB](https://foreverdb.net/guide/leveling-paladin)). Each row opens with 5 more points in that section. Blizzard's [deep dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302099) puts one-point milestone talents at 11, 16, 21 and 31 points. Several first-row and second-row talents are strong for any spec, which is why points drift between sections. The Legacy perk Talented adds up to five points early, but both Wowhead and Icy Veins note it needs Well Rested in the Legacy tree first.

### The Shockadin

Blizzard calls Holy Shock (21 points, level 30) a deliberate alternative to Twist of Light. Players who don't want to seal-twist can take a hybrid Holy build instead. Blizzard says two more talents support the playstyle: [Divine Precision](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310904) gives Holy spell hit, and [Light's Vigil](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310911) lets a second Holy Shock follow without a cooldown. Blizzard also cut Champion of the Light's Intellect-to-spell-damage bonus so Holy Paladins going deep into Retribution don't become too strong.

How the guides rate it:

- **Wowhead at 20** rated an all-Holy build (Divine Strength, Divine Intellect, Improved Seals) stronger in PvE than the Retribution build, if you stack spell power.
- **Wowhead at 30** skips it because Mana runs out, preferring the standard Retribution talents.
- **[Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/retribution-paladin-melee-dps-pve-guide)** offers an all-Holy level 30 build with Holy Shock as an alternative tab.
- **WoWSims** keeps a level 60 Holy Shock preset with 23 Holy and 28 Retribution, and picks its rotation automatically from your talents.

### The hybrid tax

Blizzard's developers said on episode 2 of the WoW: Forever Podcast that hybrid damage specs should deal about 5% less than Hunters, Mages, Rogues and Warlocks. Paladins are hybrids because they can heal and tank ([ForeverDB's write-up](https://foreverdb.net/article/blizzard-hybrid-tax-5-percent-warriors-count-as-hybrids)). The figure is an average target, not a cap. It can't be checked in the client, and no patch notes mention it. The same episode explains why Retribution didn't get strong self-healing talents: Blizzard is deliberately keeping hybrids from healing well in their damage specs, so groups still need a healer. Every hybrid buff is meant to be balanced by a cut elsewhere.

### Dual-role play

Blizzard says Dual Specialization opens at level 40. Before then:

- **Retribution can off-tank.** Instrument of Law cuts your threat only while Righteous Fury is off, so Blizzard designed it to let a Retribution Paladin switch Righteous Fury on and help tank.
- **Levelling builds can fill a second role.** ForeverDB says its Protection levelling build can tank a dungeon without a respec, and its Holy melee build can heal one.
- **Seal of Fury follows the shield.** ForeverDB's macros page has a Seal key that casts Seal of Fury with a shield on and Seal of Righteousness without (see the macros page).
- **Holy Shock and Light's Vigil work both ways**, damaging an enemy or healing a friend.

Wowhead's Retribution overview is the counterweight. It says tanking or healing well means changing your talents, so a damage build only fills in for short spells.
