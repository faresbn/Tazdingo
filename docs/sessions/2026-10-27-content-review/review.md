# Language and recommendation review — 27 October 2026

## Scope and result

Editorial sweep of all 77 content files: 54 class/spec/hybrid/macro files, 12 professions, seven world topics and four More guides. Each now starts its guide discussion with a **Tazdingo recommendation**: a default for a stated job, the case for alternatives, costs and evidence limits. The source summaries remain available below it. This is editorial synthesis and targeted source checking, not verification of every linked claim or an in-game certification.

All Field findings remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No page was promoted to Live or given a fabricated in-game verification date. Market extracts and receipts are unchanged.

## Evidence checked

The source review prioritised claims that change a build choice, route, purchase or macro action. Agreement between websites was not counted as independent confirmation.

- **Official development notes:** [topic 2360696](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/wow-forever-beta-development-notes-%E2%80%93-updated-october-26/2360696), including posts 5, 6 and 7. The official Discourse topic JSON was read for complete patch context because the web reader could not consistently open the forum HTML. The latest post was updated 26 October at 21:09:50 UTC. The raw research copy is transient, not republished.
- **Class recommendations:** reopened Wowhead level-30 guides for Hunter, Priest, Mage, Rogue, Warrior, Druid, Paladin and Shaman; reopened current Warlock overview/level-30 guides, Icy Veins Warlock guides and ForeverDB levelling/PvE material. Guide dates vary and some still describe retired mechanics; later official notes took precedence. These checks support the scoped decisions, not a new independent raid ranking.
- **Profession routes:** reopened wow-professions.com Forever Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Fishing, Leatherworking, Mining and Tailoring routes; Wowhead Herbalism and Skinning overviews. Material lists are estimates and late routes are provisional. Guessed Herbalism/Skinning URLs at wow-professions.com did not exist and were not added as sources.
- **World decisions:** reopened the official Legacy and What's Next recaps, new-dungeon overview, wow-professions.com camping and Merchant's Favor guides, ForeverDB rares/pets and Method's mount guide. Official news-feed text also confirms the planned 9 December raid opening.
- **Addon status:** checked the primary CurseForge projects for TradeSkillMaster, WeakAuras and Musca Auras on 27 October. The rest of the catalogue retains its stated 26 October snapshot date. Musca's author documents combat-log and imported-trigger limitations.

New recommendations cite the relevant primary guide or official rule inline. Existing Read more lists remain the paths to fuller source discussions. Source summaries are written in our own words.

## Remaining uncertainty

- This was not a link-by-link audit of all roughly 800 distinct references, many of which are item/spell links. The checked evidence above must not be represented as 800 independently verified claims.
- No launch gameplay, macro runtime, new sales, raid performance or profession route was tested. Numerical simulator weights and damage gains retained in source summaries are historical model snapshots; the exact simulations were not reproduced here.
- Some Warlock endgame talent-build pages could not be reopened. Their configurations remain historical candidates, and their previous absolute ordering is withdrawn.
- Frost calculator/client talent discrepancies, several gathering rank gates, rare respawns/drop rates and above-cap routes remain unresolved. The recommendations explain how to act without assuming those details are settled.
- The full official raid article could not be fetched reliably. Existing attunement notes remain provisional, including the source's ambiguous location for Rend; no new verified walkthrough is claimed.
- Crafting tables do not contain a complete binding, recipe-acquisition or stock model. Their visible caveats require checking those conditions before spending. Asking-price data cannot establish sell-through or demand.
