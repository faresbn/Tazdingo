# Source record: Druid pages (research agent, 27 Oct 2026)

Pages: `content/classes/druid/` overview, balance, feral, restoration, hybrid, macros. All pass the site's header and section checks. "What the guides say" runs long: Feral about 1,630 words (Cat and Bear plus seven WoWSims lists); Balance, Restoration and Hybrid about 1,100 each.

## Sources read (every page)

- **Blizzard:** the official Hunter and Druid class deep dive.
- **ForeverDB:** llms-full.txt, class guide, PvE, talents, builds, macros, levelling guide; articles on the hybrid tax, the deep dive, class highlights and the outlook.
- **Wowhead** (browser): Balance overview, level 20, level 30, abilities; Feral DPS overview, 20, 30; Feral tank overview, 20, 30; Restoration overview, 20, 30; the macro guide.
- **Icy Veins:** the three spec guides, the class overview, Feral levelling, the talent calculator.
- **WoWSims:** four presets.ts, the spec.ts files, all nine APLs and the talent tree JSON.
- **Kami Labs:** the macro tool and its article.
- Not used: Icy Veins' Balance and Restoration levelling pages, Method, Warcraft Tavern, Mobalytics.

## Uncertain claims

- Wowhead and Icy Veins talent builds were decoded from their calculator links using WoWSims' tree order; three were checked against Wowhead's talent-order panel and matched.
- Wowhead's level 30 damage-over-time Balance build spends only 20 points.
- ForeverDB lists Shred at level 20 in a table but 22 in its text; its two write-ups give different Lacerate numbers.
- From Wowhead alone: Thorns snapshots spell power; Skyborne is a paid upgrade; the Cat Form quest is in a Moonglade cave (ForeverDB says the Stormrage Barrow Dens).

## Contradictions and out-of-date sources (handled on the pages)

- Wowhead's Feral DPS overview and level 30 tank page still describe Tiger's Fury and King of the Jungle, both removed. Icy Veins' class overview still mentions Mangle and Tiger's Fury, and gives Hurricane a cooldown Blizzard removed. Wowhead's Balance abilities page carries Classic values (5% Moonkin aura, 20-minute Rebirth).
- Shifting Power's energy ceiling: 50 (Wowhead, WoWSims) vs 60 (Icy Veins). Icy Veins rates Rake low; others keep it up.
- Kami Labs' powershift macro claims to grant Energy, which the Furor rework says it no longer does; included with a warning.
- Talent names: the client says Natural Instinct and Blood Frenzy; Icy Veins says "Predatory Instincts" and "Primal Fury"; Blizzard's article mislabels Natural Instinct as Predatory Strikes.
- Shred: Blizzard says its damage rose significantly; ForeverDB's client check shows rank 5 lower than Classic.
- Icy Veins Restoration's embedded calculators don't match its text; the text was followed.
- WoWSims ships a bear-weave priority list although Furor was reworked to stop bear-weaving; it spends Energy-starved moments on Primal Bite and Maul instead.
