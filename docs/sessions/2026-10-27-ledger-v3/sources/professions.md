# Source record: profession pages (research agent, 27 Oct 2026)

Pages: `content/professions/` herbalism, mining, skinning, alchemy, blacksmithing, enchanting, engineering, leatherworking, tailoring, cooking, first-aid, fishing. Each has 5–7 glance bullets, 590–740 words of guide summary, levelling tables in our own words, and "Nothing recorded yet." under Ledger findings.

## Sources read for every page

- **ForeverDB:** the profession page, the camping page, the beginner's professions guide and the plain-text digest. Its levelling routes are built on Classic data, and the pages say so.
- **Icy Veins:** all 12 profession pages and the overview, read in the browser. The crafting pages are thin and date from 29 September.
- **Wowhead:** Forever guides exist only for herbalism, mining, skinning, cooking, fishing, first aid, alchemy, engineering and tailoring. Its trade-camp (Merchant's Favor recipes) and camping guides fill gaps for blacksmithing, enchanting and leatherworking.
- **wow-professions.com:** levelling guides for everything except herbalism and skinning, plus its camping, mining-changes and Merchant's Favor pages.
- **wowtbc.gg:** routes for crafting professions, cooking and first aid.
- **Warcraft Tavern:** professions overview (cited on herbalism, mining, skinning, engineering and tailoring).
- Not found: Method's and Eternadex's Forever profession pages.

## Uncertain claims

- The character level needed for each gathering rank: Icy Veins' overview says none, 10 and 25; its own herbalism page and ForeverDB say 10, 20 and 35.
- Whether Blacksmithing and Leatherworking keep their Classic specialisations in Forever (only Icy Veins says so; ForeverDB's specialisation trainers come from Classic data).
- Every route above skill 225 is untested.

## Contradictions between sources (flagged on the pages)

- Bigger campfires: Wowhead says skill 90 and 200; ForeverDB and Icy Veins say 140 and 220. wow-professions.com explains it: each kit is learned at the lower skill but needs the higher one to use, possibly a bug.
- Which ore veins drop which new Scarce material (Wowhead vs wow-professions.com).
- First Aid levelling: Wowhead uses bandages throughout; Icy Veins uses potions to 150.
- Whether all food or only most food gives the experience buff.
- Who sells the Major Healing Potion manual: a Mount Hyjal vendor (Icy Veins) or nobody (wow-professions.com).
- Tailoring: the skill for the level-10 sashes (35 or 50), and which trainers teach Artisan.

## Errors found in sources, not repeated

- Wowhead's Skinning guide calls Fel Leather and Fel Hide "three new herbs", and says Artisan trains at 200 (ForeverDB: 225).
- Icy Veins' Skinning page opens by calling it "Cooking".
