# Gameplay and version-specific research

Use for Forever gameplay, characters, quests, professions, progression, talent/gear decisions, group content, PvP, comparative Classic/TBC questions, WC3 missions and lore.

- Identify the task's **game/version**, character context and objective. Confirm level, talent and Legacy allowances, gear, spell ranks, role, target, group and available content where they alter the choice. A familiar name does not establish character identity. Distinguish Beta, release, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era; independently verify both sides of a comparison.
- For change-sensitive facts, inspect current primary evidence: Blizzard announcements for changes or intent; client data and reproducible observations for actual behaviour; version-matched databases for IDs, quests, rewards and prerequisites; qualified strategies for execution. Datamines, tooltips, simulated results and live tests are distinct claims. Tazdingo records are dated research, not proof of release mechanics.
- Check talent tier access and the precise point budget; item version, acquisition, binding, set effects, sockets and enchants; spell-school interactions; access and eligibility. Compare the **real objective**: levelling speed, downtime, dungeon damage, raids, PvP, gathering, survival or convenience. Account for resources, movement, threat, pets, fight duration and targets when consequential. Avoid extrapolating a level-60 projection to a level-20 build.
- For quests and dungeons verify quest-giver level, chain prerequisites, entrance/access level, location, turn-in and rewards separately. For professions check recipe, trainer, materials, yield and skill/access requirements; use `ANALYSIS.md` when prices matter.
- For uncertain mechanics, propose one controlled in-game test with stated variables rather than assert a result. Label community reports as reports, not verified observations.
- In TBC Anniversary, do not substitute original 2.4.3 or private-server records without qualification. In Classic Era, do not import Forever's reworks. For Warcraft III: Forsaken Kingdom, establish mission, difficulty, controls and exact obstacle; use matching walkthrough or mission text, do not invent triggers, and separate game-established lore from interpretation. Avoid unrequested spoilers.
